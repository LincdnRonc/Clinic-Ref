Diffuse large B-cell lymphoma

Limited stage: R-CHOP × 4 (+ R × 2) if PET-negative (FLYER), or short chemo + ISRT. Advanced: RT consolidates bulky or PET-positive residual sites. Doses are 30–36 Gy after CR and 36–45 Gy for residual disease.

When and how much (ILROG)

  • PET CR after chemo: 30–36 Gy ISRT to initially bulky (≥7.5 cm) or extranodal sites if consolidating.
  • PET-positive residual: 36–45 Gy (up to 40–55 Gy if refractory or RT alone).
  • Limited stage, PET-negative after R-CHOP × 4: chemo alone is acceptable (FLYER, 3 y PFS about 95%). PET3-positive: add ISRT 36 Gy ± 9 Gy boost (SWOG S1001).
  • Bulky (≥7.5 cm) in older patients: RT to bulk reduces relapse (UNFOLDER, RICOVER-noRTh, OPTIMAL >60).
  • Testicular: R-CHOP + intrathecal MTX + 30 Gy to the contralateral testis (IELSG-10).
  • CAR-T bridging: RT alone (20–40 Gy) gives in-field response.

Indolent lymphoma (follicular, marginal zone)

Stage I–II indolent lymphoma can be cured with 24 Gy ISRT. For palliation, 4 Gy in 2 fractions is effective and can be repeated.

RT-alone ISRT volumes for indolent lymphoma: Lymphoma ISRT contouring protocol, section 2.

Doses

  • Curative stage I–II: 24/12 (24–30 Gy). Larger margins if RT alone.
  • Palliative: 4/2 (FORT: CR 49% with 4 Gy vs 68% with 24 Gy). Re-treat as needed.
  • Stage I–II FL with systemic therapy: adding R-CVP to 30 Gy improved 10 y PFS 43 → 62% (TROG 99.03).
  • Grade 3 FL is treated like DLBCL.
UK FORT548 · indolent NHL · 24/12 vs 4/2ORR 91 vs 81%, CR 68 vs 49%. 24 Gy better for durable control.
TROG 99.03 ANZ150 · stage I–II FL · 30 Gy ISRT ± R-CVP10 y PFS 43 → 62% with systemic therapy.

Extranodal MALT and orbit (ILROG)

  • Gastric MALT: eradicate H. pylori first. RT for H. pylori-negative or persistent disease: 24–30 Gy in 1.5–2 Gy fractions to the whole stomach (nil by mouth for 6 h; consider DIBH).
  • Orbit/ocular adnexa: 24 Gy; 4/2 is being studied. Cataract about 33% after 2.5–6.5 Gy; retinal damage does not occur below 24 Gy.
  • Salivary: 24 Gy. Other extranodal sites 24–30 Gy, usually treating the whole organ.

Primary CNS lymphoma

High-dose methotrexate-based chemo (e.g. R-MPV) first. Reduced-dose WBRT consolidation for CR; avoid 45 Gy WBRT because of delayed neurotoxicity, especially if >60 y.

WBRT doses

  • CR after R-MPV: 23.4 Gy WBRT, then cytarabine × 2.
  • PR: ILROG 36–45 Gy WBRT (1.5–1.8 Gy) without boost; NCCN 30–36 Gy with a 45 Gy boost to residual. Example: 36/24 + SIB 43.2/24.
  • Median survival about 6 y with this approach (MSKCC).
  • Intraocular involvement: CTV includes the entire globe and optic nerves to the chiasm.
