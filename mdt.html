Localised

Very low or low risk

Decision
Active surveillance.
Options
RT or RP if the patient prefers treatment or the disease is upgraded. Very low risk with life expectancy under 20 y: observation is acceptable.
RT
If treating: 60/20, 36.25/5 (40 Gy to CTV), LDR I-125 145 Gy or HDR 27/2. Prostate only; no ADT; no nodes.
Evidence
ProtecT (2016; 15 y 2023): 15-y CSS ~97% in all arms; about 55% leave active monitoring by 10 y. PACE-B (2024), CHHiP (2016).
Check
MRI-visible T3, cribriform or intraductal pathology excludes surveillance. Schedule: PSA at least 6-monthly, MRI at least annually, confirmatory biopsy at 6–12 mo.

Localised tab →

Favourable intermediate risk

Decision
RT alone (EBRT, SBRT or brachytherapy monotherapy) or RP.
Options
Active surveillance for selected GG2 (EAU 2026: PSA <10, cT2a, <10% pattern 4, ≤3 GG2 cores, low MRI volume; not if cribriform or intraductal).
RT
60/20, or 36.25/5 with 40/5 to CTV, or LDR I-125 145 Gy / HDR 27/2. Prostate + proximal 1 cm SV. ADT usually none (EAU 2026 recommends 4–6 mo).
Evidence
CHHiP (2016): 60/20 non-inferior to 74/37. PACE-B (2024): 5 fractions non-inferior; 5-y G2+ GU 18→27%. NRG-GU005 (2026): give 40 Gy to the CTV. ProtecT (2016; 15 y 2023).
Check
Baseline IPSS and gland volume (SBRT and brachytherapy suitability). Percentage pattern 4 if surveillance is considered.

Localised tab →

Unfavourable intermediate risk

Decision
EBRT + 4–6 mo ADT.
Options
EBRT + brachytherapy boost if urinary function is good. RP ± PLND. Low Decipher or MMAI score: discuss omitting ADT.
RT
60/20 (or 36.25/5; PACE-C efficacy pending). Prostate + 1–2 cm SV; omit pelvic nodes (ASTRO, conditional). MRI-defined DIL boost is an option (FLAME).
Evidence
RTOG 9408 (2011): 4 mo ADT improved 10-y OS in intermediate risk. NRG/RTOG 0815 (2023): 6 mo ADT cut biochemical failure and metastases, OS not significant; low-Decipher men gained little. FLAME (2021): 5-y bDFS 85→92%. GETUG P05 (ASTRO 2026): no gain from a BT boost, more urinary toxicity.
Check
Recent MI (be wary of ADT). Decipher or MMAI available? Urinary function if a boost is planned.

Localised tab →

High risk

Decision
Dose-escalated EBRT + long-term ADT (18–36 mo).
Options
Add a brachytherapy boost if urinary function allows (12 mo ADT may then suffice). RP + PLND in young men with unfixed disease, accepting likely post-operative RT.
RT
60/20 to prostate + ≥2 cm SV. Pelvic nodes optional: 47/20 with 60/20, or 50/25 with 68/25 (POP-RT). MRI DIL boost (FLAME).
Evidence
RADAR (2014; 10 y 2019): 18 mo better than 6 mo for prostate cancer mortality. POP-RT (2021): pelvic RT 5-y bFFS 81→95%, DMFS 89→96%, OS equal. PEACE-2 (2026): pelvic RT borderline cPFS gain only. GETUG-AFU 18 (2026): 80 vs 70 Gy, 10-y PFS 72→84%. ASCENDE-RT (2017): LDR boost 10-y bPFS 67→85%, late G3 GU 8→18%.
Check
PSMA PET staging done? Two or more of cT3–4, GG4–5, PSA >40 moves the patient to very high risk and abiraterone.

Localised tab →

Very high risk

Decision
EBRT to prostate, SV and pelvic nodes + ADT 2–3 y + abiraterone 2 y.
Options
RP + PLND within multimodal treatment in selected men (PROTEUS (2026): perioperative apalutamide improved MFS and EFS; OS immature).
RT
60/20 prostate + SV with 47/20 pelvis, or 68/25 with 50/25. DIL boost if OARs allow.
Evidence
STAMPEDE arms G/J (2022): 6-y MFS 69→82% (HR 0.53), OS HR 0.63. Docetaxel did not show the same benefit. PEACE-2 (2026): no benefit from cabazitaxel.
Check
Fitness for abiraterone + prednisolone (liver, cardiac, diabetes). PSMA PET to exclude M1.

cN1 M0

Decision
Prostate + pelvic RT + ADT 2–3 y + abiraterone 2 y.
Options
Boost involved nodes where bowel dose allows.
RT
Pelvis 46/23–50/25 (or 47/20 with 60/20) as a single-phase SIB.

STAMPEDE arms G/J (2022) included N1 disease (RT in 85%).

Localised tab →

Limited life expectancy: low or intermediate risk

Decision
Observation: PSA 6-monthly, treat symptoms.
Options
Palliative ADT for symptoms or PSA >100. A fit man with unfavourable intermediate risk can be treated on individual grounds.
Evidence
NCCN: treat low and intermediate risk when life expectancy is ≥10 y. Actuarial estimate: add 50% for the top health quartile, subtract 50% for the bottom.

Localised tab →

Limited life expectancy: high risk or cN1

Decision
Observation or ADT alone; palliative RT for local symptoms.
Evidence
NCCN: treat high-risk disease when life expectancy is ≥5 y.

Localised tab →

Modifier: poor urinary function

Decision
Favour 60/20 and optimise LUTS first (α-blocker, urology review).
Options
Three months of neoadjuvant ADT shrinks the gland 25–40%.
RT
Avoid SBRT if IPSS >19 or outlet obstruction. Avoid a DIL boost if IPSS ≥20 or TURP <3 mo. Brachytherapy relative contraindications: IPSS >15–18, TURP defect, >60 cc, large median lobe (HDR appears safe with IPSS ≥15).
Evidence
PACE-B: 2-y G2 GU 7% if IPSS ≤11 vs 27% above.

Localised tab →

After prostatectomy

Undetectable PSA, standard-risk pathology

Decision
Observe; early salvage RT at two consecutive PSA rises.
Evidence
RADICALS-RT (2020), RAVES (2020), GETUG-AFU 17 (2020): no event-free survival gain from adjuvant RT; an early-salvage policy spares about half of men RT, with less GU toxicity.
Check
Agree the PSA schedule and the referral trigger now (EAU: do not wait for a threshold).

Post-op & relapse tab →

Undetectable PSA, GG4–5 with pT3–4, or pN1

Decision
Discuss adjuvant RT once continent (4–6 mo); otherwise close PSA monitoring with early salvage.
Options
pN1: bed + pelvic nodes with ADT; strongest case with ≥4 nodes.
RT
Bed 66/33 or 52.5/20 (RADICALS); pelvis 45 Gy if pN1.
Evidence
EAU 2026: consider adjuvant RT for GG4–5 with pT3–4 (± R1) and for pN+. These groups were under-represented in RADICALS-RT, RAVES and GETUG-AFU 17.

Post-op & relapse tab →

Persistent PSA after RP

Decision
PSMA PET, then salvage RT to the bed ± pelvic nodes with ADT if there is no distant disease.
Options
More aggressive multimodality treatment if high PSA, GG4+ or pT3b.
RT
Bed 64–72 Gy; pelvis 45 Gy; boost gross recurrence.
Evidence
About 74% of men with persistent PSA progress. The benefit of salvage RT here is not proven by RCT. SPPORT (2022).

Post-op & relapse tab →

BCR: PSA <0.5, GG ≤3, long doubling time

Decision
Salvage RT to the prostate bed alone.
Options
Pelvic nodes optional (SPPORT). Low-risk BCR (PSA-DT >12 mo, GG1–3) in a frail man: monitoring is reasonable (EAU).
RT
64.8–70.2 Gy conventional, 66/33 or 52.5/20; 68.4–72 Gy if gross disease.
Evidence
Spratt: no OS benefit from ADT at PSA <0.5; possible harm at ≤0.6 (RTOG 9601 (2017)). SAKK 09/10 (2021): 70 Gy no better than 64 Gy, more late GI toxicity. Salvage RT works best at low PSA.
Check
PSMA PET at PSA >0.2 (APCCC); if negative, still offer salvage RT.

Post-op & relapse tab →

BCR with a higher-risk feature (PSA ≥0.5, GG4–5, pT3b–4, pN1 or PSA-DT ≤9 mo)

Decision
Salvage RT + 6 mo ADT; consider 24 mo, and add the pelvic nodes.
RT
Bed 64.8–70.2 Gy (66/33 or 52.5/20); pelvis 45 Gy; boost gross recurrence.
Evidence
GETUG-AFU 16 (2016; 10 y 2019) and SPPORT (2022): 6 mo ADT reduces progression; SPPORT 5-y freedom from progression 71→81→87% (bed, + ADT, + ADT and pelvis). RADICALS-HD (2024): 24 vs 6 mo, 10-y MFS 72→78%, no OS gain. RTOG 9601 (2017): 2 y bicalutamide, OS gain mainly at PSA >0.7.
Check
Decipher if available (high scores gain most from ADT). Acute G2+ toxicity rises 18→36→44% across the SPPORT arms.

Post-op & relapse tab →

Pelvic nodal recurrence after RP

Decision
Salvage RT to bed and pelvis with a boost to PET-positive nodes + ADT.
RT
Pelvis 45 Gy with nodal boost; bed 64–72 Gy.
Evidence
OLIGOPELVIS: about half disease-free at 3 y. Node-only recurrence still generally needs systemic therapy. SPPORT (2022).

Recurrence after RT

PSA failure after RT, imaging negative

Decision
Confirm Phoenix failure (nadir + 2) and exclude a bounce; then monitor and defer ADT.
Options
ADT can wait until PSA-DT <12 mo; intermittent ADT is an option. Low-risk BCR after RT (interval >18 mo, GG1–3): monitoring.
Evidence
Bounce: rise <2 ng/mL in 10–40%, median 12–18 mo after RT; recheck in 3–6 mo. Starting ADT early risks creating nmCRPC.

High-risk BCR (PSA-DT ≤9 mo), no local salvage option

Decision
Enzalutamide + ADT.
Options
After RP this applies once salvage RT has been given or is not suitable.
Evidence
EMBARK (2023; OS 2025): M0 on conventional imaging; 5-y MFS 71→87%; 8-y OS 70→79% (HR 0.60) with the combination.

Post-op & relapse tab →

Local recurrence after RT (biopsy-proven)

Decision
Local salvage at an experienced centre: salvage RP, LDR or HDR brachytherapy, or SBRT re-irradiation.
Options
Observation or deferred ADT if comorbid or low-risk BCR.
Evidence
MASTER meta-analysis: 5-y RFS ~50–60% for all modalities; severe GU toxicity >21% after RP or whole-gland HIFU vs 4–8% with re-irradiation. SBRT re-irradiation: pooled 2-y local control ~90%, late G3–4 GU ~3%.
Check
Biopsy confirmation, local MRI and PSMA PET excluding distant disease are all needed first.

Pelvic nodal recurrence after prostate RT

Salvage pelvic nodal RT with boost + ADT, if the pelvis was not irradiated before.

Nodal SBRT for one or two nodes; systemic therapy.

Post-op & relapse tab →

Oligorecurrence (≤3–5 lesions on PSMA PET)

Decision
Metastasis-directed SBRT to all PSMA-positive sites, with or without ADT, after discussing systemic therapy.
Options
ADT + ARPI as for low-volume mHSPC. After RP with a rising PSA, add bed ± pelvic RT if not yet given.
RT
SBRT, for example 30/3 (STOMP).
Evidence
STOMP (2018): 5-y ADT-free survival 8→34%. ORIOLE (2020): 6-mo progression 61→19%. About half progress by 2–3 y; PFS benefit, no OS data.
Check
Do not upstage a PSMA-avid bone focus without a CT correlate, especially with 18F-PSMA-1007.

Metastatic, hormone-sensitive

Low volume, primary in situ

Decision
ADT + ARPI + prostate RT.
Options
Metastasis-directed SBRT to all sites as well (PFS evidence only; START-MET pending).
RT
55/20 daily or 36/6 weekly (STAMPEDE); 74/37 in PEACE-1.
Evidence
STAMPEDE arm H (2018): low burden 3-y OS 73→81% (HR 0.68). STOPCAP meta (2019): <5 bone mets, 3-y OS 70→77%. PEACE-1 (2022; RT 2024): with abiraterone, RT improved rPFS and CRPC-free survival, no OS gain.
Check
Volume is defined on conventional imaging. Benefit holds for ≤3 bone mets ± non-regional nodes if there are no visceral mets.

Low volume, primary previously treated

Decision
ADT + ARPI.
Options
Metastasis-directed SBRT to all sites, to add to or defer systemic therapy.
Evidence
STOMP (2018), ORIOLE (2020). EXTEND and RADIOSA: SBRT + ADT improved PFS (RADIOSA 15→32 mo). No OS evidence.

High volume

Decision
ADT + ARPI; triplet (ADT + docetaxel + abiraterone or darolutamide) if fit.
Options
Prostate RT for local symptoms only.
RT
No survival benefit from prostate RT in high-volume disease.
Evidence
PEACE-1 (2022; RT 2024), ARASENS (2022): triplet benefit greatest in high volume. CHAARTED (2015). STAMPEDE arm H (2018): no RT benefit in high burden.
Check
HRR/BRCA testing; bone health; sites at risk of cord compression.

Castrate-resistant

nmCRPC

Decision
PSA-DT ≤10 mo: add apalutamide, enzalutamide or darolutamide and continue ADT.
Options
PSMA PET often shows disease; metastasis-directed therapy here is trial-level.
Evidence
About 2 y of MFS gain and an OS benefit with each agent.

Metastatic tab →

Oligoprogression on ARPI

Decision
SBRT to all progressing sites and continue current systemic therapy.
RT
For example 30/5 (TRAP).
Evidence
ARTO: abiraterone ± SBRT to all sites in low-volume non-visceral mCRPC improved PFS and OS. TRAP: SBRT to ≤2 progressing sites on ARPI, median PFS 6.4 mo.

mCRPC, widespread

Decision
Change drug class: docetaxel after ARPI; cabazitaxel after docetaxel + ARPI. Avoid ARPI to ARPI.
Options
177Lu-PSMA-617 after ARPI + taxane (PSMA PET selection, no FDG-discordant disease). Radium-223 for bone-only disease with bone protection. PARP inhibitor for BRCA/ATM.

Palliative RT for painful bone metastases; urgent RT or surgery for cord compression.
Evidence
VISION (2021): OS 11→15 mo. PEACE-3 (2024; final OS 2026): enzalutamide + Ra-223, OS 32.6→38.2 mo. CARD · PROfound (2019 · 2020).

Borderline

Borderline, new diagnosis

Decision
Neoadjuvant mFOLFIRINOX (or gemcitabine/nab-paclitaxel), then restage with CT and CA 19-9.
Options
RT is decided at restaging, not upfront.
Evidence
ESPAC5 (2023): short-course neoadjuvant therapy outperformed immediate surgery (small numbers). Aim for 6 mo of chemotherapy across the pathway.

Vessel involvement recorded against NCCN criteria (SMA or coeliac ≤180°, reconstructable SMV/PV). Biliary stent in place before any RT.

Overview tab →

Borderline, response or stable after induction

Decision
Surgical exploration; chemotherapy to 6 mo in total.
Options
Selective pre-operative RT if vessel contact persists or the margin is at risk: SBRT 40/5 or chemoRT 50.4/28 + capecitabine.
RT
SBRT 40/5 in DIBH, covering perivascular tracks; or 50.4/28. Low-dose SBRT without nodal coverage is discouraged (A021501).
Evidence
For: MASTERPLAN (ESMO 2026): 12-mo locoregional recurrence 52→20%, MS 14.0→22.0 mo (check); CONKO-007 (ASCO 2022): more margin-negative resections (check). Against: PANDAS (ESMO 2024): R0 50 vs 45%, no OS gain (check); Alliance A021501 (2022): SBRT arm closed for low R0.
Check
Surgeon's view of the margin at risk; CA 19-9 trend (a fall can outrun CT response).

Locally advanced, stable after induction, bowel clear

Decision
Consolidative SBRT 40/5, then a chemotherapy break.
Options
Ablative dose with MR-guided adaptive treatment: 50/5 (or 67.5/15, 75/25). Surgical review if there is a marked response.
RT
SBRT 40/5 to GTV in DIBH (ablative 50/5 on the MR-linac). Stomach, duodenum and bowel V36 <0.5 cc. Fiducials unless MR-guided.

Restaging within about 4 weeks with no new metastases; stent working; plan for chemotherapy after RT.
