Breast (AJCC 8 anatomic stage)

The positive sentinel node

For cN0 disease with 1–2 positive sentinel nodes, completion ALND does not improve control. Either treat with breast tangents (Z-11) or replace ALND with axillary RT (AMAROS). Each option roughly halves lymphoedema compared with ALND.

Decisions

  • With 1–2 positive SLNs, about 30% have further positive axillary nodes, yet regional control is not improved by ALND (Z-11, AMAROS).
  • Micrometastases or ITC: breast RT alone; consider high tangents (IBCSG 23-01, NCCN).
  • 1–2 macrometastases, BCS: no ALND. Choose between whole breast with or without high tangents and full RNI (axilla and SCF), based on risk.
  • Mastectomy with SLN macrometastasis: do ALND if the PMRT recommendation is uncertain.
  • Lymphoedema to quote: about 15% with ALND vs 5% with SLNB (roughly double these by patient report). AMAROS at 5 y: 25% with ALND vs 12% with axillary RT.

Key trials

ACOSOG Z-11 (2011; 10 y 2017)891 · cT1–2 cN0 · BCS + tangents · 1–2 SLN+ · ALND vs noneNo difference in LRR, DFS or OS (10 y OS 86 → 84%). About half of patients had high tangents.
AMAROS (2014; 10 y 2023)1,425 · cN0 SLN+ · ALND vs axillary RT (axilla + SCF, no IMN)10 y axillary recurrence <2% in both arms. Lymphoedema about halved with RT. Mastectomy patients were included (17%).
IBCSG 23-01 (2013; 10 y 2018)934 · ≤2 SLN micrometastases · ALND vs none10 y DFS 77 → 75% (no benefit from ALND). Recurrence in the undissected axilla <1%. Late lymphoedema 4 → 13% with ALND.
SINODAR-ONE (2022)889 · cT1–2 · 1–2 macrometastatic SLNs · BCS or mastectomy · ALND vs noneNo difference in survival or recurrence. Lymphoedema 2 → 13% with ALND.

Regional nodal irradiation

RNI after BCS or mastectomy lowers locoregional and distant recurrence by about 3–5%, improving DFS, with a small breast cancer mortality benefit. The cost is about 1% more pneumonitis and 4% more lymphoedema.

Who gets nodal RT (NCCN)

  • ≥4 positive nodes: RNI including IMN (category 1).
  • 1–3 positive nodes: strongly consider RNI, especially if the tumour is central or medial.
  • pN0: RNI if central or medial, ≥2 cm and other risk factors (young age, LVSI). MA.20 high-risk node-negative: cT3, or cT2 with <10 nodes removed and G3, ER− or LVSI.
  • cN+ before neoadjuvant chemotherapy: RNI. In NSABP B-51 (cN1 → ypN0), omitting RNI did not increase recurrence over 5 y. Discuss omission for ypN0 after cN1.
  • IMN: benefit is greatest for N+ medial or central tumours and for ≥4 nodes in any location (DBCG-IMN). If the decision is borderline, consider treating only the first two intercostal spaces to spare the heart.
  • Aim to keep 10 y LRR below 5%. In EORTC 22922, a 3% breast cancer mortality benefit was seen with 10 y LRR of 5.5%.

Key trials

MA.20 (2015)1,832 · BCS + ALND · N1 85% or high-risk N0 · WBI ± RNI (IMN + SCF)10 y DFS 77 → 82%. 10 y LRR 7 → 4%. 10 y OS about 82.5% with no difference. G2+ pneumonitis 0.2 → 1.2%. G2+ lymphoedema 4.5 → 8.4%.
EORTC 22922 (2015)4,004 · stage I–III · central/medial cN0 or outer cN+ · BCS or mastectomy · ± IMN-SCF RT20 y breast cancer recurrence 30.2 → 27.3% (HR 0.88). Breast cancer mortality 21.5 → 18.2%. Non-breast-cancer deaths 15.8 → 20.4%, so there was no OS gain at 20 y.
DBCG-IMN (2016; 15 y 2022)3,089 · N+ · right-sided treated with IMN, left-sided without15 y OS 55 → 60% (HR 0.86). 15 y breast cancer mortality 34 → 32%. No increase in ischaemic heart disease deaths. Lateral tumours with 1–3 nodes did not benefit.
NSABP B-51 / RTOG 1304 (2025)1,641 · cT1–3 N1 → neoadjuvant chemo → ypN0 · ± RNI5 y breast cancer recurrence-free interval about 92 → 93% (HR 0.88, not significant). 5 y OS about 94%. The control arm had no nodal RT.
Alliance A011202 (results pending)cT1–3 N1 → neoadjuvant chemo → SLN ypN+ · ALND + RNI vs RNI (including undissected axilla)Tests whether RNI can replace ALND for residual nodal disease. Chest wall boost is mandated.

Post-mastectomy RT

PMRT reduces LRR by 19%, which translates into a 9% reduction in breast cancer mortality (EBCTCG 2014). This includes women with only 1–3 positive nodes. It is recommended for pN+ and pT4 disease. For pN1 disease with favourable features, omission can be discussed.

Indications (ASTRO 2025)

StageRecommendation
pT1–2 N0Not recommended. May benefit if there are multiple adverse features: TNBC, G3, LVSI, young age, central or medial tumour
pT3 N0Conditionally recommended. May omit PMRT or treat chest wall alone if low–intermediate grade, HR+/HER2−, postmenopausal, no LVSI and low 21-gene recurrence score
pN+ (including 1–3 nodes)Recommended. Omission may be appropriate for pN1mic or low-burden pN1a after ALND with the same favourable features
Any pT4Recommended even without other risk factors
After neoadjuvant chemotherapyStrongly consider for clinical stage III or any residual disease. Local recurrence is <10% after total pCR and 10–20% with residual disease (Mamounas)
  • Volumes: chest wall + SCF/axillary apex + IMN in most cases.
  • Chest wall boost only for close (≤2 mm) or positive margins (NSABP B-51), to 12–14 Gy. Also used for inflammatory cancer.
  • Bolus: use throughout for T4b–d. Not needed routinely otherwise (ESTRO Delphi consensus). Bolus increases skin toxicity.

Key trials

Danish 82b and 82c (1997, 1999)1,708 premenopausal and 1,375 postmenopausal · high-risk · CMF or tamoxifen ± PMRT (tangents, SCF, IMN)82b: 10 y LRF 32 → 9%, 10 y OS 45 → 54%. LRF fell from about one third to <10% in both trials. Critics point to inadequate axillary dissection.
SUPREMO (2025)1,600 · T1–2 N1 or T2 N0 high-risk · ± chest wall RT10 y chest wall recurrence 2.5 → 1.1%. 10 y OS about 82%.
HYPO-PMRT / RT CHARM (RT CHARM 2026)PMRT with reconstruction · conventional vs hypofractionationRT CHARM 2 y reconstruction complications about 12 → 14% (non-inferior). 3 y LRR 2.3 → 1.5%. Hypofractionation is acceptable with reconstruction.
HypoMast-IMNI (2026)Phase III · high-risk, post-mastectomy · PMRT including IMN · 16 fractions vs conventionalLocoregional control non-inferior with 16 fractions. Supports moderate hypofractionation when the IMN chain is treated (check).

Reconstruction and PMRT

  • Major reconstruction complications about 41% overall with PMRT in one series. Reconstruction failure about 10%.
  • Irradiating the tissue expander has more complications than irradiating the final implant. Removal 40% vs 32% in one series. Capsular contracture rates vary between series.
  • Autologous reconstruction is more often used when PMRT is planned.

Locally advanced and inflammatory breast cancer

Systemic therapy first, then surgery, then comprehensive regional RT. Inflammatory breast cancer is never treated with breast conservation.

Locally advanced (stage III, non-inflammatory)

  • Stage with CT chest/abdomen/pelvis and bone scan, or PET-CT, before starting. Clip the primary and any biopsied node.
  • Neoadjuvant systemic therapy by subtype (anthracycline/taxane; anti-HER2 for HER2+; pembrolizumab with chemo for TNBC, KEYNOTE-522). Residual disease guides adjuvant therapy (T-DM1, capecitabine, olaparib for gBRCA).
  • Base RT decisions on the worse of clinical and pathological stage. cT3–4 or cN2–3 at presentation: PMRT or whole-breast RT with regional nodes, including the internal mammary chain, whatever the response.
  • cN1 converting to ypN0: NSABP B-51 found no iDFS benefit from adding regional nodal RT (and no chest wall RT after mastectomy). Patients with cT3 or cN2+ disease were not eligible, so do not extrapolate.
  • Dose: 50/25 or 40/15 to the chest wall or breast and nodes (hypofractionated PMRT is supported by Chinese and US randomised data). Boost only for positive margins or gross residual disease.
  • Unresectable after systemic therapy: RT can make disease operable (aim about 50 Gy, reassess for surgery) or treat definitively to about 60–66 Gy to gross disease Verify.

Inflammatory breast cancer (cT4d)

  • Clinical diagnosis: rapid (within about 6 months) erythema and oedema of at least a third of the breast, with peau d'orange; dermal lymphatic emboli on biopsy support it but are not required.
  • Trimodality treatment: neoadjuvant systemic therapy, modified radical mastectomy (no skin-sparing, no immediate reconstruction, no sentinel node alone), then PMRT. Breast conservation is contraindicated.
  • PMRT: chest wall and all regional nodes including the IMC, with bolus and generous skin margins. Many centres escalate to 60–66 Gy to the chest wall (e.g. 50 Gy then a boost, or MDACC-style twice-daily fractionation) for poor responders, positive margins or age <45 Verify.
  • Expect brisk skin reactions: daily skin care, review weekly.
  • Outcomes have improved with modern systemic therapy, but local recurrence is still higher than for non-inflammatory LABC.

Locoregional recurrence

  • Restage fully: isolated locoregional recurrence is potentially curable.
  • Resect where possible, then RT to the chest wall and nodes if not previously irradiated; systemic therapy as for new disease (CALOR supported adjuvant chemotherapy for ER-negative recurrence).
  • Previous RT: consider reirradiation, often with hyperthermia where available (Reirradiation: breast).
  • After breast conservation with an in-breast recurrence: mastectomy is standard; repeat breast conservation with partial-breast reirradiation is an option for selected patients.

With reconstruction

  • Major reconstruction complications are common with PMRT (about 40% in one series). Reconstruction failure is about 10%.
  • Irradiating a tissue expander carries more risk than irradiating the permanent implant.
  • Infection 6 → 13% and skin necrosis 3 → 8% with PMRT in one implant series.

Inflammatory breast cancer

Clinicopathological dx, T4d.

  • Rapid onset of sx <6mo with erythema of >1/3rd of breast, dermal oedema (peau d’orange) and induration of skin
  • Bx proven invasive breast carcinoma
  • optional: tumour emboli in dermal lymphatics

Mgmt

  • All have NA systemic Rx
  • Surgery needs to be radical mastectomy + ALND for all
  • Adj RT is PMRT with CW + RNI regardless of disease burden
  • NA RT can be used to downstage if pt remains unresectable
  • 2 phase RT: 50Gy/25# +/- 10Gy/5# if boost to whole CW if PR or close margin. 5mm bolus.
  • CTV should cover all extent of initial disease
  • For supraclavicular or infraclavicular nodes that will not be resected, consider a boost
  • Avoid immediate reconstruction
  • Risk of early distant failure, prognosis depends on subtype, 3-8 yrs
  • 5 yr 60-70%

Fungating breast mass

  • Bleeding: silver nitrate, topical TXA soaked gauze, Hb and PRBC
  • Odour: topical metronidazole
  • Dressings, POabx as needed
  • 8Gy/1#, 20Gy/5#, 30Gy/10#

Endocrine therapy

  • Tamoxifen = vasomotor symptoms, hot flushes, night sweats, VTE, endometrial hyperplasia and uterine cancer risk. Bone protective
  • AI = OP, joint and muscle aches, vaginal dryness
  • SERD = selective estrogen receptor degrader (fulvestrant) = flushes, fatigue, IM injection pain, nausea
  • Ovarian suppression = abrupt menopause, OP
  • Oopherectomy
  • CDK4/6i = neutropaenia, diarrhoea, nausea, fatigue

Management

1. Neoadjuvant systemic therapy

  • chemotherapy: AC-T, TC
    • Doxorubicin, cyclophosphamide, paclitaxel
    • Docetaxel, cyclophosphamide
  • pembrolizumab in TNBC – KEYNOTE 522 – improved pCR by 10% and 5% OS benefit
    • carbo + taxol + pembro → AC + pembro → adj pembro
  • dual anti-HER2 treatment (TCHP)
    • docetaxol, carboplatin, trastuzumab, pertuzumab → adj HP for 12 mo or switch to TDM1 if no pCR
  • Clip positive node and primary tumour prior to NACT to ensure accurate resection

2. WLE or mastectomy

  • SLNB or axillary LND or targeted axillary node dissection

3. Adjuvant radiotherapy

  • whole breast radiotherapy +/- boost
  • regional nodal irradiation (IMC, SCF, axilla 1-3)
    • Include dissected axilla if <10 nodes, >50% positive, ENE
  • post-mastectomy irradiation indications:
  • Tumour size ≥ 50mm
  • N1 disease with high risk features (LVSI, G3, multifocal/mulicentric, ER-ve)
  • N2 or N3
  • IBC
  • SM+
  • Omission of PMRT CW and RNI if ypT0N0 and initially cN1
  • De-escalation of adjuvant nodal RT based on response (RAPCHEM, prospective registry):
    • ypN0 = whole breast alone if no mastectomy
    • ypN1 or 1-2 SLNB +ve = breast/CW +/- level 1-2 axilla if no ALND
    • ypN2 on ALND or multiple macromet on SLNB = breast/CW + RNI

4. Adjuvant systemic therapy if no pCR

  • Capecitabine after NACT – CREATE-X – improved OS by 8%. Not w/ concurrent RT
  • TDM-1 – KATHERINE - improved invasive DFS (IDFS)

5. Adjuvant systemic therapy regardless of pCR

  • Pembrolizumab – KEYNOTE 522, improves OS by 5%
    • Can be given concurrent with RT
  • Olaparib - OlympiA – improved IDFS and OS in high risk gBRCA. Not w/ concurrent RT
  • Abemaciclib + ET – monarchE – IDFS in high risk pts (pN2, G3 and any N+, high Ki67, T3+). Not concurrent w/ RT

Frail, unfit

  • ER +ve consider use of neoadjuvant ET + CDK4/6i
  • HER2 +ve: NA anti-her2 therapy +/- ET
  • TNBC or unfit for any systemic: NA RT to downstage for definitive surgery

Re-irradiation

  • RTOG 1014: 45Gy/30# BD, 90% of patients avoided mastectomy
  • Toxicity: fat necrosis, non healing ulcer, chest wall necrosis, rib fracture, cumulative cardiac and lung toxicity

EBCTCG MA

22 trials, majority of data from British Columbia and Danish trials with old CMF chemotherapy

PMRT improved LRR and BCM in N+ pts, regardless of number of nodes involved.

SUPREMO

P: T1-3, N0-1 breast ca pts with mastectomy. Modern chemo allowed.

I: PMRT vs no PMRT however only 15% of pts in PMRT had nodal irradiation. Many pts had ALND where this is outdated.

O: no difference in OS, benefit may not been seen due to omission of RNI

NSABP B-18 & B-27 pooled analysis

  • residual nodal disease after NAC was an independent RF for LRR

NSABP-51

P: T1-3N1 breast cancer with ypN0

I: omission of CW and RNI if ypN0 after mastectomy. If BCS still get WBI +/- boost without RNI

O: no difference in LRR, BCM at 5yrs

RNI appeared ‘harmful’ in TNBC and beneficial in ER+/HER2-

RAPCHEM

P: prospective registry trial, cT1-2N1

I: risk adapted RT after neoadjuvant systemic therapy

Low risk (ypN0 or micromet only on SLNB) = WBRT after lumpectomy, no RT after mastectomy

Intermediate risk (ypN1 after ALND or 1-2 macromet on SLNB) = breast/CW RT + axilla 1-2 if no ALND

High risk (ypN2 after ALND or 3+ macromet in SLNB) = WBRT/CW + axilla level III – IV if ALND, RNI if no ALND

O: 10 yr LRR low across all groups (<3%), OS 83%

PMRT

Dose fractionation: 40Gy/15# PMRT to CW & RNI. VMAT technique.

Consider boost 10-16Gy/2# if residual disease

Pre-sim

  • scar healed
  • no fluctuating seroma

Sim

  • Supine, half vac bag, arms up
  • DIBH
  • 1cm anterior chest wall bolus if thin, IBC or involved skin margin [ESTRO consensus paper recommends against universal use of bolus due to increase in skin toxicity without proven reduction in LR]

Daily CBCT with bone match

Volumes

  • CW: from inf clavicle head to 1cm below inframammary fold [use contralateral breast as guidance]
  • RNI see above

Oligometastatic

From the exam summary.

Management

1. Systemic therapy

  • ER+ve/HER2-: ET, CDK4/6 first line unless visceral crisis, rapid response required in which case chemo (paclitaxel) can be added
  • HER2+ve: chemo (paclitaxel)+ dual anti-HER2
  • TNBC: chemo (paclitaxel) + IO if PDL1 CPS >10

2. SABR to mets if ≤4 DM

  • No evidence of PFS benefit, thus not indicated

3. Oligoprogressive disease whilst on systemic therapy

  • No evidence of PFS benefit with MDT aside from Oligoma trial

4. Other

  • Bone modifying agents: zoledronic acid given if bone mets to reduce SRE, no OS benefit
  • Repeat biopsy in oligorecurrence due to chance of biological transformation (acquired HER2, triple negative)

NRG-BR002

P: ≤4 DM and systemic therapy without progression for up to 1 year and treated locoregional disease

I: Standard systemic therapy +/- SABR to all mets

O: No difference in PFS, systemic treatment alone did better

EXTEND

P: ≤5 DM

I: MDT vs no MDT

O: no PFS benefit

CURB

P: Oligoprogressive MBC

I: MDT vs no MDT

O: no PFS benefit

OLIGOMA

P: Oligometastatic breast cancer (1-5mets). Accrual closed early, <90 pts

I: MDT + systemic therapy vs no MDT and systemic therapy

O: PFS benefit of 36mo vs 21mo

AVATAR

P: pts on CDK4/6i + ET with oligoprogression

I: SABR vs no SABR

O: excellent LC and delays time to change systemic therapy (improves PFS)
