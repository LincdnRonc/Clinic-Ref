Cervix: treatment by stage

Surgery for small early tumours (open, not minimally invasive), chemoradiation plus MRI-guided brachytherapy for bulky or node-positive disease. Overall treatment time ≤50 days.

IMRT is the standard for adjuvant pelvic RT (PARCER: halves late bowel toxicity).

Definitive chemoradiation

  • EBRT: 45/25 pelvis (± para-aortic) with weekly cisplatin 40 mg/m² ×5–6. Node SIB 55–57.5/25 (EMBRACE II).
  • Brachytherapy: MRI-guided, interstitial needles for bulky, asymmetric or parametrial residual disease (>5 cm, thick vaginal involvement).
  • Total time: keep within 50 days; each extra week costs local control.
  • Para-aortic RT: EMBRACE II extends to the renal vessels for ≥3 pelvic nodes, common iliac or para-aortic nodes ("large pelvis + low pAO" or higher).
  • Intensification: INTERLACE (6 wk induction carbo/paclitaxel) and KEYNOTE-A18 (pembrolizumab with and after chemoRT) both improved OS. OUTBACK (adjuvant carbo/paclitaxel after chemoRT) did not.
  • Response: PET-CT at 3–6 mo; persistent FDG uptake predicts worse CSS (5-y 32% vs 80%). Biopsy suspected persistence before salvage.

Cervix brachytherapy

Volume-based (GEC-ESTRO / ICRU 89) planning. Aim HR-CTV D90 ≥85–90 Gy EQD2 while respecting D2cc limits.

  • HR-CTV: residual GTV + whole cervix + presumed extracervical extension at BT (grey zones in parametria, uterus, vagina, rectum, bladder).
  • IR-CTV: HR-CTV + initial extent, with 5–15 mm margins.
  • Cervix superior border ≥1 cm above the uterine vessels. CT-only planning: assume ~3 cm cervical height and treat the whole tandem length.
  • Per-fraction OAR doses after 45/25: 5 × 3.5 Gy ≈ 65 Gy; 5 × 3.9 Gy ≈ 70 Gy; 5 × 4.75 Gy ≈ 80 Gy total.

Cervix EBRT planning

IMRT with an internal target volume for the moving uterus and vagina.

Contour step by step with the Gynae contouring protocol (primary targets and ITV, section 3; nodes and SIB, section 4).

Volumes (intact cervix)

  • Sim with full and empty bladder (ITV), IV contrast, vaginal marker; fuse MRI ± PET in treatment position.
  • CTV1: GTV + cervix + uterus → PTV1 +1.5 cm.
  • CTV2: parametria (with ovaries) + vagina (upper half if minimal involvement, upper ⅔ if upper vagina involved, whole vagina if extensive; ≥3 cm below disease) → PTV2 +1 cm. Whole mesorectum if uterosacral involvement.
  • CTV3 nodes: common, external and internal iliac, obturator, presacral; vessels + 7 mm. Add para-aortic to renal vessels when indicated; inguinal if lower third of vagina involved.
  • Postop: vaginal ITV using full/empty bladder scans; vaginal cuff to upper third of symphysis.

EBRT constraints

  • Bone marrow: V40 <37%, V10 <90% (RTOG 0418; higher V40 linked to haematological toxicity).
  • Bowel: V40 <30% (TIME-C). Limit V57 for nodes; EMBRACE: 45 Gy with nodal SIB and bowel D2cc <60 Gy cut late GI toxicity.
  • Duodenum (para-aortic): V55 <1 cc or V50 <4 cc for <10% G2+.
  • Keep elective nodal dose at 45 Gy to reduce long-term diarrhoea.
