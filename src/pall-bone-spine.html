Bone metastases

8/1 is as good as multiple fractions for pain, at the cost of more retreatment (20% vs 8%). Use SBRT for selected spinal metastases or oligometastatic disease.

Decisions (ASTRO 2017)

  • Uncomplicated pain: 8/1, 20/5, 24/6 or 30/10 are equivalent for pain. Overall pain response 60–80% (about 50% in trials for either 8/1 or 20/5).
  • Retreatment: after at least 1 month. 30–60% respond to re-irradiation (more if 8/1 was first). 8/1 is suitable for retreatment.
  • Spine SBRT for painful spinal metastases: 24/2 doubles complete pain response vs 20/5 (SC.24: 14 → 35% at 3 months).
  • Pathological fracture risk: refer to orthopaedics for fixation, then RT.

Metastatic spinal cord compression

Dexamethasone immediately, MRI of the whole spine, and a surgical opinion. Fit patients with a single level of compression should have decompression then RT. Otherwise 8/1 or 20/5.

Decisions (NOMS)

  • Steroids: IV dexamethasone 10–16 mg, then taper.
  • Surgery: single level, good prognosis (>3 months), paraplegia <48 h, instability, or radioresistant histology with high-grade compression. Then 30/10 (Patchell).
  • RT alone: 8/1 or 20/5 (SCORAD III: ambulation similar). 20/5 is non-inferior to 30/10 (SCORE-2). Consider 30/10 for longer survivors.
  • Radiosensitivity: sensitive (myeloma, lymphoma, seminoma); moderately sensitive (breast, prostate, ovary); resistant (colon, NSCLC, HCC); highly resistant (thyroid, renal, sarcoma, melanoma, which suit SBRT).
  • Stability (SINS): 0–18 points. ≥7: neurosurgical referral (potentially unstable). >12: unstable.
  • Bilsky grade: 0 bone only; 1 epidural without cord compression; 2 cord displaced, CSF visible; 3 CSF not visible.

Spine SBRT

Single-fraction 24 Gy gives the best local control but about 40% vertebral compression fracture. 24/2 or 27/3 are common. Separation surgery first if the cord is abutted in radioresistant tumours.

Contour step by step with the Spine and bone SBRT contouring protocol (St George v9).

Dose and cord tolerance

  • Regimens: 24/1 (local failure <5%, Yamada), 24/2 (SC.24), 27/3. RTOG 0631 used 16–18/1.
  • Cord Dmax (HyTEC, de novo, 1–5% myelopathy risk): 12.4–14 Gy/1, 17/2, 20.3/3, 23/4, 25.3/5.
  • Re-irradiation: cumulative BED2 ≤135.5 Gy; no single course ≥102 Gy BED2. Thecal sac SBRT EQD2 Dmax ≤25 Gy.
  • Leave at least 4 mm between GTV and cord for 24/1.
  • VCF: about 40% with 24/1 and about 20% with 20–23 Gy (Sahgal). Consider prophylactic vertebroplasty/kyphoplasty for lytic disease or extensive body involvement.
  • SBRT can increase VCF, pain flare and radiation myelopathy risk compared with conventional RT.
