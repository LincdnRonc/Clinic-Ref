Kidney (RCC)

SABR is a curative non-surgical option for primary RCC in inoperable patients, and a way to delay or protect systemic therapy in oligometastatic disease.

SABR for primary RCC FASTRACK II

  • Dose: 26/1 for ≤4 cm; 42/3 for >4 cm (FASTRACK II, TROG 15.03/ANZUP). Other series: 36/3, 40/5; range 26/1 to 30–45 Gy in 3–5.
  • Eligibility (FASTRACK II): biopsy-proven, ≤10 cm, ECOG ≤2; excluded eGFR <30, bowel contact, horseshoe kidney, prior systemic therapy.
  • Results: LC 100% at 36, 60 and 84 mo; CSS 100%; G3 10%, no G4–5.
  • Renal function: mean eGFR fall ~11 at 1 y, ~15 at 2 y, then plateau. One patient needed dialysis (baseline eGFR 34, central 59 mm tumour). Counsel on this for large/central tumours.
  • Single fraction is safe in a solitary kidney (IROCK). Cost-effective vs RFA/cryo in Australian analysis.
  • Coverage goal D99% ≥Rx (D95% acceptable for OARs); peak 125–143%.

Oligometastatic and oligoprogressive RCC

  • SABR in lieu of systemic therapy (MDACC Tang, ≤5 mets, serial SBRT): most stayed off systemic therapy; G3+ 0–10% vs ~50% ipi/nivo, 64% sunitinib.
  • Oligoprogression: SABR to 1–2 sites lets patients stay on current systemic therapy (mostly favourable/intermediate IMDC, clear cell).
  • Dose: aim BED10 ≥59.5 Gy; single fraction ≥20/1, preferably ≥24/1 (local PFS 88% vs 21% below 24 Gy). 25/5 had 18% 1-y local failure.
  • Brain mets: SRS LC ≥90%.
  • CYTOSHRINK: SBRT to primary during ipi/nivo did not improve PFS. NRG-GU012/SAMURAI ongoing.

Holding systemic therapy around RT

  • Bevacizumab (t½ ~20 d): suspend ~4 wk before and after.
  • Sunitinib / sorafenib: pause 5–10 d before and after.
  • Ipilimumab: pause 2 d either side. PD-1/PD-L1 agents: limited data, generally safe with conventional RT (watch pneumonitis).

Systemic therapy and surgery

  • Stratify by IMDC: favourable 0, intermediate 1–2, poor ≥3 factors.
  • First line: IO combinations (nivo/ipi for intermediate/poor; IO + TKI e.g. pembro/axitinib, cabo/nivo across risk groups).
  • After PD-1: belzutifan + lenvatinib beat cabozantinib for PFS (LITESPARK-011).
  • Adjuvant pembrolizumab after nephrectomy (KEYNOTE-564, OS benefit); LITESPARK-022 adds belzutifan for modest DFS gain.
  • Cytoreductive nephrectomy: not upfront for intermediate/poor risk (CARMENA). Consider with only 1 IMDC factor, oligometastatic disease, or after response. Small renal masses: biopsy if it changes management; partial nephrectomy standard; AS if comorbid.
  • Recurrence after surgery for ≥pT3a: lung 53%, liver 20%, bone 18%; >10 cm and G4 favour bone and brain.
