EQD2 and BED

Linear-quadratic model: BED = D × (1 + d / α/β); EQD2 = D × (d + α/β) / (2 + α/β). For education and cross-checking only. Verify every result independently.

Single schedule

The model is less reliable at very high doses per fraction (SABR/SRS) and ignores overall time and incomplete repair between twice-daily fractions.

Cumulative dose across courses

Converts each course to EQD2 with the same α/β and adds them. Enter the dose the organ received (a point dose such as D0.1cc), not the prescription. This gives point-dose arithmetic only: no spatial information.

Tim Wang's OAR dose limit calculator

For a full re-irradiation check, use OAR Dose Limit Calculator (EQD2 & BED re-irradiation tool) by A/Prof Tim Wang. It takes up to three previous courses, applies a repair discount (0, 25, 33 or 50%), checks the cumulative dose against a library of organ limits (QUANTEC, HyTEC and others), and gives the remaining EQD2, the permissible dose for the planned course and text for the plan note.

Its own stated limits: point-dose estimates only, no spatial dose information; it does not replace image registration and cumulative dose-volume assessment. Needs an internet connection.

Unscheduled treatment gap

Estimates the tumour BED lost when overall time is prolonged, using BED − K × (T − Tk). It shows the size of the problem; how to compensate (weekend or twice-daily fractions, or added dose) is a clinical and departmental decision.
