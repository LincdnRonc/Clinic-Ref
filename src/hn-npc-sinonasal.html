Nasopharyngeal carcinoma

IMRT 70/33–35. Stage I: RT alone. Stage III–IVA: induction (gemcitabine/cisplatin or TPF), then cisplatin chemoRT. Use MRI for all target volumes.

2024 international NPC dose-level volumes: Head and neck contouring guide, section 6.

By stage (ASCO/CSCO)

  • Stage I: RT alone 70/35 (10 y LC and DSS >90%).
  • T2 N0: chemo not routine; consider if bulky or EBV DNA high. Low-risk stage II: RT alone was non-inferior to chemoRT with fewer G3–4 AEs (46 → 17%) in one Chinese trial.
  • T2 N1: consider concurrent cisplatin.
  • T3 N0: chemoRT; induction or adjuvant may be considered.
  • Stage III–IVA: induction GC × 3 or TPF, then cisplatin 100 q3w or 40 weekly to a cumulative dose ≥200 mg/m². Without induction, give adjuvant PF × 3.
  • De novo M1: chemo, then locoregional RT for responders (SYSUCC5010: 2 y OS 55 → 76%).

Volumes (35 or 33 fractions)

  • Doses: 70/63/56 in 35, or 69.96/59.4/54 in 33.
  • CTVp_70 = GTVp + 5 mm (down to 1 mm near critical OARs). CTVn_70 = GTVn + 5 mm (1 cm if ENE).
  • CTVp_59.4 = GTVp + 1 cm + whole nasopharynx, at least 5 mm anterior to the choanae.
  • Elective nodes: retropharyngeal and levels II–V. Level Ib may be omitted unless anterior nasal cavity involved, bulky level II, ENE or bilateral disease. Consider omitting the lower neck on an uninvolved side.
  • GTVs include the pre-induction extent, especially in bone.
  • PTV ≥5 mm. PRV ≥2 mm around OARs.
  • Temporal lobe: Dmax <65 Gy (T1–2) or <70 Gy (T3–4) to the PRV; 72 Gy is an upper limit. Above Dmax 72 Gy or D1cc 62 Gy, necrosis risk rises.

Nasal cavity and paranasal sinuses

Surgery and PORT is preferred. Nodal risk depends on site and histology. Treat the ipsilateral neck unless the tumour crosses midline.

Decisions

  • Surgery followed by PORT appears better than either alone for nasal cavity cancer. RT alone may suit T1 N0 (5 y OS >70%).
  • Maxillary sinus PORT: positive margins, PNI, LVSI or adenoid cystic histology.
  • Neck: cover RP and Ib–IV if node-positive. Elective coverage of the N0 neck is controversial; consider for G3, SqCC or undifferentiated histology. Adenoid cystic: neck coverage not needed.
  • Olfactory neuroblastoma: Kadish A → surgery (preferred) or RT 70 Gy. Kadish B/C → surgery then PORT.
  • PORT is usually SIB in 30 fractions.
