Before simulation

Things to settle at consent

  • Intent, dose and fractionation, target and the imaging needed for planning (diagnostic MRI or PET in treatment position, fusion requests).
  • Pregnancy status for anyone who could be pregnant; fertility preservation referral before pelvic or testicular RT.
  • Cardiac devices (pacemaker or ICD), other implants, previous RT (get old plans and doses for reirradiation), connective tissue disease, radiosensitivity syndromes.
  • IV contrast: renal function, allergy, metformin per local policy.
  • Can the patient lie in the position for the expected session time? Pain relief, anxiety, claustrophobia (masks, MR-linac).
  • Dental review before H&N RT; feeding tube and dietitian plan for high-risk H&N and oesophagus.
  • Concurrent systemic therapy timing (Targeted and immunotherapy with RT).

Useful surface and bony landmarks

  • C2: angle of mandible level. C6: cricoid. T2–3: sternal notch. T4–5: angle of Louis and carina. T10: xiphoid. L4: iliac crests. S2: posterior superior iliac spines and end of the thecal sac (variable, check MRI).
  • Hippocampus, cochlea and optic apparatus are only reliably seen on MRI: always fuse for these.

Motion management

Strategies

  • Motion-encompassing (ITV): 4DCT; simple and robust; largest volumes.
  • Breath-hold: DIBH (breast, mediastinal lymphoma, some lung and liver) or end-exhale; needs a cooperative patient and monitoring (SGRT, spirometry).
  • Gating: beam on only in part of the cycle; longer treatment.
  • Abdominal compression: reduces liver and lower-lobe lung motion; can be uncomfortable.
  • Tracking: beam follows the target (MR-linac, robotic linac, MLC tracking).
  • Pelvic organs move with filling, not breathing: bladder and bowel protocols, daily CBCT, and adaptive plans.
