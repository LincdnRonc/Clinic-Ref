Spine and Bone SBRT — Target Volume Delineation Guide

Target volumes for intact mobile spine (ISRC sectors), post-operative spine, sacrum, non-spine bone (including post-fixation long bone) and re-irradiation, with selection, simulation, cord and thecal sac delineation, PRVs, dose schedules, coverage goals, OAR constraints for 1–5 fractions and names.

GTV CTV (anatomic bony compartments) PTV (2 mm, minus cord PRV) Spinal cord / thecal sac PRV
St George Cancer Care Centre spine SBRT protocol (draft v9, 2024) · Bone and soft tissue metastases SABR protocol v1.0 (Oct 2021) · ISRC (Cox 2012) · Redmond 2017 post-op · Dunne 2020 sacrum · Nguyen 2022 non-spine bone (and 2020 practice patterns) · ESTRO 2024 spine SBRT guideline · HyTEC spinal cord (Sahgal) · CCTG SC.24 protocol · national spine SABR protocol (2023) — Version 1.0 · Nov 2026
Governing principles of this guide
  • The departmental standard is the St George spine SBRT protocol (draft v9): ISRC anatomic CTV for the mobile spine, Dunne 2020 for the sacrum, 5 mm anatomic epidural margin when there is epidural disease, PTV = CTV + 2 mm minus the cord PRV, cord PRV = cord + 2 mm.
  • The CTV is anatomic, not a margin: include the whole compartment (body, pedicle, transverse process/lamina, spinous process) that holds GTV, plus the next marrow space at risk (Cox 2012).
  • The cord comes first: cord PRV dose is prioritised over PTV coverage; contour the cord on MRI and the thecal sac as the cauda equina surrogate.
  • Every case is peer reviewed at the SRS planning meeting (Tuesday 07:45) before planning; no more than 14 days from simulation and planning MRI to the first fraction.
  • Dose to the right level: count levels on CT and MRI, contour adjacent vertebrae for matching, and have the RO check the level on CBCT.

1.1 Workflow (St George)

  1. RO review: can the patient tolerate MRI and lying flat (pacemaker, claustrophobia, pain, dyspnoea); optimise analgesia; consider pre-sim/RT analgesia and dexamethasone.
  2. Neuro-oncology MDT if needed. Systemic therapy: immunotherapy may continue; stop TKIs 1 day before to 1 day after; stop chemotherapy ≥1 week before to 1 week after (confirm with the medical oncologist).
  3. SRS planning meeting (Tuesday 07:45): suitability, then formal peer review of contours.
  4. Maximum 14 days from CT simulation and planning MRI to fraction 1.

Spine protocol (folder): scan 3 vertebrae above and below; consider 4D-CT from mid-thoracic spine down to build OAR ITVs for moving organs; hexapod frame cut-out; RO checks fusion with priority on the spinal canal.

2.1 Image registration checklist

  1. Count the levels on CT (from C2 or from the last rib/sacrum) and confirm the target level on the sagittal MRI before contouring.
  2. Register MRI to CT locally on the target vertebra (rigid, box around the target ± one level); check the canal, the posterior vertebral body wall and the pedicles.
  3. If the patient's curvature differs between scans, register each level separately rather than accept a poor match at the target.
  4. Post-op: register the pre-operative MRI as well (it defines the CTV) and use the post-op MRI or CT myelogram for residual disease and the cord.
Check St George v9 says "no motion management required"; the folder spine protocol suggests 4D-CT for mid-thoracic and lower lesions to make ITVs for moving OARs (oesophagus, bowel). Confirm the local policy for lower thoracic/lumbar targets near bowel CHECK.

Section 3 · Scenario A

3Intact mobile spine (cervical, thoracic, lumbar)

3.1 Volumes (St George; Cox 2012)

  • GTV: all gross tumour on CT and T1/T2 MRI, including epidural and paraspinal components.
  • CTV: GTV + abnormal marrow signal + the whole anatomic compartment(s) by the ISRC table; drawn on the planning CT.
  • Epidural disease: 5 mm anatomic margin beyond GTV within the epidural space (not into cord), including cranio-caudally (St George; SC.24). Donut CTV allowed at RO discretion (SC.24).
  • Paraspinal disease: SC.24 requires ≥5 mm beyond GTV; bone/soft tissue protocol 5 mm.
  • Multiple levels (≤3 contiguous): contour each level's CTV separately and join (St George; SC.24).
  • PTV = CTV + 2 mm minus the cord PRV; may be modified at the dura and next to critical structures unless the GTV is compromised.
  • Osteophytes need not be included (folder spine protocol).

Cox 2012: no epidural CTV expansion without epidural disease; circumferential (cord-encircling) CTVs only when body, both pedicles/laminae and spinous process are involved or epidural disease is near-circumferential. ISRC kappa: GTV 0.65, CTV 0.64. The St George 5 mm epidural margin (from SC.24) applies only when there is epidural disease. The ISRC body-and-both-pedicles row is given as the CTV description in the paper; sector numbers here are derived from it.

3.3 Walkthrough

  1. Sagittal T2: confirm level, epidural extent, cord position. Axial T1: marrow replacement (dark) and the GTV.
  2. Draw GTV on each axial slice (bone, epidural, paraspinal), checking on CT for cortical breach.
  3. Pick the CTV row from the table; paint whole sectors from the top to the bottom endplate of the involved level(s).
  4. Add 5 mm epidural (not into cord) and ≥5 mm paraspinal if present; then cord on MRI; cord PRV; PTV = CTV + 2 mm − PRV.

4.1 Imaging

  • Hardware artefact can hide the cord: use a CT myelogram (intrathecal contrast) when MRI is degraded (bone/soft tissue protocol; folder spine protocol).
  • Fuse pre-op MRI (extent of disease) and post-op MRI (residual, cord).
  • Department dose for post-op spine: 30–35 Gy/5 preferred (Redmond phase 2); 24 Gy/2 or 27–30 Gy/3 alternatives (bone/soft tissue protocol). St George v9 lists de novo schedules only CHECK.

4.2 Pitfalls

  • Drawing the CTV on the post-op anatomy only: the CTV must cover the whole pre-operative extent.
  • Including rods, screws and the scar adds volume without benefit (Redmond).
  • Epidural recurrence is the common failure after surgery; do not under-cover the epidural space next to the decompression.
  • Redmond kappa was only 0.58 for CTV and PTV — peer review matters most here.

The sacral classification divides each level into 8 segments: 1 body; 2 left anterior ala; 3 left posterior ala; 4 left lamina; 5 spinous process; 6 right lamina; 7 right posterior ala; 8 right anterior ala. At S3–S5 the ala may appear as a single segment.

5.1 Rules that are easy to miss (Dunne 2020)

  • Cranio-caudal extent follows the vertebral levels. Exception: if the GTV touches any part of the S1 ala, start the CTV at the top of the S1 ala, which sits above the S1 body.
  • Ossification line: when adding an ala only as the adjacent marrow space, the visible ossification line can limit the CTV (anterior or posterior part next to the disease). If the GTV is in the ala, do not use the line as a barrier.
  • Contour the thecal sac as the cauda equina surrogate; the sacral nerve roots (S1–S5) are separate OARs.
  • Most sacral failures were within the vertebral body or toward the alae/SI joint when these were not covered.
Different policy elsewhere The national spine SABR protocol (2023) uses CTV = GTV with PTV = CTV + 10 mm for the sacrum. St George follows Dunne 2020 (anatomic CTV, 2 mm PTV); this page does too.

6.1 Evidence behind the margins

  • Nguyen 2022 (9 international experts, 10 cases): mean kappa 0.72, DSC 0.77. Median intraosseous margin 7.3 mm, extraosseous 7.9 mm; most used 5 mm with a fused MRI, some 7–10 mm (larger without MRI).
  • Nguyen 2020 practice patterns: doses ranged from 18–24 Gy/1 to 42–50 Gy/10 (all ≥BED10 60 Gy); 35 Gy/5 was the most common; some used a two-level SIB (e.g. GTV/CTV1 30 Gy/3 and CTV2 15–24 Gy/3).
  • Pelvis/ilium attracted larger intraosseous margins (marrow spread is harder to see).

6.2 Walkthrough

  1. GTV on CT + MRI (T1 marrow, STIR/T2 for oedema vs tumour) ± PET.
  2. Expand 5–10 mm inside the same bone along the marrow; 5–10 mm into soft tissue only where there is extraosseous disease or a cortical breach.
  3. Crop: stop at joint spaces, intact cortex, pleura, peritoneum and uninvolved OARs.
  4. Check fracture risk (Mirels, cortical involvement) before treating weight-bearing bones.

7Re-irradiation

St George v9 excludes previously irradiated regions. Where re-irradiation SBRT is considered elsewhere (bone/soft tissue protocol; folder spine protocol), volumes follow the de novo rules and the cord limits change:

  • HyTEC (Sahgal): cumulative thecal sac EQD22 Dmax ≤70 Gy; re-irradiation SBRT thecal sac EQD22 Dmax ≤25 Gy; SBRT/cumulative ratio ≤0.5; interval ≥5 months.
  • Dose: 30–35 Gy/5 preferred (Thibault), or 27–30 Gy/3.
  • Folder spine protocol: one prior course allowed if prior BED was <100 Gy2 and >5 months ago; earlier Sahgal data showed no myelopathy with initial course <50 Gy EQD22, SBRT course <25 Gy and interval >5 months.
  • Import every prior plan and sum doses to the cord/thecal sac.

8Pearls and common errors

  • Wrong level is the classic error: count twice, contour the vertebra above and below for matching, and have the RO confirm the level on the first CBCT.
  • Cord: draw on T1/T2 MRI fused locally; at least 10 mm above and below the PTV (St George), one vertebra above and below (other protocols). Below the conus use the thecal sac.
  • Gap to cord: RTOG 0631 required 3 mm between cord and epidural disease, whereas SC.24 only excluded symptomatic cord compression or cauda equina syndrome (ESTRO 2024). St George requires tumour ≥2 mm from the cord.
  • No epidural CTV without epidural disease; no cord-encircling CTV unless disease is (near-)circumferential (Cox 2012).
  • Fracture risk: higher single-fraction doses such as 24 Gy/1 raise the vertebral compression fracture risk; ESTRO recommends more than BED10 50 Gy (about 18 Gy/1) for durable control and accepts 2–5 fraction schedules.
  • Pain flare: dexamethasone 8 mg daily from ≥1 h before fraction 1 and for 4 days after (bone/soft tissue protocol); St George reviews on the last day and considers short-term dexamethasone.
  • Paraspinal mass: the folder spine protocol excludes masses ≥5 cm.
  • Bring prior PTVs and dose into the plan for any adjacent-level treatment to avoid unrecognised overlap.

11.1 ROI algebra

  1. CTV_Tx for each level from the ISRC/Dunne table (manual), plus GTV + 5 mm in the epidural space (minus cord) when epidural disease is present.
  2. CTV = ∪ CTV_Tx (≤3 contiguous levels).
  3. SpinalCord_PRV02 = SpinalCord + 2 mm (or CaudaEquina for lumbosacral levels).
  4. PTV_Unclipped = CTV + 2 mm; PTV_2400 = PTV_Unclipped − SpinalCord_PRV02.
  5. Evaluation: PTV D90%, GTV D99%, max ≤140% inside GTV; cord PRV and thecal sac D0.035cc.
  6. Matching aids: contour the vertebra above and below and the spinal canal.

11.2 Peer review and QA

  • SRS planning meeting (Tuesday 07:45) at St George; fortnightly SABR audit (bone/soft tissue protocol).
  • Mock-up/collision check before treatment; dose grid ≤1.5 mm (ESTRO) or ≤2.5 mm (SC.24).
  • Follow-up: 4–6 weeks clinically; MRI at 3 months, then every 2–3 months to 1 year and 3–6-monthly after (St George); most VCFs occur in the first 4 months.
