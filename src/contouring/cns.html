CNS — Target Volume Delineation Guide

Target volumes for glioblastoma, IDH-mutant grade 2 and 3 glioma, meningioma (WHO 1 to 3), vestibular schwannoma, intact brain metastases SRS, resection cavity SRS and hippocampal-avoidance whole brain, with imaging, anatomical-barrier editing, doses, OAR definitions and constraints.

Governing principles of this guide
  • No Liverpool or St George CNS protocol is in the folder: this page follows the ESTRO-EANO guidelines for glioma, RTOG 0539 for meningioma, ISRS for vestibular schwannoma, Soliman 2018 and the Canberra Health Services guideline for SRS, and RTOG 0933/NRG CC001 for HA-WBRT. Local margins and IGRT should be confirmed CHECK.
  • Single-phase glioma CTVs: GTV + isotropic margin, edited at true anatomical barriers (skull, falx, tentorium 0 mm; ventricles 5 mm) but not along white-matter routes (corpus callosum, peduncles).
  • Planning MRI close to RT: post-op MRI within 72 h for residual tumour, and a planning MRI within 2 weeks before RT for glioma; volumetric thin-slice contrast T1 for SRS.
  • SRS has no CTV margin for intact metastases; cavities get the whole cavity and tract plus dural margins where the tumour touched dura.
  • OARs follow the EPTN atlas; brain dose is reported as brain minus GTV.

1.1 Fusion checks

  • Match on the skull base and the region of the target; check the brainstem, optic apparatus and the cavity edges.
  • For SRS, the planning MRI should be recent (Canberra: review the planning MRI to confirm suitability; new lesions change the plan).
  • Hippocampal contouring needs a fused 3D T1 within the RTOG Hausdorff tolerance (≤2 mm per protocol; >7 mm unacceptable).

1.2 Systemic therapy and premedication around SRS (Canberra)

  • Off chemotherapy ≥7 days before SRS; BRAF/MEK inhibitors stopped ≥1 day before and after; no radiosensitisers; nivolumab/pembrolizumab need not be stopped (half-life ~26 days); trastuzumab, endocrine therapy and bisphosphonates may continue, but T-DM1 after SRS raises necrosis risk.
  • Dexamethasone 4–8 mg PO before SRS (on top of baseline); lorazepam 0.5–1 mg SL if anxious.
  • Cavity SRS ideally within 4 weeks of surgery; most cavity shrinkage happens in the first days, so waiting beyond 1–2 weeks gains little.

2.1 Walkthrough

  1. Fuse the planning MRI (≤2 weeks old) and the 72-h post-op MRI; review preoperative FLAIR.
  2. GTV: cavity + every enhancing nodule; add FLAIR areas that look like tumour (mass-like, cortical thickening, corpus callosum involvement).
  3. CTV = GTV + 15 mm; on bone window remove skull; trim at falx and tentorium to 0 mm; to 5 mm inside ventricles.
  4. Re-add any FLAIR abnormality suspicious for infiltration outside the CTV (e.g. splenium) without a further margin (Niyazi Fig. 2).
  5. PTV = CTV + 3 mm; brain OAR = brain − GTV.

2.2 Dose (Niyazi 2023)

60 Gy/30 with temozolomide for fit younger patients. Frail or elderly (>65–70) or poor prognosis: 40.05 Gy/15 or 34 Gy/10; 25 Gy/5 for frail/elderly patients with smaller tumours. At least two VMAT arcs (often non-coplanar) give the best dosimetry; PTV 95% isodose coverage, median close to prescription.

3.1 Walkthrough

  1. 3D FLAIR and T2 fused with contrast T1; compare with pre-op imaging.
  2. GTV on FLAIR (grade 2: all abnormal signal felt to be tumour), plus cavity and any enhancing residual.
  3. CTV 10 mm (G2) or 15 mm (G3); trim at skull, falx, tentorium; keep across the corpus callosum or into the hippocampus if spread is possible.
  4. PTV ≤3 mm. OARs per EPTN (section 10), including hippocampi if sparing is attempted.

3.2 Pitfalls

  • Trimming the CTV off the hippocampus to spare memory when the tumour is medial temporal: Baumert says do not.
  • Grade 3: including vasogenic oedema inflates the volume; use PET or perfusion if unsure.
  • Post-operative ischaemia on diffusion MRI can mimic tumour; compare with the 72-h MRI.
  • Metal artefact from clips: use artefact-suppression sequences (Baumert).

Fields of the older era (EORTC/RTOG) used CT oedema + 20 mm or FLAIR + 20 mm; the 2025 guideline reduced margins for MRI-defined targets (Baumert Table 2). Baumert 2025 values verified from the paper; the site's earlier "check" on this item is cleared.

Section 5 · Scenario D

5Vestibular schwannoma (ISRS, Tsao 2017)

  • Imaging: volumetric 3D post-gadolinium T1 (1–1.5 mm) with T2 (CISS/FIESTA-type) to see the nerve and the canal fundus.
  • GTV: enhancing tumour including the intracanalicular part; dose prescribed to the GTV margin.
  • PTV: 0 mm with a frame; 0–2 mm for relocatable frames/FSRT.
  • Koos grade: 1 intracanalicular; 2 into the CPA; 3 reaches the brainstem; 4 deforms the brainstem and shifts the 4th ventricle.
Regimen (ISRS)Dose
Single-fraction RS11–14 Gy to the GTV margin (strong consensus); 12–14 Gy series: 5-y hearing preservation 41–79%
Hypofractionated5 Gy × 5, 3 Gy × 10, 4 Gy × 10, 6 Gy × 3, 4 Gy × 5 (moderate consensus)
Conventional FSRT50–57.6 Gy in 1.8–2.0 Gy

Pseudoprogression in 20–30% within ~3 years; observe if no progressive mass effect.

6.1 Selection and dose (Canberra)

  • 1–5 metastases (more at RO discretion); intact ≤40 mm and cavity ≤50 mm; lesion ≥8 mm; single-fraction PTV ≤10 cc intact or ≤15 cc cavity; total ≤15 cc (hypofractionated <35 cc); ECOG 0–2.
  • Intact, by diameter (eviQ): ≤20 mm 18–24 Gy/1; 20–30 mm 18 Gy/1; 30–40 mm 15–16 Gy/1; 20–40 mm 24–30 Gy/3–5. TROG: ≤20 mm 20 ± 2 Gy/1; 20–40 mm 24 Gy/3; brainstem 22–24 Gy/3.
  • Cavity (eviQ): ≤20 mm 16–20 Gy/1 or 24–30 Gy/3–5; 20–30 mm 14–18 Gy/1 or 24–30 Gy/3–5; 30–50 mm 12–14 Gy/1 or 25–40 Gy/5–10.
  • Prescribe to the 100% isodose: PTV D99 = 100%, CI 1.0–2.0 (consider hypofractionation >1.5), min >95%, max 125–142.9% inside GTV.

Section 7 · Scenario F

7Hippocampal-avoidance whole brain (RTOG 0933; NRG CC001)

  • CTV: whole brain parenchyma to C1 (C2 if posterior fossa metastases).
  • Hippocampus: on thin-slice contrast T1 — the T1-hypointense grey matter medial to the temporal horn, separate from the T1-bright parahippocampal gyrus (below-medial) and fimbria (above-medial). Start at the floor of the temporal horn; medial edge up to the ambient cistern; the uncal recess separates it from the amygdala (anterior/superior); end at the hippocampal tail beside the atrium (Gondi 2010).
  • Hippocampal avoidance region = hippocampi + 5 mm.
  • PTV = CTV minus the avoidance region; no set-up margin added (NRG CC001).
  • Dose 30 Gy/10 with memantine (CC001: lower risk of cognitive failure, HR 0.74).
  • Metastases within 5 mm of the hippocampi were 3.3% in the RTOG 0933 data — the reason the avoidance zone is 5 mm.

Section 8

8Pearls and common errors

  • Old MRI: glioblastoma can grow between the post-op scan and RT; plan on MRI ≤2 weeks old (Niyazi).
  • Bone window to trim the skull; do not trim the corpus callosum or peduncles.
  • Ventricles: allow 5 mm into them, not 0 (ependymal spread).
  • Meningioma dural tail is not automatically GTV (RTOG 0539).
  • Cavity SRS: draw on the planning MRI, but check the pre-op MRI for dural/sinus contact; T2 can show the cavity edge better (Canberra note).
  • Surgical tract: consensus includes it, though a later failure analysis did not show better control with it (Canberra caveat).
  • Hippocampus contours drift: check on sagittal; avoid including the amygdala or fimbria.
  • Brain dose for SRS includes the PTV (V12, V14 per HyTEC).

10Organs at risk and constraints

10.1 Delineation

  • Minimum set for glioblastoma: optic nerves, chiasm, eyes, lenses, brain, brainstem; also cochleae, lacrimal glands, pituitary, hypothalamus and hippocampi as guidance (Niyazi 2023). Use the EPTN atlas (2018; 2021 update) for definitions.
  • ISLHD atlas: brainstem on MRI from the foramen magnum to the first slice of the cerebral peduncles; spinal canal from the bottom of the brainstem; cochlea on CT bone window with a 2 mm brush, the smaller, more medial cavity under the mastoid air cells.
  • SRS (Canberra): lenses, eyes, optic apparatus, brainstem, brain and spinal cord by RTs, approved by the RO; others as needed.
