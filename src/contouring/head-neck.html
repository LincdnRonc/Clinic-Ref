Head and Neck — Target Volume Delineation Protocol

Target volumes for definitive mucosal SCC (primary "5 + 5 mm" CTV-P1/P2, nodal CTV-N-HR and elective levels by subsite and N stage), post-operative volumes (DAHANCA; TORS/PATHOS risk groups; flaps), nasopharynx (2024 international dose-level guideline), cutaneous SCC (HNCIG) and perineural spread, with the full neck node level border tables, doses, constraints and names.

Governing principles of this protocol
  • No departmental head and neck protocol is in the folder; the page follows the DAHANCA/Grégoire international framework (GTV → CTV1 +5 mm → CTV2 +10 mm total, elective CTV3), Biau 2019 for which levels, and Grégoire 2014 for level borders. Local PTV and IGRT need confirming CHECK.
  • Geometric then anatomic: grow the GTV, then edit for air, uninvolved bone (mandible, maxilla, spine, hyoid), fascia and non-contiguous mucosa — unless infiltrated (Grégoire 2018).
  • Total GTV to CTV2 margin ≤12 mm (DAHANCA).
  • Elective nodes are whole levels, chosen by subsite and N stage; add the next level, and muscle/skin/parotid next to an involved node with ENE.
  • Use post-induction volumes with care: primary and nodal high-risk CTVs keep the pre-chemotherapy extent at lower dose (Biau; Lin 2025).

Section 1

1Simulation and imaging

  • Supine, thermoplastic head-and-shoulder mask; mouth bite/tongue depressor as needed; planning CT from a few cm above the skull base to a few cm below the clavicles, 2–3 mm slices (preferably 2 mm) with IV contrast (Biau 2019).
  • Fuse diagnostic MRI (oral cavity, oropharynx, nasopharynx, sinonasal, PNI) and FDG-PET; re-look at nodes on the planning CT, since disease may have changed since staging (Biau).
  • Pathological node criteria (Biau): short axis >10 mm (5–8 mm for retropharyngeal; 12–15 mm for upper level II), necrosis, round shape, clusters, ENE signs (skin, muscle or adjacent structure fixation, nerve palsy).
  • After induction chemotherapy: co-register the pre-chemotherapy imaging (Biau; Lin 2025).
  • Post-op: wire scars; use the operative note, pathology (margins, ENE, PNI) and pre-op imaging; delineate flaps from the operative report (flap guideline 2020).

3Definitive: primary CTV (Grégoire 2018; DAHANCA 2018)

VolumeDefinitionDose (DAHANCA SIB)
GTV-T / GTV-NAll gross tumour and involved nodes from examination, endoscopy, CT, MRI and PET—
CTV1 (CTV-P1)GTV + 5 mm isotropic; larger if poorly defined, smaller into air, uninvolved bone or natural borders. Uninvolved bone not included66–68 Gy in 33–34
CTV2 (CTV-P2)GTV + 10 mm (i.e. CTV1 + 5 mm), edited for air, bone, fascia and non-contiguous mucosa; may add a high-risk anatomical subregion. GTV to CTV2 ≤12 mm60 Gy
CTV3CTV2 + elective nodal levels without margin (section 4)50 Gy
PTV1–3CTV + set-up margin from departmental data; PTVs may be split near surfaces or OARs—

3.1 Edits to the geometric expansion

  • Air: always remove pharyngeal, laryngeal, oral, nasal and sinus air.
  • Complex anatomy: a soft palate GTV expansion is edited off the mobile tongue; an interarytenoid GTV off the post-cricoid area.
  • Barriers: mandible, maxilla, cervical spine, hyoid and muscular fascia — edit unless likely infiltrated.
  • Small tumours after endoscopic/TORS surgery did well with smaller margins (selected patients).
  • Pathology: 4–5 mm microscopic spread beyond the GTV covers most tumours; one series saw up to 15 mm after induction therapy (Grégoire 2018 review).

3.2 Walkthrough

  1. GTV on fused MRI/PET/CT with the endoscopy drawing.
  2. zGTV+5 → CTV1; zGTV+10 → CTV2; edit both for air and barriers.
  3. Check CTV2 on coronal and sagittal for submucosal routes (e.g. pre-epiglottic space, parapharyngeal space, base of tongue).
  4. Add elective levels (CTV3) and nodal CTVs (section 4).

Section 4 · Scenario A (cont.)

4Nodal CTVs and level selection by subsite (Biau 2019)

  • CTV-N-HR (involved node) = GTV-N + 5 mm: ENE extends <5 mm beyond the capsule in 96–97% of nodes. After induction chemotherapy, use the pre-chemotherapy node + 5 mm.
  • Node abutting muscle, skin or parotid, or with clear ENE: include those structures near the node in the elective CTV for at least the whole invaded level and at least 1 cm in all directions.
  • cN+: add the adjacent level (e.g. bulky upper II → VIIb; II abutting the submandibular gland → Ib). IIb may be omitted in a node-negative ipsilateral neck. IVb if IVa involved.

5Post-operative, TORS and flaps

VolumeDAHANCA 2018 post-operativeDose
CTV1Only after R1/R2 resection or for ENE: the pre-operative tumour (non-radical area) + 5 mm, more if ill-defined, edited for air and uninvolved bone66–68 Gy

5.1 TORS oropharynx — PATHOS risk groups

GroupPathologyTrial arms
ANo adverse featuresNo adjuvant treatment
BT3 (or T1–2 with risk factors), N2a/N2b, PNI or LVI, close margins 1–5 mm, no ECS60 Gy/30 vs 50 Gy/25

PATHOS (Owadally 2015, BMC Cancer): HPV+ OPSCC T1–T3 N0–N2b treated with transoral surgery and neck dissection.

5.2 Flaps (2020 guideline)

  • Identify the flap type, size and position from the operative report (FAMM, pectoralis, radial forearm, anterolateral thigh, fibula, scapula).
  • Delineate the flap body and the vascular pedicle separately; split into fat, fascia/muscle, skin and bone components where possible.
  • The flap is not the tumour bed: the CTV is the interface where tumour was, not the whole flap; record flap dose for future dose–effect work.

5.3 Pitfalls

  • Drawing the post-op CTV on altered anatomy without the pre-op GTV.
  • Missing the neck dissection bed or scar when ENE was present.

6.1 Elective nodal coverage

  • Bilateral lateral retropharyngeal nodes in all (medial group rarely involved) — 50 Gy.
  • Bilateral level II (up to skull base) in all — 50 Gy.
  • Bilateral III and Va in all — 50 Gy.
  • IV and Vb–c only if III/Va on that side is involved (upper neck irradiation was non-inferior in N0–1: 3-y R-FFS 97.7% vs 96.3%, Tang trial); extend to the clavicle and ≥2 cm below the lowest GTVn on an involved side.
  • Ib only with specific indications (e.g. Ib nodes) — see paper.

6.2 Pitfalls

  • Using the post-induction GTV but forgetting that the pre-induction extent needs ≥60 Gy.
  • Covering medial retropharyngeal nodes increases dysphagia without benefit (Mao data cited).
  • Example CTVp_50 structures for an eccentric T1 tumour: vomer, lower 5 mm of sphenoid sinus, both foramina lacerum, ipsilateral foramen ovale, anterior third of clivus (paper Fig. 1).
  • 2017 international NPC CTV guideline (Lee) is also in the folder for the older approach.

Bolus as needed over scars and superficial targets; IGRT recommended for IMRT (HNCIG). N0 undissected neck levels follow Grégoire 2014 with skin-specific modifications (e.g. Xa, Xb, VIII for scalp/periauricular primaries).

7.1 Perineural invasion (Ko 2014)

  • Follow the named nerve back from the tumour bed toward the skull base along its foramina: e.g. infraorbital nerve → infraorbital foramen → orbit floor → foramen rotundum (V2); inferior alveolar/mental → mandibular foramen → foramen ovale (V3); facial nerve → stylomastoid foramen → internal acoustic meatus.
  • Include interconnections (auriculotemporal nerve links V3 and VII; greater superficial petrosal via the pterygopalatine ganglion).
  • Doses: microscopic PNI, negative nerve margin ≥54 Gy to the nerve course; gross PNI, negative margin 60 Gy; gross PNI with positive margin 66 Gy to the bed and 60 Gy along the nerve, within brainstem tolerance.

Section 8

8Pearls and common errors

  • Level IVa ends 2 cm above the manubrium; IVb (medial SCF) is added only when IVa is involved (Biau).
  • Level V starts at the hyoid, not at the SCM–trapezius convergence; upper "V" above the hyoid belongs to Xb (Grégoire 2014).
  • VIIa covers lateral retropharyngeal nodes only; VIIb (retro-styloid) is for bulky upper II.
  • Retropharyngeal node threshold is small (5–8 mm short axis).
  • CTV expansions across bone or air look large but deliver dose for nothing; edit them.
  • Do not edit CTV off muscle when a node with ENE abuts it — include ≥1 cm of the muscle (Biau).
  • Pre-epiglottic and paraglottic spaces, parapharyngeal fat and the base of tongue are the routes most often under-drawn on axial CT; check sagittal.

ROI algebra (definitive)
  1. CTV1 = (GTVp + 5 mm) ∪ (GTVn + 5 mm) − air − uninvolved bone
  2. CTV2 = (GTVp + 10 mm) − air − bone − barriers ∪ CTV1 (check ≤12 mm from GTV)
  3. CTV3 = CTV2 ∪ elective levels
  4. PTVs = CTV + departmental margin; PTV_6000only = PTV_6000 − PTV_7000; PTV_5000only = PTV_5000 − PTV_6000
