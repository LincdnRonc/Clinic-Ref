Rhabdomyosarcoma

Dose depends on the surgical group, not stage. Treat pre-chemo volumes (unlike neuroblastoma) with IMRT GTV + 1 cm. Favourable sites: orbit, non-parameningeal head and neck, GU (not bladder or prostate).

Dose by group (COG)

GroupDefinitionDose
IR0, N0RT only for alveolar (fusion-positive) histology: 36 Gy

IVMetastaticPrimary and metastatic sites; SBRT 30–35/5 or whole lung 12–15 Gy (ARST 1431)
  • Volumes: IMRT GTV (pre-chemo extent, edited for anatomy) + 1 cm CTV + 0.3 cm PTV.
  • Delayed primary excision only if R0/R1 is achievable; otherwise give the full dose.
  • Tumours ≥5 cm have about 20–25% local failure (HR 2.0), hence the 59.4 Gy boost.
  • Breaks: add 1 fraction for a 2–3 week break, 2 fractions if >3 weeks.
  • Metastatic with 0–1 Oberlin risk factors: 3 y EFS nearly 70%.

Ewing sarcoma

VDC/IE induction, then local therapy (surgery, RT or both), then consolidation. 5 y OS is about 85% for localised disease, and <30% without local therapy.

Wilms tumour (COG)

Most children are cured with surgery and chemotherapy. Flank RT is for stage III and unfavourable histology. Doses are low.

When and how much

  • No flank RT: stage I–II favourable histology, stage I CCSK. Very low risk (<2 y, stage I FH, <550 g): no adjuvant therapy.
  • Flank RT (stage III, or any stage with unfavourable histology except stage I CCSK): 10.8/6; 19.8/11 for diffuse anaplasia or rhabdoid tumour. Boost residual disease.
  • Flank volume: pre-op GTV + 1 cm; cover the whole vertebral body to avoid scoliosis.
  • Whole abdomen (diffuse spill, peritoneal disease): 10.5/7; just over 20 Gy for diffuse unresectable peritoneal metastases. Limit the contralateral kidney to 14.4 Gy.
  • Whole lung: 12/8 if >12 months old (otherwise 10.5/7).
  • Stage IV/V FH with lung metastases: 4 y OS >90%.

Neuroblastoma and retinoblastoma

High-risk neuroblastoma: RT to the primary after surgery and stem cell transplant. Unlike RMS, treat the post-chemo/pre-surgery tumour. Retinoblastoma is now mostly treated without EBRT.

High-risk neuroblastoma

  • Dose: 21.6/12 to the tumour at surgery (ANBL 0532). Boost gross residual disease (previously 36 Gy to residual >1 cm³).
  • If the CTV reaches a vertebral body, include the whole vertebral body.
  • ANBL 1531 constraints: ipsilateral kidney mean 18 Gy; contralateral kidney 18 Gy to 25% and 8 Gy to 50%; liver mean 15 Gy; lung 20 Gy to 30%.

Retinoblastoma

  • Heritable (germline RB1) disease carries a high risk of second tumours, especially after RT. Avoid EBRT where possible.
  • Regional extraocular disease (stage II–III): chemo × 4 → 45 Gy involved-field RT.
  • EBRT to the globe if needed: 36–45 Gy.
  • CNS spread: 36 Gy CSI, 45 Gy cranium, 50.4 Gy pineal.
