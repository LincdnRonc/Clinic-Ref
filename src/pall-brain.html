Brain metastases

SRS for 1–4 metastases (and conditionally 5–10). After resection, give cavity SRS. If WBRT is needed, use hippocampal avoidance with memantine. For poor-prognosis patients, steroids and supportive care may be as good as WBRT (QUARTZ).

Decisions (ASTRO and ASCO/SNO/ASTRO guidelines)

  • 1–4 intact metastases, good PS: SRS alone (strong). 5–10: SRS conditionally recommended.
  • Resected: cavity SRS (strong) rather than WBRT.
  • WBRT: if favourable prognosis, use HA-WBRT 30/10 + memantine (no hippocampal lesions, expected survival ≥4 months).
  • Asymptomatic, CNS-active systemic therapy available: deferring local therapy is conditionally acceptable (for example EGFR/ALK NSCLC, some melanoma).
  • Poor prognosis (KPS ≤50, or ≤70 without systemic options): best supportive care. QUARTZ showed no meaningful QALY gain from WBRT in NSCLC.
  • Steroids: dexamethasone for symptoms only (e.g. 4–8 mg; 10 mg load then 4–6 mg q6h for severe symptoms) with PPI. No routine antiepileptic prophylaxis.
  • Use the disease-specific GPA (brainmetgpa.com) for prognosis.

>4 cmDiscuss surgery; fractionated SRS if not resectable
CavitySingle fraction (12–20 Gy by size, N107C) or fractionated 27/3 or 30/5; fractionate cavities >3 cm
  • Radionecrosis: single-fraction brain V12 >8–8.5 cc → >10% risk. 27/3: keep V18 <30.2 cc (about 5% risk). Cavity 27/3: V24 <16.8 cc.
  • Brainstem (JLGK0901): 20 Gy if <1 cc, 18 Gy if <4 cc, 16 Gy if <10 cc.
  • HA-WBRT (NRG CC001): hippocampal D100% ≤9 Gy, Dmax ≤16 Gy; PTV D98% ≥25 Gy.
