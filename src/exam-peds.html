RETINOBLASTOMA LD

Epidemiology: 5th most common solid paeds malignancy; most common ocular tumour; ~90% <5y; M=F.

Hx/Exam: Leukocoria, strabismus, ↓vision, redness/pain; assess for bilateral/multifocal disease; FHx RB/early cancers.

Investigations

Ophthalmic exam under anaesthesia with indirect ophthalmoscopy.

Imaging: Ocular US (calcifications), MRI brain/orbits (optic nerve, chiasm, extraocular, trilateral RB). Avoid biopsy (seeding).

Genetics: RB1 germline testing; counsel for surveillance (contralateral eye, pineal region).

Diagnosis is clinical (no biopsy). LD

Unilateral, intraocular

Group A: focal therapy only (laser/cryotherapy/TTT).

Group B: systemic VCE (vincristine/carbo/etoposide) 2–6 cycles → focal consolidation.

Group C: VCE → focal after ~6 cycles.

Group D: VCE; attempt eye salvage; consider intra-arterial melphalan/topotecan ± intravitreal chemo for seeding; focal as feasible.

Group E: Primary enucleation.

Bilateral: Chemo-based eye salvage prioritizing better-seeing eye; focal consolidation; enucleate non-salvageable eye (often E).

Extraocular/ON+ at margin/high-risk pathology: Enucleation + adjuvant chemo (e.g., VCE); consider RT for pathologically high-risk features.

Refractory/relapsed/vitreous seeds: Intravitreal chemo (melphalan), intra-arterial chemo, plaque BT.

Metastatic/CNS: Intensive multi-agent chemo ± autologous transplant; CSI rarely considered in select leptomeningeal cases.

Systemic: Vincristine + carboplatin + etoposide (VCE).

Radiation therapy technique LD

Indications

  • Intraocular salvage when chemo/focal inadequate or not feasible.
  • Adjuvant post-enucleation for high-risk pathology (e.g., post-laminar ON invasion to margin, massive choroidal/scleral/extraocular extension).
  • Palliative/rare CNS spread scenarios.

External beam RT (EBRT)

Dose: 45 Gy/25# (1.8 Gy/#).

Target: Entire globe + 1 cm proximal optic nerve; include chiasm if ON involved to canal/chiasm suspected.

Technique

Lens-sparing 3DCRT: lateral 6 MV half-beam block; posteriorly angled to spare contralateral eye; posterior field as needed.

Consider bolus for anterior segment involvement.

Alternatives: direct anterior photon/electron (accept cataract risk); IMRT for conformity/sparing.

Setup: Immobilize head; eye positioning/retinal fixation if possible; shield contralateral eye.

Genetics: ~60% sporadic unilateral; ~40% germline

Origin/macro: Arises from retina; chalky white mass, often calcified; endophytic (into vitreous) vs exophytic (subretinal); can fill globe → proptosis; optic nerve invasion → leptomeningeal/CSF spread;

LN+ rare (anterior segment/conjunctiva/sclera).

Staging/classification: International Intraocular Classification (Groups A–E).

  • A: <3 mm tumours.
  • B: >3 mm or close to fovea/optic disc.
  • C: focal vitreous/subretinal seeding, deposits <3 mm from tumour.
  • D: diffuse vitreous/subretinal seeding, deposits >3 mm.
  • E: >2/3 globe, lens involvement, neovascular glaucoma/hemorrhage, phthisis—“eye destroyed.”
