Craniospinal irradiation and paediatric CNS

Volumes follow the current COG, SIOP-Europe or ACNS protocol for each tumour. The SIOPE CSI guideline (Ajithkumar 2018) is the reference for the craniospinal CTV, which is where most marginal misses happen.

CSI CTV: the areas that get missed

  • Brain: the whole brain and meninges, including the cribriform plate and the inferior frontal lobes, the inferior temporal lobes (middle cranial fossa) and the posterior fossa down to the foramen magnum.
  • Optic nerves back to the globe, and the CSF around the skull-base foramina and cranial nerve roots.
  • Spine: the whole thecal sac to its end on sagittal MRI (often about S2, but check each patient), with lateral coverage of the nerve-root sleeves.

Boosts and focal volumes (protocol-based)

TumourTypical CTV approach
MedulloblastomaTumour bed and residual + margin (ACNS0331 used about 1.5 cm, edited for anatomy) after CSI.
EpendymomaTumour bed and residual + about 1 cm (ACNS0121), edited at bone and tentorium.
GerminomaWhole ventricles (whole-ventricular RT), then a boost to the primary.
DIPG / diffuse midline gliomaT2/FLAIR GTV + about 1 cm, edited.

Margins are as used in the named trials; confirm against the current protocol.

Paediatric solid tumours

The common principle: the CTV is based on the pre-chemotherapy tumour extent, then pulled back where normal tissue has moved back after the tumour shrank (for example lung or bowel that the tumour had pushed aside but not invaded).

By tumour

TumourTypical CTV approach
RhabdomyosarcomaPre-chemo GTV (and involved nodes) + about 1 cm, edited for anatomy and pushing borders (COG, EpSSG).
Ewing sarcomaPre-chemo bone and soft-tissue extent + about 1–1.5 cm, edited where soft tissue has returned to normal; boost to the post-chemo volume.
Wilms tumour (flank)Pre-operative kidney and tumour + margin, as the protocol defines; cover the full vertebral body width to avoid asymmetric spinal growth where the field crosses the spine.
NeuroblastomaPre-surgery, post-chemo primary site + margin. Inter-observer variation is large (Jazmati 2023).
Hodgkin lymphomaInvolved-site RT (ILROG / EuroNet-PHL), see the lymphoma protocol.

These margins are typical of COG and SIOP protocols; check the current protocol before using them.

Paediatric OARs

Contour more OARs than you would in an adult, because late effects matter over a lifetime.

Commonly added structures

  • Hippocampi (consider sparing in CSI, Padovani 2018), pituitary and hypothalamus, cochleae, lens and lacrimal glands.
  • Growth plates and vertebral bodies, thyroid, breast buds, gonads, kidneys and heart, by field.
  • Teeth and the developing jaw for head and neck fields (Davey 2023 survey).
