Recurrent or second primary head and neck SCC

ReCOG/ESTRO/ASTRO consensus (Biau 2026), MIRI collaborative data and ARS 2022.

  • Review the previous plan to separate in-field recurrence from marginal failure (100% consensus).
  • Within 6 months of the first course: generally not advised, except urgent palliation such as uncontrolled bleeding.
  • 6–12 months: only highly selected cases: small, localised, outside the high-dose region, after MDT review.
  • Post-operative reirradiation: consider for RPA class I with positive margins or extracapsular extension; also where an R0 resection was unlikely even if pathology does not report a positive margin.
  • RPA class III should generally not be offered curative-intent reirradiation.
  • MIRI cohort (412 patients, IMRT, median 60 Gy): acute grade ≥3 toxicity 19%, grade 5 1.2%; 2-year grade ≥3 late toxicity 14.2%.

Technique (Biau 2026, panel 2)

  • Post-operative: normofractionated IMRT, not SBRT.
  • Unresected, RPA class II, ≤25 cc (about 3 cm, cT1–2): IMRT or SBRT give similar outcomes.
  • Unresected, RPA class II, >25 cc: IMRT gives better outcomes than SBRT.
  • SBRT for unresected laryngeal or hypopharyngeal recurrence: caution, higher risk of severe toxicity.
  • Concurrent chemotherapy: case by case, mainly large tumours in fit patients.
  • Protons can be considered to spare critical organs; comparative data are few.
  • Brachytherapy remains valid for small (<25 cc), well-delineated tumours.

MIRI dose analysis (Caudell 2018): ≥66 Gy for definitive IMRT in fit patients; 50–66 Gy adequate after resection of gross disease.

SBRT data (Pittsburgh)

  • Phase 2 (Vargo 2015, n=50, prior ≥60 Gy): 40–44 Gy in 5 fractions on alternate days with cetuximab: median OS 10 months, 1-year OS 40%.
  • Carotid (Gebhardt 2018, 75 patients): bleeding events 5.3%, carotid blowout 2.7%; no blowout with carotid D0.1cc <47.6 Gy from the SBRT course. Modelled bleeding risk 0.8% at D0.1cc 20 Gy and 5% at 50 Gy (Ling 2019).

Dose accumulation and constraints (Biau 2026, panels 4–5)

  • Retrieve the previous CT, contours and dose; use deformable registration with rigorous QA where available; express cumulative OAR dose in EQD2 (α/β 2–3 Gy); voxel-wise 3D EQD2 summation where possible. Conservative manual point-dose addition for cord, brainstem and carotids remains acceptable.
  • Carotid: cumulative EQD2 above 120–125 Gy (α/β 2) significantly raises blowout risk: avoid when feasible. Encasement beyond 180° and previous ulceration add risk; so does daily (not alternate-day) SBRT.
  • Carotid blowout in a 1554-patient reirradiation cohort with carotid involvement: 2.6%, with 76% mortality.
  • Mandible: osteoradionecrosis risk rises significantly above a cumulative EQD2 of 114–119 Gy; also depends on high-dose subvolume, dose per fraction, dental status and trauma. Reported rates 2–18%.
  • Spinal cord: target cumulative EQD2 D0.1cc ≤50 Gy. Brainstem: ≤55 Gy. Case by case, about 60 Gy at the surface may be accepted depending on interval, prior dose distribution and reliability of dose accumulation.
  • Recovery factors of 10–25% for cord or brainstem: use with caution and document explicitly.
  • Consent specifically for carotid blowout, osteoradionecrosis, fistula, feeding-tube and tracheostomy dependence, and treatment-related death.

Locally recurrent nasopharyngeal carcinoma

International recommendations (Ng 2021), the hyperfractionation trial (You 2023) and post-operative guidance (Li 2024).

Selection

  • Exclude nodal and distant disease (FDG-PET/CT); MDT review.
  • Surgery first for resectable recurrence where expertise exists. Post-operative reRT for a positive margin; consider for close margins.
  • Radical reRT not recommended at ≤6 months from the first course; some prefer 12 months.
  • Exclude if prior toxicity was grade ≥1 at brainstem, cord or chiasm, or grade ≥3 at temporal lobe, optic nerve, brachial plexus, soft tissue or bone.
  • Bulk alone is not an exclusion.

Volumes and dose

  • rGTV on co-registered MRI ± PET. CTV = rGTV + up to 5 mm, edited at barriers and critical OARs (0 mm acceptable next to them). PTV = CTV + 2–3 mm. No elective nodal irradiation.
  • 60–66 Gy EQD2; doses ≥68 Gy were associated with excess fatal toxicity.
  • Preferred: hyperfractionation 1.1–1.2 Gy twice daily, ≥6 hours apart (lower-agreement item in 2021, since supported by a phase 3 trial).
  • Priorities: (1) brainstem, cord, chiasm, both optic nerves, rGTV; (2) PTV, temporal lobe, one optic nerve; (3) carotid.
  • Chemotherapy recommended for rT3–4N0 and any node-positive recurrence; induction then concurrent cisplatin preferred.
