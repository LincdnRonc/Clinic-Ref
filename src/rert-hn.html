Recurrent or second primary head and neck SCC

MIRI collaborative data, ARS 2022 and the 2026 ReCOG/ESTRO/ASTRO consensus.

  • 412 patients, IMRT ≥40 Gy after ≥40 Gy; median reRT dose 60 Gy; median interval 2.4 years; chemotherapy in about three quarters.
  • Class III patients are poor candidates for protracted chemoradiation whether resected or not: consider SBRT, systemic therapy or supportive care.
  • Toxicity: acute grade ≥3 19%, grade 5 1.2%; 2-year grade ≥3 late toxicity 14.2%.
  • Surgery first when resectable; reirradiate post-operatively for positive margins or extranodal extension.

Dose and volumes

  • Definitive IMRT: ≥66 Gy may give better outcomes in fit patients (Caudell 2018, MIRI).
  • Post-operative, gross disease removed: 50–66 Gy appears adequate (Caudell 2018).
  • No clear benefit from elective nodal irradiation in the MIRI analysis: treat recurrent disease with a small margin. Verify for the margin: 5 mm GTV-to-CTV is typical.
  • Hyperfractionation (1.2 Gy bd to 60 Gy with chemotherapy) was the schedule of RTOG 9610 and 9911. Verify
  • Concurrent systemic therapy: platinum, or cetuximab with SBRT; no randomised proof of benefit over reRT alone.

SBRT (Pittsburgh experience)

  • Phase 2 (Vargo 2015, n=50, prior ≥60 Gy): 40–44 Gy in 5 fractions on alternate days with cetuximab (400 mg/m² day −7, 250 mg/m² days 0 and +8): median OS 10 months, 1-year OS 40%.
  • Dose by size in the later series: GTV <25 cm³ 40 Gy; >25 cm³ 44–50 Gy in 5 fractions, at most 3 fractions a week.
  • Carotid (Gebhardt 2018, 75 patients): bleeding events 5.3%, carotid blowout 2.7%; no blowout with carotid D0.1cc <47.6 Gy; complete encasement was not excluded. Modelled bleeding risk 0.8% at D0.1cc 20 Gy and 5% at 50 Gy (Ling 2019).
  • Earlier series linked blowout to >180° carotid involvement, skin invasion or ulceration, and daily fractionation.

Cumulative constraints and consent

  • Carotid: cumulative about 120 Gy EQD2 (Biau 2026).
  • Mandible: below about 114–119 Gy where feasible (Biau 2026).
  • Cord, brainstem, optic: see cumulative limits.
  • Consent specifically for carotid blowout, osteoradionecrosis, fistula, feeding-tube and tracheostomy dependence, and treatment-related death.

Locally recurrent nasopharyngeal carcinoma

International recommendations (Ng 2021), the hyperfractionation trial (You 2023) and post-operative guidance (Li 2024).

Selection

  • Exclude nodal and distant disease (FDG-PET/CT); MDT review.
  • Surgery first for resectable recurrence where expertise exists. Post-operative reRT for a positive margin; consider for close margins.
  • Radical reRT not recommended at ≤6 months from the first course; some prefer 12 months.
  • Exclude if prior toxicity was grade ≥1 at brainstem, cord or chiasm, or grade ≥3 at temporal lobe, optic nerve, brachial plexus, soft tissue or bone.
  • Bulk alone is not an exclusion.

Volumes and dose

  • rGTV on co-registered MRI ± PET. CTV = rGTV + up to 5 mm, edited at barriers and critical OARs (0 mm acceptable next to them). PTV = CTV + 2–3 mm. No elective nodal irradiation.
  • 60–66 Gy EQD2; doses ≥68 Gy were associated with excess fatal toxicity.
  • Preferred: hyperfractionation 1.1–1.2 Gy twice daily, ≥6 hours apart (lower-agreement item in 2021, since supported by a phase 3 trial).
  • Priorities: (1) brainstem, cord, chiasm, both optic nerves, rGTV; (2) PTV, temporal lobe, one optic nerve; (3) carotid.
  • Chemotherapy recommended for rT3–4N0 and any node-positive recurrence; induction then concurrent cisplatin preferred.
