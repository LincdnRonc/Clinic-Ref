Unresectable stage III NSCLC

Concurrent chemoRT to 60 Gy, then a year of durvalumab (or osimertinib for EGFR-mutant disease). Do not escalate above 60–66 Gy outside trials. Decide resectability before starting.

Decisions

  • Standard: 60/30 with weekly carboplatin/paclitaxel or cisplatin/etoposide, then durvalumab for 1 y (PACIFIC). 60–63 Gy usually appropriate; up to 70 Gy may be considered (ARS 2024). Doses above 70 Gy are not appropriate.
  • EGFR-mutant: chemoRT followed by osimertinib (LAURA: median PFS 6 → 39 months).
  • Bulky disease where V20 or MLD is unacceptable: sequential chemotherapy then RT.
  • Not fit for concurrent: sequential chemo → RT ± durvalumab (PACIFIC-6, G3–4 AEs 19%). Or hypofractionated RT alone: 60/15 or 55–60/20 with IMRT.
  • Poor PS, symptomatic: palliative 30/10 or 45/15; 20/5 or 1–2 fractions also appropriate.
  • Concurrent durvalumab: no benefit (PACIFIC-2). Start durvalumab after chemoRT.
  • PCI: not standard for NSCLC. RTOG 0214 reduced brain metastasis (1 y 18 → 8%) without an OS benefit.

Resectable stage III and PORT

Perioperative chemo-immunotherapy is now standard for resectable stage II–III disease. PORT halves mediastinal relapse but does not improve DFS or OS for completely resected N2. Reserve it for R1/R2 or very high-risk N2.

PORT

  • R0 pN2: not routine. Discuss for high-risk N2 (multistation, ECE, low nodal sampling). If given: 50–54 Gy sequentially after chemotherapy.
  • R1 or ECE: 54–60 Gy. Concurrent chemoRT then chemo for a positive margin.
  • R2: 60 Gy or more, with concurrent chemotherapy.
  • pN1: PORT only if not receiving chemotherapy (ANITA).
  • Volumes: CTV = bronchial stump + involved stations + ipsilateral hilum, mediastinum ± subcarinal (follow Lung ART). PTV = CTV + 5 mm with daily CBCT (up to 1 cm otherwise).

Neoadjuvant and perioperative options

  • Chemo-immunotherapy → surgery: nivolumab (CheckMate 816), pembrolizumab (KEYNOTE-671) or durvalumab (AEGEAN), with adjuvant immunotherapy for the latter two.
  • Superior sulcus (T3–4 N0–1): chemoRT 45 Gy with cisplatin/etoposide → resection (INT 0160). 5 y OS 54% after R0, 44% overall. pCR 29%. Brain is the commonest site of failure.
  • cN2 (single station, <3 cm, lobectomy planned): may be surgical. Multistation N2, N3, ECE or progression on chemotherapy → definitive chemoRT.

Planning for conventional thoracic RT

Use 4DCT and IMRT. Involved-field only (no elective nodal RT). Watch lung V20, mean heart dose, heart V50 and oesophageal V60.

Volumes

  • GTV: primary and nodes ≥1 cm short axis or PET-avid (SUV >3). Include 2+ nodes in a high-risk station.
  • CTV: GTV + 5–8 mm, edited off bone and uninvolved structures. ESTRO-ACROP option: iGTVn + 5 mm and iGTVp + 8 mm.
  • PTV: CTV + 5 mm with ITV and daily IGRT. Without ITV: free breathing 1.5 cm craniocaudal and 1 cm axial; DIBH 1 cm craniocaudal and 0.5 cm axial.
  • Motion >7–10 mm: use active breathing control or gating. Motion <7 mm: ITV.
