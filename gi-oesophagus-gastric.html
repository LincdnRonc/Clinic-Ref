Oesophagus

Adenocarcinoma has moved towards perioperative FLOT (ESOPEC); CROSS remains the standard for SCC and for patients unfit for FLOT. Use 50 Gy for definitive chemoRT: dose escalation has not helped.

Anatomy and nodal risk

SegmentFrom incisorsNodes at risk
Cervical15–20 cm (cricoid to thoracic inlet)Supraclavicular, neck

Lower thoracic30–40 cm (to GOJ)Paraoesophageal, coeliac, lesser curve
Abdominal / GOJ40–45 cmCoeliac, left gastric, gastrohepatic; splenic for Siewert II–III
  • Siewert: I = 1–5 cm above GOJ; II = 1 cm above to 2 cm below; III = 2–5 cm below (treated as gastric).
  • Node risk: T1a 7% · T1b (submucosa) ~20% · T2 ~40%. No serosa, so early spread.
  • AJCC 8: location by epicentre; coeliac nodes are regional for lower/GOJ tumours.
  • 5-y OS: stage I 70–80%, roughly halving with each stage; pCR at any stage behaves like stage I.

Work-up

  • Gastroscopy + biopsy; EUS ± FNA for T/N (T ~90%, N 80–85% sensitivity).
  • CT chest/abdomen ± pelvis, then PET-CT (finds M1 in ~15%; N sensitivity ~60%).
  • Bronchoscopy if at or above carina (exclude TOF). Staging laparoscopy for GOJ/Siewert II–III may upstage 10–15%.
  • Biomarkers: HER2 (10% oesophageal, up to 33% GOJ), PD-L1 CPS, MMR/MSI.
  • Dietitian early; consider feeding tube if dysphagia and weight loss. Smoking cessation.

Organ preservation

  • SANO: cCR after 41.4/23 nCRT → active surveillance non-inferior to surgery (2-y OS 74% vs 71%). About half avoided surgery; durable cCR in only ~10% overall.
  • Response assessment at 6 and 12 wk with endoscopy + bite-on-bite biopsies, EUS-FNA, PET-CT.
  • SCC is more radiosensitive (CROSS pCR 49% SCC vs 23% AC), so organ preservation is more plausible.
  • FFCD 9102: in responders, more chemoRT was as good as surgery for OS with lower 3-mo mortality (9% → 1%), at the cost of more local failure and stents.

Palliation of dysphagia

  • RT or chemoRT preferred over stent for durable relief: 70–90% respond, 50–70% stay dysphagia-free until death; transient worsening in ~30%.
  • 20/5 vs 30/10 + 12/1 BT: persistent/recurrent dysphagia 64% → 42%; consider higher dose if longer life expectancy.
  • Brachytherapy gives more durable relief and fewer complications than stenting; BT meta: G3–4 fistula 8%, stenosis 12%.
  • ROCS: adding RT to stent did not reduce dysphagia deterioration (~47%) but delayed bleeding.
  • Stent in situ before radical treatment: consider induction chemo then remove the stent (stent + RT morbidity).

Gastric

Perioperative FLOT is the standard; RT is now for incomplete surgery, margin-positive disease, patients unfit for perioperative chemo, and palliation (ASTRO 2025).

Doses and volumes

  • Pre- or postop: 45/25 (strong). Boost 5.4–9 Gy for R2/gross residual. Definitive: 45/25 CTV + 54–59.4 Gy GTV.
  • Preop CTV: whole stomach, perigastric extension, ≥1 cm proximal oesophagus (more for cardia/GOJ) and regional nodes (coeliac, common hepatic, splenic, left gastric, hepatoduodenal, peripancreatic, SMA, regional para-aortic).
  • Postop CTV: tumour bed, anastomoses, duodenal stump, 2 cm beyond margins, regional nodes. Include gastric remnant only if margin positive. Proximal third: add paraoesophageal and hepatogastric; middle/distal: supra- and infrapyloric.
  • Coverage: preop PTV D95 ≥95%, D2cc ≤107%; postop PTV D100 ≥95%.
  • Fast 2 h before sim and treatment. 4DCT/motion management with IMRT; daily IGRT.
  • D2 dissection: recurrences still locoregional as often as distant; >¾ of regional relapses outside the D2 field (stations 16a2, 8, 12, 16b1, 9).

Systemic therapy pointers

  • FLOT ×4 → surgery → FLOT ×4 (5-FU 2.6 g/m² CI, leucovorin, oxaliplatin, docetaxel).
  • Metastatic HER2-negative, CPS ≥5: nivolumab + chemo. HER2+: trastuzumab + pembrolizumab + chemo; trastuzumab deruxtecan after progression. Second line: ramucirumab + paclitaxel.
  • Test HER2 (~20% gastric, higher in intestinal type), PD-L1, MMR.

Oesophagus planning

Simulation, volumes and constraints for oesophageal and GOJ cancer.

Simulation and IGRT

  • Fast ≥2 h (empty stomach), arms up on wingboard/vac-lok, IV contrast, 4DCT.
  • Daily CBCT (or kV daily + CBCT days 1–3 then weekly). For GOJ, match on the gastrohepatic region.
  • Motion (fiducial study): CC peak-to-peak ~9 mm; required PTV with CTV-based match ~7 mm LR, 8 mm AP, 11 mm CC.

Target volumes

  • CTVp: GTVp + 3–4 cm CC (≤2 cm into involved stomach), 1 cm radial (0.5 cm into heart/liver). Submucosal spread: 3 cm covers 94% of SCC; AC spreads further distally (5 cm covers 94%).
  • CTVn: GTVn + 0.5–1.5 cm.
  • PTV: CTV + 0.5–1.0 cm.
  • ENI by site: cervical → SCV ± higher neck; upper third → bilateral SCV + mediastinal; middle → paraoesophageal + mediastinal; distal/GOJ → paraoesophageal, lesser curve, coeliac; consider splenic for Siewert II–III.
  • PROTECT (involved-field) approach: GTVp + 3 cm CC with node stations along that length, GTVn + 1 cm, no extensive elective nodes.
  • Optional cone-down for small, non-operative tumours: 45/25 elective (4 cm CC) and 50/25 primary (2 cm CC) as SIB, then 54–60 Gy sequential to 1 cm.

Constraints (conventional)

OrganGoal
LungV20 <20%, V30 <15%, V10 <40%, V5 <50–60%, MLD <15 Gy
HeartV30 <20–30%, mean <20 Gy where possible (15–26 Gy used); V40 <30%, V50 <20%

Each 10% rise in heart V30 ≈ 24% higher cardiac event risk. Protons cut heart V5 87→42% and 2-y cardiac events 18→11% in one series. 3D vs IMRT: IMRT lowers heart high dose but spreads low lung dose.

Follow-up and failure patterns

  • H&P q3–6 mo ×2 y, q6–12 mo for 3 y, then annually. If organ-preserving: gastroscopy q3–6 mo ×2 y, q6 mo in year 3.
  • Most relapses within 2 y. After definitive 50.4 Gy (MDACC), local failure 50%, 90% within GTV; risks: T3+, >8 cm, poor PET response.
  • After CROSS, only ~1 in 8 fail locoregionally; distant failure dominates.
  • Strictures 12–30% after RT; balloon dilatation works in 80–90%.
