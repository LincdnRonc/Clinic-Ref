UTUC, urethra, penile, benign

Rare sites: doses and indications in one place.

Upper tract urothelial cancer

  • Any N+ is stage IV. Kidney-sparing surgery for low risk with two functioning kidneys.
  • High risk after nephroureterectomy: adjuvant platinum-gemcitabine (POUT: 3-y DFS 46→71%); adjuvant nivolumab if chemo unsuitable. Adjuvant RT data immature.
  • LRR risk rises with risk factors (pelvis + ureter, T2+, N+, G3, SM+): 5-y LRR 5% / 16% / 44% for 1 / 2 / ≥3. ART field renal fossa + RP nodes L1 to aortic bifurcation covers ~82% of failures.
  • Inoperable: definitive IMRT ≥60 Gy (retro dose-response); SBRT 40/8 alt days, 50/10 or 30–40/5 in elderly (high response, bladder recurrence ~15% at 1 y).

Urethral cancer

  • Clinically involved nodes are almost always positive (unlike penile/vulvar). Work-up: cystourethroscopy, MRI pelvis, chest imaging.
  • Tis/Ta/T1: TUR with 2 cm margin. T2–4 or N+: chemoRT (cisplatin-based for N+; 5-FU/MMC CR ~80%) or urethrectomy ± cystectomy.
  • EBRT: pelvis + inguinal nodes 45 Gy; primary + 2 cm and involved nodes to 66 Gy.
  • BT (lesions <4 cm, T1–early T2): LDR alone 60 Gy; boost after 45 Gy EBRT 20–30 Gy. Females: 8–12 needles to tumour + 1–2 cm, 60–70 Gy.
  • Perineal skin reaction limits EBRT.

Penile cancer

  • Nodes: up to 25% of cN0 are pN+; only ~50% of palpable nodes are positive. Surgical staging (dynamic SLNB) for cN0 ≥T1b. cN+: FNA, then ILND; PET-CT or CT staging.
  • Completion pelvic LND for ECE or ≥2 inguinal nodes; one node without ENE needs no PLND. Node >4 cm or fixed: neoadjuvant chemo (TIP).
  • Organ preservation: circumcise first. BT alone for <4 cm with <1 cm corporal invasion (T1–2): long-term penile preservation ~75–90%.
  • BT dose: LDR 60–65 Gy over 5–7 d, urethra ≤50 Gy; HDR e.g. 3 Gy BD ×9 d = 54 Gy (wait 6 h between). Penile V125 <40%, V150 <20%; urethra V115 <10%.
  • EBRT: shaft 45–50.4 Gy + primary + 2 cm to 65–70 Gy; uninvolved nodes 45–50, gross nodes 65–70. Use 2 Gy fractions, finish within 45 d. Concurrent cisplatin (or 5-FU/MMC).
  • Adjuvant RT (weak evidence): pN2–3; 45 Gy to IF/pelvic nodes, boost ECE/gross to 60–70.
  • Sim: supine, Foley, penis suspended in bolus; frog-leg for inguinals.
  • Toxicity: BT necrosis 3–15% (higher >60 Gy), urethral stricture 10–40%; lymphoedema 10–35% after inguinal RT (63% after ILND).
  • Follow-up: penile-sparing q3m ×2 y then q6m ×3 y; penectomy + LND q6m ×2 y then annually ×3 y.

Peyronie's disease (benign)

  • Transient in up to 50% within 12–18 mo. RT for early stage only.
  • Dose 20/10 (range 8–36 Gy at 2–3 Gy/fx), 4–8 MeV electrons or 4–6 MV photons, penis upright in a tube (DEGRO).
