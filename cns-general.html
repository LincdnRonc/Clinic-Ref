Uveal (choroidal) melanoma

For medium tumours, plaque brachytherapy gives the same survival as enucleation (COMS) and preserves the eye. Survival is driven by liver metastases, so follow up with liver imaging.

By size (COMS)

SizeDefinitionManagement
SmallApical height ≤3 mm, basal diameter 5–16 mmObserve for growth (21% at 2 y, 31% at 5 y); treat on growth
MediumApical height 2.5–10 mm, basal diameter <16 mmI-125 plaque = enucleation for survival (5 y OS about 81%)
LargeBasal diameter >16 mm or apical height >10 mmEnucleation (pre-op EBRT added nothing). 5 y OS about 60%
  • Plaque dose: 85 Gy to the tumour apex (or to 5 mm from the inner sclera if height <5 mm), with a 2 mm margin around the base. Dose rate 0.60–1.05 Gy/h over 4–7 days. Ru-106: 90 Gy.
  • Alternatives: protons 70 GyE (long-term LC about 95%) or SRS 25–40 Gy to the 50% isodose.
  • COMS medium: 5 y local failure about 10%; enucleation for complications or failure about 15%. Vision 20/40 or better fell from 70% at baseline to 34% at 3 y.
  • Distant metastasis: 5 y 14–20% after local therapy; mostly liver (about 90%). Median survival with metastases about 6 months.

Brain and orbit tolerances

Necrosis risk rises above 60 Gy and with fractions >2.5 Gy. Lens, lacrimal gland and cornea limits drive orbital planning.

Tolerances

StructureDose and risk
Brain necrosis (<2.5 Gy/fx)<3% below 60 Gy; about 5% at BED3 120 Gy (72/36); about 10% at BED3 150 Gy (90/45). BID RT is steep above BED3 80 Gy
Optic nerves and chiasm<3% at 55 Gy, <7% at 60 Gy, 7–20% above 60 Gy. SRS: very low <8 Gy; <10% at 12 Gy
Hippocampus (HA-WBRT)100% of hippocampus + 5 mm <9–10 Gy, Dmax <16 Gy (30 Gy/10)
LensCataract in about 33% after 2.5–6.5 Gy (8 y latency) and about 66% after 6.5–11.5 Gy
Lacrimal glandDry eye TD5/5 35 Gy, TD50/5 50 Gy. Dmax 30 Gy recommended
CorneaUlceration >60 Gy; keratitis absent <59 Gy, almost certain >70 Gy
Eyelashes and lidsLoss >20 Gy; chronic lid changes >50 Gy
  • TMZ: G3+ thrombocytopenia about 7%; check bloods every 2 weeks. Neutropenia also occurs.
