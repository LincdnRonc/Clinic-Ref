Oesophagus and GOJ contouring guide

Target volumes for oesophageal cancer (squamous cell and adenocarcinoma) treated with neoadjuvant or definitive chemoradiation, for GOJ tumours (Siewert I–II), for cervical and upper-third tumours including involved supraclavicular nodes, and for adjuvant RT after a positive margin. Siewert III and gastric cancer are in the stomach guide.

1.2 GTV

  • GTVp: the primary tumour, contoured on the contrast-enhanced planning CT. Include the whole circumference of the oesophageal wall at every involved level, including the wall beside nodes that lie away from the primary (SW ODN).
  • GTVn: each involved node (GTVn1, GTVn2…). Include disease seen on any modality (CT, EUS or PET), even if it shows on only one (SW ODN).
  • Use every source: endoscopy, EUS, barium, CT and PET (AGITG/TROG; Wu 2015). PET ideally within 6 weeks of starting RT (SW ODN).
  • For SCF nodes more than 30 mm from GTVp, contour them as a separate GTVn (SCOPE2 nodal protocol).
Getting the length right
  1. Write the endoscopic top and bottom (cm from incisors) and the EUS length on the planning note.
  2. Find the tumour on PET/CT and check that its length agrees; if not, use the longer extent.
  3. Look for wall thickening, luminal narrowing and fat stranding on CT; CT alone often misses the cranio-caudal ends.
  4. Fiducial clips placed at endoscopy mark the ends reliably; with markers, a Dutch pathology study suggested microscopic spread along the wall is usually within 1–1.5 cm (Machiels 2019) — not yet a reason to cut the margins below in routine practice.

Cranio-caudal GTV extent is where observers disagree most; adding PET reduced that variation in some cases (Nowee 2019, Dutch multicentre study). Tumour location by distance from incisors (cervical, upper, middle, lower thoracic) follows the AJCC definitions CHECK with the current AJCC edition.

This page follows
  • SCOPE2 / SW ODN 2023 for building the CTV and PTV in every intact scenario: involved field, 20 mm along the lumen, 1 cm radial, the lower-third rules and the published constraint set. It is the most recent complete, protocol-based method in the folder and matches the IFI evidence (Wang 2022).
  • Wu 2015 for anatomy: where the heart, liver and vertebra edits go, how far into the stomach, and the borders of the coeliac, gastrohepatic and SCF regions when they are treated.
  • Elective coeliac/gastrohepatic coverage (Wu) is an option for node-positive GOJ adenocarcinoma at neoadjuvant doses; decide case by case and document it CHECK departmental practice.
  • SCOPE2 nodal protocol for supraclavicular node-positive upper-third disease; ARS 2025 and the head-and-neck team for cervical SCC dose.

2.1 Why involved field?

  • Wang 2022 meta-analysis (23 studies, 4120 patients): IFI had better 5-year OS than ENI (pooled RR 0.78, 95% CI 0.68–0.90) but no difference at 1–3 years, and less grade ≥2 (RR 0.79) and grade ≥3 (RR 0.51) acute oesophagitis. No difference in grade ≥3 pneumonitis or late toxicity. Mostly Chinese, mostly squamous, many non-randomised studies — read it as "IFI is safe", not "ENI harms".
  • Wu 2015 itself notes that only periesophageal nodes are regional in AJCC 7, and that routinely adding all mediastinal stations gives much larger lung doses; for distal tumours, superior mediastinal stations need not be added beyond the 1 cm radial margin.

Section 3 · Scenario A

3Thoracic oesophagus: neoadjuvant and definitive core build

The same volumes are used for neoadjuvant and definitive treatment; only the dose differs (SW ODN). Build the CTV in three helper steps, as SCOPE2 does.

3.1 Step by step (SCOPE2 / SW ODN)

  1. CTV_A — draw along the lumen: GTVp + 20 mm superiorly and inferiorly, and GTVn + 10 mm superiorly and inferiorly. Do this by hand following the oesophagus, not with a geometric expansion.
  2. CTV_B — grow CTV_A by 1 cm circumferentially into the adjacent mediastinum, where microscopic disease may lie (this covers the periesophageal nodes, station 8; Wu 2015).
  3. CTV_C — CTV_A + 0.5 cm circumferentially. This is the floor for editing.
  4. Edit CTV_B off uninvolved bone, lung, heart and great vessels, but keep at least 5 mm from GTVp (use CTV_C as the guide). CTV_B is the final CTV.
  5. Lower third with 4D-CT: repeat on the reference, maximum-inhale and maximum-exhale phases and combine into the ITV; check every slice (Section 4).

3.3 Walkthrough, cranial to caudal (mid-thoracic tumour)

  1. Above the GTV: start 20 mm above GTVp (or 10 mm above the highest node). Contour the oesophagus plus 1 cm of periesophageal fat; trim lung.
  2. At the GTV: GTVp + 1 cm. Trim off the vertebral body, aorta and heart, keeping at least 5 mm around GTVp. The airway is not on the SW ODN edit list; keep the 1 cm margin there unless clearly uninvolved CHECK.
  3. At node levels (stations 7, 8): GTVn + 10 mm cranio-caudally and 1 cm radially; include the oesophageal wall at that level (SW ODN). Join node and primary volumes where they lie close together, rather than leaving a gap along the oesophagus.
  4. Below the GTV: continue 20 mm along the lumen, following its curve.
  5. Check that CTV_C (5 mm) sits inside the final CTV on every slice; smooth in all three planes.

3.4 Neoadjuvant points

  • CROSS used 41.4 Gy/23 with carboplatin–paclitaxel (van Hagen 2012). In ESOPEC, perioperative FLOT gave longer survival than CROSS for resectable adenocarcinoma (3-year OS 57.4% vs 50.7%, HR 0.70; Hoeppner 2025). Neoadjuvant chemoRT for adenocarcinoma is now mainly for patients who cannot have FLOT; it remains a standard for SCC.
  • Plan with the surgeon: stomach that will become the conduit and the planned anastomosis site should not get unnecessary dose CHECK local surgical preference.

4Distal oesophagus and GOJ (Siewert I–II)

Build the CTV as in Section 3, then apply the lower-third rules. Siewert III tumours, and tumours extending more than 5 cm into the stomach, are outside the scope of Wu 2015; contour them with gastric guidelines (stomach guide; Matzinger 2009).

4.3 Walkthrough, GOJ tumour

  1. Thoracic part as Section 3 (20 mm along the lumen above GTVp).
  2. Through the hiatus, follow the oesophagus into the cardia. Cover gastric mucosa below GTVp up to the SW ODN limit (≤20 mm; ≥2 cm per Wu at 50.4 Gy — in practice 20 mm).
  3. Contour involved subdiaphragmatic nodes separately (+5 mm). Extend the CTV down to 1 cm below the lowest node.
  4. If elective coverage is chosen (Section 2), add the gastrohepatic/paracardial fat and para-aortic tissue down to the coeliac origin, with the borders above.
  5. Contour the CTV on the reference, MaxIn and MaxEx phases; union = ITV. Expand each slice as needed so the CTV is covered through the whole cycle (SW ODN).

4.4 Pitfalls

  • Under-estimating motion: GOJ tumours often move more than 1 cm; use gating or compression above that (Wu 2015).
  • Variable gastric filling moves the perigastric nodes and changes the stomach dose; keep the same fasting-and-drink routine every day (Wu 2015; SW ODN).
  • Spleen dose: keep Dmean <10 Gy. If higher, treat as at risk of hyposplenism: pneumococcal, Hib and meningococcal vaccines at least 2 weeks before RT, prophylactic antibiotics from the start of RT and an emergency supply (SW ODN, after BCSH guidance) CHECK Australian equivalent.

Section 5 · Scenario C

5Cervical and upper-third oesophagus, supraclavicular nodes

Cervical SCC is uncommon (<5% of oesophageal cancers) and is treated much like head-and-neck SCC: larynx preservation with definitive chemoRT is preferred where possible (ARS 2025). Simulate in a head-and-neck shell, arms down (SW ODN), and plan with the head-and-neck team.

Upper thoracic: SCOPE2 / SW ODN involved field, adding the whole bilateral SCF only for SCF node-positive disease (SCOPE2 nodal protocol). Cervical SCC treated to head-and-neck doses: discuss elective neck levels (ARS 2025, Wu 2015) with the head-and-neck team and record the decision CHECK.

5.2 SCF node-positive disease (SCOPE2 nodal protocol)

  1. GTVn: whole involved SCF node (separate GTVn if >30 mm from GTVp).
  2. CTVn = GTVn + 5 mm in all directions.
  3. CTVscf = CTVn + the whole SCF region on both sides (lymph from the proximal third drains to both deep cervical chains).
  4. PTVscf = CTVscf + 5 mm (6 mm SI on 3 mm slices); join to the primary PTV for the 50 Gy/25 PTV.

5.3 Pitfalls

  • Arms-up set-up for an upper-third tumour pushes the shoulders into the beam path and makes the neck irreproducible; use arms down and a shell (SW ODN).
  • Cervical dose escalation brings laryngeal oedema and fatal oesophagitis (ARS 2025); keep the larynx and pharyngeal constrictors as OARs (contour as in head-and-neck practice).
  • Thoracic inlet: the CTV should step smoothly from the mediastinal to the SCF volume; extending the anterior border towards the clavicular heads above the arch helps (Wu 2015).

9.4 Technique and IGRT

  • IMRT/VMAT (or helical) for curative treatment, prescribed and reported per ICRU 83; conformal for 40/15 palliative; simple fields for low-dose palliation (cord ≤100% for 30/10) (SW ODN).
  • Daily CBCT with online correction, matched to the PTV unless the clinician specifies an OAR-priority match; daily kV if CBCT is unavailable (SW ODN). Departmental match region and tolerance CHECK.
  • For GOJ: check stomach filling and diaphragm position on each CBCT; if filling differs, apply the drink routine again rather than shifting.
  • Weekly on-treatment review, weekly bloods and weight for radical patients, early dietitian input (SW ODN).
