Departmental Contouring Protocol · Lower GI Radiation Oncology

Anal canal contouring protocol

Definitive chemoradiation with VMAT and a simultaneous integrated boost for T1N0 (including excised T1) and T2–4 or node-positive anal canal and perianal squamous cell carcinoma: primary and nodal GTV/CTV, every elective nodal region with border tables, the ischiorectal fossa decision, risk-adapted options (NOAC), doses, constraints and names.

GTVp / GTVn CTV54 / CTV50.4 (high dose) CTV45 / CTV42 (elective) PTV

SWSLHD (Liverpool & Macarthur) anal canal SCC contouring protocol (1 Sept 2020) · UK National Guidance for IMRT in Anal Cancer v4 (Dec 2016) · AGITG atlas (Ng 2012) · RTOG anorectal atlas (Myerson 2009) · ECOG-ACRIN EA2182 guideline (2022) · NOAC risk-adapted CTVe (Nilsson 2023) · Shakir 2020 patterns of relapse — Version 1.0 · Nov 2026

Section 1

1Scope, preparation, simulation and imaging

1.1 Before simulation (department)

  • Stage with examination/DRE, EUA or anoscopy ± endoscopic ultrasound, biopsy with p16, CT chest/abdomen/pelvis. Consider pelvic MRI and strongly consider FDG-PET (neither is Medicare-rebated for anal SCC). If no PET and groin nodes are equivocal, ultrasound and FNA.
  • Cervical screening if not up to date (do not delay CRT). Test for HIV if at risk; if positive, plan with infectious diseases.
  • Bowel/sphincter function: consider a defunctioning colostomy for severe obstruction, fistula or severe incontinence. UK guidance: stoma for tumours infiltrating the posterior vagina or with significant incontinence from sphincter invasion.
  • Chemotherapy: mitomycin-C with infusional 5-FU or capecitabine. Book category 1 (5 days a week, machine down days, no BD catch-up): anal cancer is technically category 2 but longer treatment time is harmful.

1.3 Contouring order

  1. Find the anal verge (marker or MRI) and the anorectal junction (levators meet the external sphincter; mesorectal fat disappears) on the sagittal.
  2. GTVp on T2 MRI and PET, transferred to CT with the examination and wires (GTVp MRI, GTVp PET → GTVp). GTVn on CT/MRI/PET.
  3. High-dose CTV (CTV50.4 or CTV54): GTVp + margin, then edit in the whole anal canal and sphincters; add CTVn.
  4. Elective CTV (CTV42 or CTV45): regions in section 5, joined, with the anterior 10 mm into organs.
  5. Small bowel loops individually (RO) to 1.5 cm above the PTV; other OARs by radiation therapists.
  6. Registrar contours prefixed REG_; replan structures RP_.

Anal canal borders (department, UK): superior at the anorectal junction (about 4 cm above the verge); inferior at the anal verge (marker or MRI); circumferentially the lumen and outer wall.

3.1 Walkthrough (sagittal first, then axial cranial to caudal)

  1. Sagittal: mark the verge and the anorectal junction; draw GTVp on MRI.
  2. Axial: GTVp + 10 mm; trim off levator/gluteal muscle and bone only (not off fat or the IRF).
  3. From the anorectal junction to the verge, add the whole canal and both sphincters on every slice; at the GTV level include the full lumen and wall.
  4. Elective: mesorectum from its top, presacral strip, internal iliac and obturator, IRF; join; extend 10 mm into bladder/vagina/prostate anteriorly.
  5. Carry CTV42 down to 10 mm below the GTV or the verge.

3.2 Options for selected T1N0 (not department routine)

  • UK v4: small good-prognosis tumours may have the primary plus a margin only.
  • AGITG: select T1N0 with major comorbidity may omit nodes above the caudal SIJ and the inguinal nodes (<5% failure).
  • NOAC: <1 cm perianal T1N0 not involving the canal — ENI probably omissible; awaiting ACT3.

3.3 Pitfalls

  • Drawing only the tumour plus margin and leaving out the rest of the canal; the department and UK both require the whole canal.
  • Trimming CTV off the ischiorectal fat: only muscle and bone are edited off the primary CTV.
  • After excision there is no gross disease; the scar and excision site define GTVp (department), so wire the scar.

4.2 Walkthrough

  1. GTVp and GTVn on MRI/PET → CT; check wires for skin disease.
  2. CTV54: GTVp + 15 mm (muscle and bone off), whole canal and sphincters added; GTVn + 5–10 mm.
  3. CTV45, cranial to caudal: common iliac bifurcation (or 20 mm above the highest GTV) → internal and external iliac + presacral → obturator → mesorectum (whole) → IRF → inguinal compartments from where the external iliac leaves the pelvis down to the bottom of the ischial tuberosities.
  4. Anterior edge 10 mm into bladder/uterus/vagina/seminal vesicles/prostate at the levels of those organs.
  5. Inferiorly to 15 mm below GTV or the verge; for perianal disease, the skin is in the CTV and bolus/self-bolus must bring dose to it.

4.3 Pitfalls

  • Perianal or anal margin tumours: cover the skin with a radial and caudal margin (AGITG asks for 20 mm) and check self-bolus; UK wants 5 mm of tissue around the GTV.
  • Inguinal nodes: include every visible node or lymphocoele even outside the compartment borders (department, AGITG, ECOG).
  • Nodes in the mesorectum or upper pelvis may need a higher cranial border (NOAC; section 6).
  • PTVs not involving skin are edited to 5 mm inside the body contour for optimisation (UK); ECOG names these PTV_xxxx-xx.
  • Tumour invading the posterior vagina: consider a stoma (UK); include 10 mm into the vagina anteriorly and use the vaginal dilator rules if one is used (ECOG).

4.4 Patterns of relapse with the UK approach (Shakir 2020)

385 consecutive patients in 5 UK centres treated with UK-guidance IMRT/SIB: 86.7% complete response within 6 months; 3-year DFS 75.6% and OS 85.6%. 83.4% of relapses were at the primary site and only 2 were isolated relapses in nodes not involved at the start, supporting the UK elective volumes and elective dose. Male sex, higher N stage and not completing RT predicted recurrence.

Section 8

8Pearls and common errors

Pearls and common errors

  • Anorectal junction: where levators fuse with the external sphincter and mesorectal fat tapers away; on CT a plane from the lower edge of the symphysis to the coccyx crosses the rectum here (AGITG Fig. 1).
  • Obturator region is short: only 3–5 mm cranio-caudally above the canal (AGITG).
  • External iliac to inguinal transition: between the acetabular roof and the superior pubic ramus, where the artery leaves the pelvis; RTOG places it at the caudal obturator vessels.
  • Don't remove bowel blindly from the nodal CTV: anal guidelines allow up to 10 mm into bowel or bladder because their daily position varies (UK; ECOG).
  • Circumflex iliac and superolateral groin nodes: include visible nodes along the circumflex iliac vessels when the ipsilateral groin is involved (NOAC).
  • Lymph node ECE: AGITG's 10 mm minimum nodal margin came from head and neck ECE data.
  • Most failures are local: 83% of relapses at the primary (Shakir 2020); spend effort on the GTV and the canal, and on finishing treatment on time.
  • Sacral hollows: AGITG includes them; UK and NOAC leave them out unless infiltrated.

11.1 ROI algebra

T1N0
  1. zGTVp+10 = GTVp + 10 mm − muscle − bone
  2. CTV_5040 = zGTVp+10 ∪ AnalCanal (manual: lumen, wall, sphincters)
  3. CTV_4200 = CTV_5040 ∪ Mesorectum ∪ IntIliac ∪ PreSacral ∪ Obt ∪ IRF, + anterior 10 mm into organs, − Bowel_Small − muscle − bone; check inferior limit (10 mm below GTV or verge)
  4. PTV_5040 = CTV_5040 + 10 mm; PTV_4200 = CTV_4200 + 10 mm
T2–4 / N+
  1. CTV_5400 = (GTVp + 15 mm − muscle − bone) ∪ AnalCanal ∪ (GTVn + 5–10 mm − muscle − bone − skin)
  2. CTV_4500 = CTV_5400 ∪ all seven regions, cranial limit = max(CIA bifurcation, highest GTV + 20 mm), inferior = min(GTV − 15 mm, verge)
  3. PTV_5400, PTV_4500 = CTV + 10 mm; optimise on PTV_4500 − (PTV_5400 + 3–5 mm) ring
  • Registration: MRI (T1, T2) and PET rigid to CT on pelvic bone; check the anal canal and verge marker.
  • Peer review the GTV and elective selection before planning; record the stage-based volume choice in the plan notes.
