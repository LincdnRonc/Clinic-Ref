Hippocampus in 6 rules (RTOG 0933 atlas)

  • Thin-slice 3D T1 post-gadolinium MRI (≤1.5 mm), fused.
  • T1-dark grey matter medial to the temporal horn; start at the horn's crescent-shaped floor.
  • Leave out the fimbria (T1-bright, above-medial) and parahippocampal gyrus (T1-bright, below-medial).
  • Uncal recess = anterior limit; grey matter in front is amygdala/uncus.
  • Medial: ambient cistern, then the lateral edge of the quadrigeminal cistern.
  • Stop when the tail leaves the atrium (crus of fornix in front, splenium behind). Check on sagittal.

CC001 limits, 30 Gy/10

  • PTV: D2% ≤37.5 Gy, D98% ≥25 Gy, V30 ≥95%.
  • Hippocampi: D100% ≤9 Gy (≤10), Dmax ≤16 Gy (≤17).
  • Optic nerves and chiasm: Dmax ≤30 Gy (≤37.5).
  • Not for: metastasis within 5 mm of a hippocampus, LMD, survival <4 months.

Helmet pitfalls

  • Do not trim the internal auditory meatus to spare the cochlea (SIOPE).
  • Foramen lacerum is not included; foramina stop at the outer table.
  • Lens stays out; the optic sheath ends at the back of the globe.
  • No hippocampal avoidance with LMD.

Section 2 · Scenario A

2Standard whole brain (VMAT)

Field-based WBRT used opposed laterals with the collimator rotated to block the anterior orbit, about 1 cm below the skull base anteriorly to make sure of the temporal lobes, the inferior border at C1–2 (also convenient for future matching), and 1 cm of flash above and behind the skull (handbook). With volumes, the target is simply the intracranial contents; the plan supplies the margin.

2.3 Walkthrough (cranial to caudal)

  1. Brain window W 1500–2000 / L 300–350 so the inner table edge is crisp. Start one slice above the vertex.
  2. Vertex to orbital roofs: follow the inner table; easy slices — interpolate every 2–3 slices, then check.
  3. Orbital roofs: switch to bone window W 3000 / L 400. Carry the contour down between the orbits onto the cribriform plate; this sits lower than the orbital roofs.
  4. Middle cranial fossae: the temporal poles lie anterior and inferior, behind the greater wings of the sphenoid. Include them to the floor of the fossa on every slice until they disappear.
  5. Sella: include the pituitary fossa; follow the clivus and petrous ridges.
  6. Posterior fossa: inner table around the cerebellum to the foramen magnum.
  7. Stop at the foramen magnum (or C2 if there are posterior fossa metastases).
  8. Review on sagittal and coronal for gaps at the cribriform plate and temporal poles.

2.4 Pitfalls

  • Accepting an auto-segmented "Brain" as the CTV: it is the parenchyma, so it misses CSF over the convexity, the pituitary fossa and often the tonsils. Start from it if you like, then expand to the inner table.
  • Under-covering the cribriform plate and temporal poles: the classic marginal-miss sites in CSI series (SIOPE) and the reason field-era WBRT kept 1 cm below the skull base.
  • Stopping above the foramen magnum, or running down to C2 by habit when there is no posterior fossa disease — the CC001 volume stops at the foramen magnum.
  • Scans that do not include the vertex or are acquired with the mask tilted.

Section 3 · continued

3HA-WBRT: planning criteria and pitfalls

The target of avoidance is the neural stem cell niche (subgranular zone of the dentate gyrus), so you contour the grey-matter hippocampus (dentate gyrus and cornu ammonis), not the whole limbic circuit. Keeping the avoidance region small limits the risk of disease in it (Gondi 2010).

3.1 Eligibility and evidence

  • CC001 (2020): 518 patients, WBRT 30 Gy/10 + memantine ± HA. Cognitive failure lower with HA (adjusted HR 0.74, 95% CI 0.58–0.95); less fatigue, less difficulty remembering and speaking. No difference in OS or intracranial PFS. Relapses in the HA region: 11 with HA vs 16 without.
  • Eligible: metastases outside a 5 mm margin around either hippocampus, KPS ≥70. Excluded: hydrocephalus or distorted ventricles, leptomeningeal metastases, prior WBRT.
  • Benefit emerged from 4 months; forgo HA if expected survival is <4 months (Brown 2020 discussion).
  • Risk in the avoidance region: in 371 patients with 1,133 metastases, 8.6% of patients (upper 95% CI 11.5%) and 3.0% of metastases were in the hippocampus + 5 mm; none were inside the hippocampus itself (Gondi 2010).

3.3 Hippocampus walkthrough (atlas slides 4–23)

  1. Start caudally at the lowest slice where the crescent-shaped floor of the temporal horn appears. Contour the T1-hypointense grey matter medial to the CSF of the temporal horn — not the bright white matter.
  2. Go cranially along the medial edge of the temporal horn. Leave out the fimbria (T1-bright, superomedial) and the parahippocampal gyrus (T1-bright, inferomedial).
  3. Anterior limit: the anterior edge of the temporal horn, then the uncal recess when it appears — grey matter in front of it is amygdala and uncus; leave it out.
  4. Medial limit: the edge of the T1-hypointensity up to the ambient cistern; the medial edge of the uncal recess; then, higher, the lateral edge of the quadrigeminal cistern.
  5. The contour moves supero-posteriorly slice by slice (the "banana"). Where the temporal horn is no longer seen, keep using the quadrigeminal cistern medially.
  6. The tail passes behind the thalamus, antero-medial to the atrium of the lateral ventricle, curving toward the splenium.
  7. Stop when the hypointense structure no longer borders the atrium: the crus of the fornix appears in front and the splenium of the corpus callosum behind (Gondi 2010: at the lateral edges of the quadrigeminal cistern, before the crus of the fornix).
  8. Check the shape on sagittal (head inferior-anterior, body, tail superior-posterior; amygdala separate in front) and coronal (parahippocampal gyrus separate below).
  9. Expand 5 mm in 3D → Hippocampi_PRV05. Subtract from CTV_3000 → PTV_3000.

3.6 Pitfalls

  • Including the amygdala (anterior to the uncal recess) or the fimbria: enlarges the avoidance region and the risk of under-dosing disease. Check on sagittal.
  • Stopping too low: the tail climbs well above the level of the temporal horn, beside the atrium. Follow it until it leaves the atrium.
  • Contouring on CT or on a thick-slice MRI: the atlas needs ≤1.5 mm 3D T1.
  • Adding a PTV margin and then subtracting the HA region: CC001 adds none; if your department adds one, build PTV = (CTV + margin) − HA region and say so in the plan CHECK.
  • Coverage between the hippocampi: coplanar arcs can only reach the midline bridge of PTV between the hippocampi through a narrow gantry window (about 340–20°), so blocking the hippocampi works only with non-coplanar arcs (Liu 2026 Fig. 5).
  • A new metastasis inside the HA region on the planning MRI: HA is no longer appropriate; replan as standard WBRT or unilateral sparing.

Section 4 · continued

4Helmet: walkthrough, skull base map and pitfalls

The "German helmet" field is a slightly larger whole-brain field: the posterior half of the orbits is included to cover all meningeal surfaces, the inferior border is the C2–3 interspace, and a 5° posterior gantry tilt avoids divergence into the lenses (handbook). For VMAT the same intent becomes a CTV of the whole intracranial subarachnoid space and its extensions, borrowing the cranial part of the SIOPE CSI CTV and the EANO–ESMO target for cranial neuropathies.

4.4 Walkthrough (follows the SIOPE atlas order)

  1. Build CTV_Brain (section 2) on brain window.
  2. Bone window W 3000 / L 400, 1 mm slices. Start just above the superior orbital fissures; outline each fissure as it opens between the greater and lesser sphenoid wings.
  3. Cribriform plate: confirm it is inside the CTV on every slice between the orbits (bounded laterally by the lateral lamella).
  4. Optic canals (bone window), then the optic nerves (W 350 / L 40) forward through the orbit to the back of the globe. Keep the lens out.
  5. Pituitary fossa: whole sella included.
  6. Internal auditory meati (petrous bone, antero-superior to the jugular foramen): fill the canal to its lateral end. Do not trim toward the cochlea.
  7. Foramen rotundum (greater wing, supero-lateral to the vidian canal), then foramen ovale (postero-lateral to rotundum). Leave out the foramen lacerum.
  8. Jugular foramina (supero-lateral to the foramen magnum, below the carotid canal), then hypoglossal canals (in the occipital condyles, infero-medial to the jugular foramen).
  9. Below the foramen magnum switch to the thecal sac: the CSF space inside the canal with the root sleeves at C1–2, to the inferior endplate of C2. Use T2 MRI if fused.
  10. Union all parts → CTV_Helmet. Review in 3D: the foramina should look like short spurs off the skull-base CTV (SIOPE Fig. 3D).

4.5 Pitfalls

  • Using the whole-brain CTV for LMD: highly conformal plans can under-dose CSF in the skull-base foramina if those extensions are not contoured (Noble 2017, cited by SIOPE).
  • Sparing the cochlea by cutting the internal auditory meatus: SIOPE advises against it; the CSF sleeve lies right beside the cochlea.
  • Including the lens while chasing the posterior globe: the nerve sheath ends at the back of the globe; the field era tilted the gantry for this reason.
  • Hippocampal avoidance with LMD: excluded in CC001; CSF disease bathes the temporal horns.
  • Stopping at the foramen magnum: the helmet volume runs down the upper cervical thecal sac to C2.
  • Forgetting the junction: if spinal RT is likely later, note the PTV's inferior edge (a C1–2 or C2–3 border was traditionally chosen with future matching in mind; handbook).
  • For fit patients with controlled systemic disease, consider whether proton CSI (randomised phase II benefit in CNS-PFS and OS; EANO–ESMO) is the better option before committing to a helmet.

Section 5

5Pearls and common errors

5.1 All three volumes

  • Inner table, not brain: set the window first (W 1500–2000 / L 300–350); a soft-tissue window makes the inner table look further in than it is.
  • The two classic misses are the cribriform plate (low, between the orbits) and the temporal poles (low, in the middle fossae). Check both on sagittal and coronal before signing.
  • Name the inferior extent in the prescription note (foramen magnum, C2) so the planner and checker know it is intended.

5.2 HA-WBRT

  • Grey matter, not white: the hippocampus is T1-dark; the fimbria and parahippocampal gyrus are T1-bright.
  • The uncal recess is the anterior landmark; anything in front of it is amygdala.
  • The tail is above the temporal horn and beside the atrium; most contours that are too short miss it.
  • Review the hippocampal contour against the current MRI: a metastasis within 5 mm changes the plan.

5.3 Helmet

  • Use 1 mm bone-window CT for the foramina; the SIOPE atlas order (SOF → cribriform → optic canal → sella → IAM → rotundum → jugular → ovale → hypoglossal) works top-down.
  • The foramina are short spurs: they stop at the outer table.
  • C2 means the bottom of C2 for the CTV.

6.1 IGRT

  • HA-WBRT has no PTV margin around the transition zone, so daily volumetric or kV imaging matched to the skull is advised CHECK departmental IGRT protocol.
  • Standard and helmet: departmental mask protocol CHECK. For the helmet, check neck flexion at C1–2 on the match, since the cervical CTV moves with the chin.

6.2 Arc geometry

  • HA-WBRT, 4 arcs (Liu 2026): isocentre midway between the hippocampi. Two coplanar full arcs (181→179° clockwise for the upper brain, 179→181° counter-clockwise for the lower brain, 2 cm overlap) plus two half arcs at couch 90° (gantry 0–179°, collimator 15°).
  • Handbook alternative: non-coplanar VMAT 182–178°, 178–182° and a half arc 2–178° at couch 90°.
  • Standard and helmet: two coplanar arcs usually suffice; add a non-coplanar arc if the lens or cochlea doses are high CHECK local class solution.

7.3 Prescribing and reporting

  • HA-WBRT: normalise so PTV_3000 V30Gy ≥95%; report D2%, D98%, hippocampal D100% and D0.03cc (CC001; Liu 2026).
  • Gondi 2010 normalised the linac plan to 92% of the PTV at 30 Gy; CC001 later set ≥95% — use CC001.
  • Standard and helmet: ICRU 83 style — D98%, D50%, D2% and coverage of PTV; lens D0.03cc.

8.2 Optimisation notes

  • Liu 2026 blocked the hippocampi for entry and exit only on the non-coplanar arcs; on coplanar-only plans blocking left the PTV between the hippocampi under-dosed.
  • Gondi 2010 starting objectives (linac IMRT): PTV min 32 / max 34 Gy; hippocampus max 11 Gy, 9 Gy to ≤40%; eyes max 7 Gy; lenses max 5 Gy. Useful as a first pass; final evaluation against CC001.
  • Gondi 2010 showed coverage and homogeneity improve if you judge the PTV 2 mm further out from the HA region (hippocampus + 7 mm): expect the dose fall-off to sit in the first few mm outside the HA region.
  • Name the plan with the volume type (WB, HA-WB, Helmet) and the inferior extent so checkers can verify the build.

8.3 QA before approval

  1. Fusion checked on skull base and temporal bones (HA, helmet).
  2. Hippocampi reviewed on sagittal and coronal; volume plausible (about 3 cc together).
  3. CTV includes cribriform plate, temporal poles, sella; ends at foramen magnum (or C2).
  4. Helmet: all six foramina pairs and the optic sheaths are present; thecal sac reaches the C2 endplate; lens outside.
  5. DVH against section 7; hot spots outside the PTV checked.
