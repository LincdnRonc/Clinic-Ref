Early-stage NSCLC: SABR

SABR is standard for medically inoperable T1–2 N0 NSCLC. For operable patients, lobectomy remains standard outside trials. At BED10 ≥100 Gy, local control is over 90% at 5 y. Choose the regimen by location.

Decisions

  • Inoperable: generally FEV1 ≤40% or DLCO ≤50% predicted, or FEV1 <1 L for lobectomy (<2 L for pneumonectomy). A thoracic surgeon should see every SABR candidate (ASCO/ASTRO).
  • Operable: lobectomy with mediastinal node dissection. SABR only on trial (VALOR, STABLE-MATES). In STARS, 7 y OS was 69% after surgery and 81% after SABR.
  • Without a biopsy: reasonable if the probability of malignancy is >85% (for example, by Herder or Brock model). For 17–85%, biopsy if PET-positive. Below 17%, observe. Document the multidisciplinary discussion.
  • Tumours >5 cm: SABR remains appropriate with an acceptable therapeutic ratio.
  • Interstitial lung disease: high risk of fatal pneumonitis. ASPIRE-ILD reported 1 y OS 79% and G5 toxicity 8%. Consent carefully and consider de-escalating (50/10, 45/15).
  • Systemic therapy: I-SABR (SABR + 4 doses of nivolumab) showed 6 y OS 56 → 70% (HR 0.55) in a 141-patient phase II trial. This is not yet standard.
  • Numbers to quote (RTOG 0236, at 5 y): local recurrence 7%, involved-lobe recurrence 20%, distant metastasis about 30%, OS 40%. These are the "10-20-30-40" figures.

  • HyTEC doses to the PTV periphery for maximum control: T1 42/3, 46/4, 48/5; T2 45/3, 50/4, 53/5 (each ± 1 Gy).
  • For squamous cell carcinoma, BED10 >150 Gy (54/3) was associated with better local control in one retrospective series (3 y LC 70 → 97%).
  • Ultracentral HILUS (56/8, tumours ≤1 cm from mainstem) had G5 toxicity of 10% overall and 18% in the main bronchus arm, mostly from fatal haemoptysis (median 15 months after treatment). Limit the PBT to about 90–95% of the prescription, not 105%.

SABR planning and toxicity

Use 4DCT with ITV and a 5 mm PTV. Prescribe so the PTV gets 100% of dose to 95% of the volume. Keep the dose fall-off tight. Chest wall, rib and plexus limits matter most for peripheral tumours.

Coverage

  • PTV = ITV + 5 mm (consider 10 mm craniocaudal). Use breath-hold or gating if motion is >7–10 mm.
  • PTV D95 ≥100%. RTOG 0915 also used D99 ≥90%. Dmax 125–150% (ESTRO).
  • ICRU 91: minimum PTV D98 BED10 100 Gy and GTV/ITV mean BED10 150 Gy.
  • High-dose spill: volume outside the PTV getting 105% should be <15% of the PTV. Report R50 and D2cm.
  • Ultracentral (MDACC): ITV V100 >99%, PTV V95 >99%, PTV Dmax <120%. PBT Dmax about 90–95% of prescription. BED3 to PBT below 180–220 Gy.
