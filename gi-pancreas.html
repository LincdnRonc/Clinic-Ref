Pancreas: overview and resectability

Systemic therapy drives survival. RT's role is local control, margin clearance in borderline disease, and pain. Only about half of "resectable" cases end up R0.

Resectability (NCCN)

CategoryArterialVenous
ResectableFat plane around coeliac axis, SMA and CHANo SMV/PV contact, or ≤180° without vein irregularity
BorderlineSMA ≤180° (head) or CA ≤180° (body/tail); CA >180° with uninvolved GDA (modified Appleby); CHA contact reconstructableSMV/PV >180°, distortion or occlusion but reconstructable; IVC contact
UnresectableSMA or CA >180° (unless Appleby); aortic invasionUnreconstructable SMV/PV; contact with first jejunal branch

Distant metastases, including non-regional nodes, = unresectable.

Work-up

  • Pancreas-protocol multiphase CT (arterial 25–35 s, portal venous 65–70 s) or MRI. EUS ± FNA. CA 19-9 after biliary decompression.
  • Staging laparoscopy is institutional; higher yield in body/tail tumours (more occult mets).
  • Germline testing for all (BRCA, PALB2: platinum and PARP inhibitor relevance); MMR/MSI (pembrolizumab if dMMR).
  • Place biliary stents before simulation where possible.
  • Imaging underestimates size by ~0.5 cm; occult extrapancreatic extension in 60–90% of "resectable" tumours.

RT dosing, SBRT and planning

Conventional 45–54 Gy, 15-fraction hypofractionation, or SBRT. Aim for BED10 >60 Gy (e.g. 40/5) to gross disease when ablating.

Planning

  • NPO 2 h; neutral oral contrast (water) and the same volume daily. Triple-phase contrast CT. Fiducials if not on MR-linac.
  • Motion: ~2 cm SI, 6–8 mm AP. 4DCT minimum; DIBH, gating or compression. Compression can push bowel into the field.
  • Conventional PTV 1.5–2 cm AP/lat, 2–3 cm CC (non-gated); SBRT PTV ~3 mm, goal PTV V40 >95% where OARs allow.
  • Nodes: head — proximal 1–1.5 cm CA, proximal 2.5 cm SMA, porta hepatis, suprapancreatic, pancreaticoduodenal, para-aortic T11–L3. Tail — coeliac, SMA, splenic, lateral suprapancreatic.
  • Elective volume (NRG atlas 2024): cover perineural/perivascular spread — CA from origin to trifurcation (to CHA for body tumours abutting CA), SMA from aorta to the lowest pancreatic head, ≥1 cm anatomically edited margin on GTV. >90% of failures lie within 1 cm of CA, SMA or retroperitoneum.
  • Postop (RTOG 0848): PV, PJ, CA (1–1.5 cm), SMA (2.5–3 cm), aorta + bed; expand 1–1.5 cm on vessels, aorta 2.5–3 cm right/1 cm left/2–2.5 cm anterior/0.2 cm posterior; PTV 0.5–1 cm. Pancreaticogastrostomy not included.

Landmark trials

Design and population, arms, then the result.

Adjuvant

CONKO-001Phase III · n=354 resected (80% R0) · observation vs gemcitabine ×6MS 20.2 → 22.8 mo; 5-y OS 10 → 21%; DFS 6.7 → 13.4 mo.
ESPAC-4Phase III · resected (60% R1) · gemcitabine ± capecitabineGem-cap better OS. Over half develop local failure after upfront resection.
PRODIGE 24Phase III · n=493 R0/R1 (75% N+, ~50% R1), CA 19-9 <180 · gemcitabine vs mFOLFIRINOXDFS 13 → 21 mo; 3-y DFS 21 → 40%; 5-y OS 43% with mFOLFIRINOX. New adjuvant standard for fit patients.
RTOG 0848Phase II/III · R0/R1 → 6 mo chemo ± chemoRT (capecitabine)No OS gain overall (3-y OS 39%, MS 30 mo); possible benefit in pN0 (hypothesis only).

Neoadjuvant and locally advanced

PREOPANC-1Phase III · resectable/borderline · upfront surgery vs 36/15 + gemcitabine (neoadjuvant + adjuvant gem)R0 43 → 72%, node positivity 82 → 35%; improved DFS and long-term OS. Control arm gemcitabine is no longer standard.
PREOPANC-2Phase III · n=375 resectable/borderline · mFOLFIRINOX ×8 vs gem-based chemoRT → surgeryNo OS difference (MS ~21.5 mo, 3-y OS ~34%); resection ~76% both.
Alliance A021501Phase II · borderline · mFOLFIRINOX ×8 vs ×7 + SBRT 33–40/5 (or 25/5)SBRT arm closed for low R0; chemo arm beat historical OS. Criticised: no nodal coverage, low dose, no laparoscopy.
LAP07Phase III · n=449 locally advanced · after 4 mo gem ± erlotinib: 2 more chemo vs chemoRT 54 Gy + capecitabineNo OS gain (MS ~16 mo); local progression 46 → 32%; longer time off treatment (3.7 → 6.1 mo).
SMARTProspective · n=136 borderline (43%)/LA (57%) after ≥3 mo chemo · MR-guided adaptive SBRT 50/5G3+ GI 0–2.2%; 2-y OS from diagnosis 54%; 35% went to surgery.
ESPAC5Phase II · borderline · surgery vs neoadjuvant GEMCAP, FOLFIRINOX or 50.4 Gy + capecitabineShort-course neoadjuvant chemotherapy outperformed immediate surgery (small numbers, short follow-up).
RASolute 302Phase III · previously treated metastatic, ECOG 0–1 · chemo vs daraxonrasibRoughly doubled OS and PFS.
