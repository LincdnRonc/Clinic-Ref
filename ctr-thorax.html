Locally advanced NSCLC (ESTRO-ACROP, Nestle 2018)

Involved-field, not elective nodal, RT. The GTV comes from PET-CT (within about 3 weeks) read with the contrast CT and EBUS / mediastinoscopy results; motion is handled with 4D-CT.

Full-depth version: Lung contouring protocol (SABR, stage III, PORT, station borders, constraints).

Volumes

VolumeDefinition
GTVpPrimary on lung window (−600 / 1600 is a common setting), checked against PET. Include spiculation; exclude atelectasis when PET or MRI separates it.
GTVnNodes that are PET-positive, enlarged (short axis ≥ 1 cm), or positive at EBUS / mediastinoscopy. Mediastinal window.
CTVpGTVp + about 5 mm (ESTRO range 5–8 mm), edited off bone, heart and great vessels unless invaded.
CTVnGTVn + margin; ESTRO allows a margin of about 5 mm or the whole involved nodal station.
ITVFrom the 4D-CT (all phases or MIP checked on each phase), or mid-ventilation approach.
PTVInstitutional, based on IGRT (typically 5 mm with daily CBCT).

ENI is not recommended. Pre-chemotherapy extent is used for the CTV after induction chemotherapy, edited for normal tissue that has re-expanded.

Post-operative RT (Lung ART / ESTRO)

  • CTV: bronchial stump, ipsilateral hilum, involved nodal stations, and the stations that link them to the stump, usually with station 7 and the ipsilateral 4.
  • Read the pathology station by station (IASLC map). Spoelstra 2010 tabulates the stations by lobe.

Lung ART CTV stations and upper/lower limits by involved station are tabulated in the lung contouring protocol, section 5.

Early-stage SBRT and SCLC

For SBRT the GTV is the CTV; the margin goes into ITV and PTV. For SCLC the newest guideline is ESTRO-EORTC 2026.

SBRT (ESTRO-ACROP, Guckenberger 2017)

  • GTV on lung window across all 4D phases (ITV) or mid-ventilation; no CTV expansion in most protocols.
  • PTV about 5 mm with daily CBCT. Check the proximal bronchial tree (2 cm around the main bronchi, Kong 2011) to decide central vs peripheral dose.

Limited-stage SCLC (Levy 2026)

  • Primary: post-chemo volume when chemo comes first; nodes: the pre-chemo involved stations.
  • ENI not recommended; PET staging assumed.

Read Levy 2026 for nodal rules after induction chemo before relying on this summary.

Thoracic OARs (Kong 2011; Hall 2008)

Contour on the planning CT with consistent windows; the OAR definitions matter because the constraints were derived from them.

Definitions

OARHow to contour
LungsBoth lungs minus GTV (or ITV) on lung window; exclude trachea and main bronchi.
Proximal bronchial treeDistal 2 cm of the trachea, carina, main, upper-lobe, intermediate, middle-lobe, lingular and lower-lobe bronchi.
OesophagusOuter wall from the cricoid to the GEJ, mediastinal window.
HeartPericardial sac, from the pulmonary trunk / great vessels down to the apex (consider substructures).
Spinal cordBony canal as surrogate (or the cord itself on MRI for SBRT), extended beyond the PTV.
Brachial plexusIpsilateral, for apical tumours: from the C4–5 to T1–2 neural foramina, between the anterior and middle scalene, then along the subclavian neurovascular bundle over the first rib.
Chest wall / ribsFor SBRT: ribs or a 2 cm chest-wall shell around the lung.
