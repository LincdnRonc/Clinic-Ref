Medulloblastoma

Maximal safe resection, then craniospinal irradiation (CSI) and a boost, then chemotherapy. Use the involved-field (tumour bed) boost, not the whole posterior fossa. For children <3 y, delay CSI with chemotherapy where possible.

  • Boost: tumour bed + 1–1.5 cm is as good as whole posterior fossa (ACNS 0331: local recurrence about 3%, distant about 10%).

Ependymoma

Ependymoma is a surgical disease: aim for gross total resection, with second-look surgery if needed. Posterior fossa tumours all get conformal RT, even after GTR. There is no CSI unless the tumour is metastatic.

Decisions

  • Infratentorial: PORT for all, including after GTR.
  • Supratentorial: PORT after STR; after GTR only if grade 3.
  • Dose: 54 Gy + 1 cm (newer protocols 0.5 cm). Boost to 59.4 Gy to residual and bed above the cord, limiting brainstem to 57.8 Gy. 59.4 Gy has been shown to be safe (Merchant).
  • Age 12–18 months: stop at 54 Gy, and omit RT after GTR (ACNS 0121). Chemo and second-look surgery are standard for infants <18 months.
  • 1q gain is a poor prognostic marker. Myxopapillary (spinal) grade 1: the role of RT is controversial.

Germ cell tumours, craniopharyngioma and paediatric glioma

Germinoma is very sensitive to chemo and RT, so doses are low. Craniopharyngioma: limited surgery plus RT gives the best control with less morbidity. DIPG: 54 Gy as soon as possible.

Intracranial germ cell tumours

  • Germinoma, chemo then CR: whole-ventricular RT 18 Gy + boost 12 Gy (total 30 Gy).
  • Germinoma, PR or limited residual: WVRT 24 Gy + boost to 36 Gy.
  • Germinoma, RT alone: WVRT 23.4–25 Gy + boost to 45–50.4 Gy. Disseminated: CSI (21–24 Gy, or 30 Gy RT alone) + boost to discrete lesions.
  • Non-germinomatous GCT: chemo × 4–6 → CSI 30–36 Gy + boost 14.4–18 Gy (to 54 Gy).

Craniopharyngioma

  • GTR alone: 70–85% DFS, at the cost of diabetes insipidus, vision loss and hypothalamic injury. STR alone: 30% DFS. STR + RT: 85–90% DFS.
  • Dose: 54 Gy conventional (50–55 Gy acceptable).
  • Image during RT: 20–30% have cyst growth within weeks, which may need drainage or replanning.

Paediatric glioma

  • Low-grade glioma: resection where possible. If unresectable and <10 y, use chemo first (carboplatin/vincristine delays RT by about 3 y; dabrafenib/trametinib for BRAF V600E). Older than 7 y: RT 50.4–54 Gy. 10 y LC about 70%.
  • DIPG: steroids and 54 Gy as soon as possible (median OS 9–11 months). Hypofractionation 39/13 is reasonable (median OS 9.6 vs 8.7 months for 54/30). H3 K27M in about 85%.
  • Re-irradiation at progression can give palliation (for example 25/10 at 16 months).
