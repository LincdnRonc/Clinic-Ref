PSA doubling time

Roach formulas

LN = ⅔ × PSA + (GS − 6) × 10; SV = PSA + (GS − 6) × 10; ECE = 1.5 × PSA + (GS − 3) × 10. Derived in the pre-PSMA era and known to overestimate nodal risk in contemporary cohorts. Verify

D'Amico risk group

Low: PSA ≤10, GS ≤6 and T1c–T2a. High: PSA >20, GS 8–10 or ≥T2c. Intermediate: the rest. Verify

Breast

Late recurrence, DCIS and local recurrence estimates, and adjuvant benefit tools.

CTS5 (late distant recurrence after 5 years of endocrine therapy)

For post-menopausal ER-positive disease, recurrence-free at 5 years. Formula: 0.438 × nodes + 0.988 × (0.093 × size − 0.001 × size² + 0.375 × grade + 0.017 × age), nodes grouped 0, 1, 2–3, 4–9, >9 and size capped at 30 mm (Dowsett 2018). Entered from memory Verify: cross-check with the official CTS5 calculator.

USC / Van Nuys Prognostic Index (DCIS)

Treatment thresholds by score are not built in: read them from Silverstein's publications. Verify

Liver and GI

Liver function scores for HCC and liver SBRT. BCLC staging and liver dose limits: hepatobiliary page and liver SBRT guide.

Child-Pugh score

Verify

ALBI grade and MELD

ALBI = 0.66 × log₁₀(bilirubin µmol/L) − 0.085 × albumin g/L (Johnson 2015). MELD = 3.78 × ln(bilirubin mg/dL) + 11.2 × ln(INR) + 9.57 × ln(creatinine mg/dL) + 6.43; MELD-Na and MELD 3.0 are not calculated. Verify

mrTRG (rectal MRI tumour regression grade)

GradeMRI appearance
1Complete radiological response: no tumour signal, or a thin scar only
2Good response: dense fibrosis, no obvious residual tumour
3Moderate response: more than 50% fibrosis or mucin, with visible intermediate signal
4Slight response: mostly tumour, little fibrosis
5No response: same appearance as baseline

Verify against the MERCURY group definitions.

Gynaecological

Brachytherapy dose summation and post-operative risk criteria. Targets and EMBRACE II aims: gynae contouring guide.

EBRT + brachytherapy EQD2 (cervix)

Linear-quadratic sum, α/β 10 for target and 3 for OARs; assumes the same dose each brachytherapy fraction (enter the mean). The departmental or GEC-ESTRO spreadsheet remains the record. Verify

Sedlis criteria (cervix, after radical hysterectomy)

LVSI + deep third (any size); LVSI + middle third ≥2 cm; LVSI + superficial third ≥5 cm; no LVSI + middle or deep third ≥4 cm (GOG 92). Verify

Endometrial molecular classification (ProMisE / TCGA order)

  1. POLE pathogenic mutation → POLEmut
  2. Otherwise, mismatch repair protein loss → MMRd
  3. Otherwise, abnormal p53 → p53abn
  4. Otherwise → NSMP

Test in this order; "multiple classifiers" are assigned by the first positive step. Verify
