Staging

Tis

Nx

Mx

DDx for breast lump

Benign: mastitis, galactocele, fibroma, lipoma, fat necrosis, seroma, abscess, cyst, phyllodes tumour

Malignant: DCIS, IDC, ILC, IBC, secretory carcinoma, sarcoma, lymphoma, skin cancer/melanoma

Path:

  • Graded based on nuclear pleomorphism, nuclear size, mitoses, necrosis, growth pattern

Growth patterns (C2PMS):

  • Comedo
  • Cribriform
  • Solid
  • Papillary
  • Micropapillary

Adverse features:

  • Comedo pattern
  • Necrosis

Paget’s disease:

  • Intraepidermal adenoCa with eczema of nipple due to epidermotropic spread.
  • Underlying DCIS more common than underlying invasive breast carcinoma

Phyllodes tumour

  • Biphasic epithelial and stromal tumour, leaf-like architecture.
  • Benign, borderline, malignant
  • Stromal features determine malignancy – cellularity, atypia, mitoses, stromal overgrowth, margins
  • Borders depend on nature
  • Malignant has preferential haematogenous spread over lymphatic
  • Axilla surgery not routine unless clinically suspicious nodes

LCIS

  • Classic: single discohesive invasive cell within lobules
  • Pleomorphic: pre-invasive cells have more atypical larger appearance with necrosis or calcifications
  • Florid: expansion of acini with pre-invasive cells, mass forming
  • Pleomorphic and florid should be completely excised with 2mm margin + adj RT
  • Increased risk for bilateral breast ca, 1-2% annual cancer risk

Screening:

  • Screening MMG/US 2nd yearly from 50-74yo

Management

  1. WLE +/- SLNBx if suspicious for foci of IDC
  2. Obtain 2mm margins or re-excise
  3. Whole breast adjuvant radiotherapy
  4. Reduce LRR by 1/2 (EBCTCG MA, sweDCIS, MSKCC calculator)
  5. 1% chance per year of recurrence up to 20yrs, half will be invasive (ECOG, EBCTCG MA)
  6. 40Gy/15# (UK START B)
  7. 26Gy/5# - 1% higher late tissue effect, apply more caution to large breasts due to increase separation and heterogeneity and age <50yo (FAST-FORWARD)
  8. Boost for non-low risk DCIS: age<40 OR age>40 with: size >15mm, radial margin <10mm, IG or HG, comedo pattern, central necrosis, multifocal (TROG BIG)
  9. DCISionRT genetic score – retrospectively validated in sweDCIS
  10. Cardiac risk minimal (Danish cohort trials)
  11. Partial breast radiotherapy
  12. <20mm, unifocal, low-intermediate grade, margins >2mm, no BRCA, visible seroma, age >50
  13. Conditional factors (should not have multiple): high grade, HER2 +ve in EBC, close margin <2mm, size 20-30mm
  14. 40Gy/15# PBI or 40Gy/36Gy/15# reduced dose WBRT - IMPORT LOW
  15. APBI 30Gy/5# - Florence trial
  16. Multicatheter HDR brachytherapy (GEC-ESTRO ph 3 RCT)
  17. Single channel HDR brachytherapy (Mammosite trial)
  18. Brachytherapy on trial (ABPI – HEARTBEAT)
  19. Intra-op 20Gy/1# - TARGIT

4. Endocrine therapy

  • not routine in AU however, tamoxifen shown to reduce IBTR, esp if not giving RT
  • Baby tamoxifen: 5mg for 3 years reduces recurrence risk by 50% at 10 yrs with lower toxicity than full dose tamoxifen (TAM-01)

P: T1-3N0-1 invasive breast cancer after BCS or mastectomy

IMPORT LOW

P: Unifocal, ≤3cm, pN0, margins >2mm after BCS

I: WBI vs PBI vs reduced dose RT (40Gy tumour bed, 36Gy whole breast)

O: 5 yr IBTR non-inferior for both, late toxicity less for PBI

GEC-ESTRO ph III

-Mutlicatheter HDR brachytherapy had no diff in 10 yr OS or IBTR vs WBRT EBRT

Mammosite trial

Single arm/centre prospective study with single channel HDR PBI showed low 5 yr IBTR <5% with good cosmesis

TARGIT

Intra-op 20Gy/1# - 5 yr IBTR was inferior vs WBRT, 20% needed EBRT after pathology review

FAST FORWARD

P: T1-3N0-1 invasive breast cancer after BCS or mastectomy (20%) OR DCIS

I: 40Gy/15# vs 26Gy/5# vs 27Gy/5#

O: 10 yr:

26Gy IBTR non-inferior and numerically lower (2% vs 3.6%). Toxicities similar between the two, 1% late soft tissue effect increase

5 yr: 26Gy had 1.5% increase in fibrosis at 5 yrs, esp in women <50yo

27Gy inferior and higher toxicity

Nodal substudy for 26Gy/5# to breast & axilla suggests safe at 5 years

Pathology

IDC:

  • Nottingham grade 1-3 based on TNM: tubule formation (more is better), nuclear pleomorphism, mitoses.
  • Score:
  • 3-5 = G1
  • 6-7 = G2
  • 8-9 = G3

ILC:

  • loss of E-cadherin, multifocal, diffuse spread

Other:

  • Mucinous carcinoma: less aggressive, older women
  • Papillary carcinoma: less aggressive, older women
  • Tubular carcinoma: less aggressive,
  • Medullary carcinoma: TNBC high grade appearing cells but biological behaviour is indolent
  • Secretory carcinoma: TNBC but indolent
  • Apocrine carcinoma: TNBC but indolent [AR +ve]
  • Micropapillary: unfavourable
  • Metaplastic carcinoma: unfavourable, biphasic (mesenchymal, epithelial)
  • M4STAP

Biological subtypes:

Luminal A:

  • ER/PR +, HER2-, Ki67<14% , G1-2
  • 50% of breast cancer
  • Less aggressive
  • Older post-menopausal women
  • Less response to chemotherapy
  • Late relapse, good prognosis

Luminal B:

  • ER/PR +, HER2 +/-, Ki67 ≥14%, G1-3
  • 20% of breast cancer
  • More aggressive than luminal A
  • Younger women
  • Responds to chemotherapy
  • Intermediate prognosis

HER2 enriched:

  • 15% of breast cancer
  • ER/PR-, HER2+, G3
  • Any age, most younger
  • Aggressive
  • Responds well to anti-HER2 therapy
  • Intermediate prognosis

Basal type

  • ER/PR/HER2-, G3, expresses basal markers EGFR, CK5/6, CK14/17
  • 15% of breast cancer
  • Young women, BRCA 1
  • Most aggressive
  • Responds to chemotherapy and immunotherapy
  • Poor prognosis

Management

1. Surgery

  • WLE + adj RT = mastectomy (Milan, NSABP B-06)
  • Most N0 require SLNBx
  • Omission of SLNB if favourable EBC in pts >65 is an emerging option (SOUND, INSEMNA, ASTRO guidelines)
  • Reconstruction immediate or post RT (RT charm, FABREC)
  • If cN0 but SLN+ → ALND can be omitted if ≤2 nodes

2. Adjuvant chemotherapy / HER2

  • Fit, young pts with: luminal B, node positive, HER2 enriched
  • Doxorubicin/cyclophosphamide 3 weekly x4 + paclitaxel weekly x12
  • Docetaxel/cyclophosphamide 3 weekly x4
  • dual HER2 therapy: pertuzumab, trastuzumab

3. Adjuvant radiotherapy

  • 40Gy/15# or 26Gy/5#. WBI or PBI.
  • 48Gy SIB boost if: age <50, SM close, LVSI, grade 3 (EORTC, Import High)
  • RNI: axilla/SCF/IMC if 1-2 SLN+ instead of axillary dissection
  • High tangents if N1mi
    • Z11, SENOMAC, AMAROS show that in cT1-2 SLN 1-2 cN0 pts there is equiv long term OS with RT vs ALND. Axilla recurrence <3%. LE 12 vs 25%. 30% chance of further node +ve with ALND
  • RNI + ALND if <10 nodes dissected or >50% nodes +ve on dissection
    • RNI improves BCM/DFS in addition to ALND (MA 20, EBCTCG MA)
    • Concern for increased toxicity with IMC however with more modern cardiac sparing techniques this is improving

4. Omission of RT:

  • T1N0, luminal A, age ≥70yo
  • Increases risk of LRR by 9% but not survival
  • LRR higher than above if ER+ low <50% (19% vs 0%), or ET non-adherence (PRIME II)
  • HrQoL worse with ET vs RT (EUROPA, final results pending)

5. Adjuvant endocrine therapy for 5 years

  • Tamoxifen if pre-menopausal
  • Aromatase inhibitor if post-menopausal

6. Bisphosphonates

  • Small OS benefit in post-menopausal women
  • Reduces skeletal events

Level 2: posterior to pec minor. Between axillary vessels crossing medial and lateral edge of pec minor

Level 3: superior and medial to pec minor. Superior – pec minor inserts to coracoid. Inferior- axillary vessels cross medial pec minor. Posterior and medial – chest wall and thoracic inlet

Late Toxicity pathogenesis

Central pathway:

  • stem cell depletion, fibroblast activation, vascular injury
  • Radiation → DNA damage + ROS → endothelial dysfunction → chronic inflammation (TGF-B) → fibroblast activation → fibrosis + microvascular loss → hypoxia → perpetuates fibrosis
  • Other cytokines: TNF-a, IL-1, IL-6, PDGF

Cardiac:

  • Dose volume relationship – aim MHD <2Gy and LAD dose Dmean <10Gy, Dmax <15Gy
  • Endothelial injury → pro-thrombotic state → release TGF-B + microvascular damage and ischemia → accelerated atherosclerosis → CAD
  • TGF-B → activation of fibroblasts to myofibroblasts → collagen deposition and ECM remodelling → constrictive pericarditis, constrictive cardiomyopathy, diastolic dysfunction, valvular disease, arrythmia
  • Depletion of myocytes further reduces regenerative ability
  • Synergy between RT and traditional CV RFs

Skin

  • Consequential late effects from basal keratinocyte depletion, failed repopulation, barrier dysfunction +/- infection → chronic ulcer Chronic inflammation → TGF-B → activation of fibroblasts → collagen deposition, fibrosis and induration
  • Fibrosis and endothelial cell damage → hypoxia → compensatory superficial telangiectasia
  • Melanocyte stimulation or apoptosis → pigmentation change

Pathology

Inflammatory breast cancer:

Clinicopathological dx, T4d.

  • Rapid onset of sx <6mo with erythema of >1/3rd of breast, dermal oedema (peau d’orange) and induration of skin
  • Bx proven invasive breast carcinoma
  • optional: tumour emboli in dermal lymphatics

Mgmt:

  • All have NA systemic Rx
  • Surgery needs to be radical mastectomy + ALND for all
  • Adj RT is PMRT with CW + RNI regardless of disease burden
  • NA RT can be used to downstage if pt remains unresectable
  • 2 phase RT: 50Gy/25# +/- 10Gy/5# if boost to whole CW if PR or close margin. 5mm bolus.
  • CTV should cover all extent of initial disease
  • For supraclavicular or infraclavicular nodes that will not be resected, consider a boost
  • Avoid immediate reconstruction
  • Risk of early distant failure, prognosis depends on subtype, 3-8 yrs
  • 5 yr 60-70%

Fungating breast mass:

  • Bleeding: silver nitrate, topical TXA soaked gauze, Hb and PRBC
  • Odour: topical metronidazole
  • Dressings, POabx as needed
  • 8Gy/1#, 20Gy/5#, 30Gy/10#

Endocrine therapy

  • Tamoxifen = vasomotor symptoms, hot flushes, night sweats, VTE, endometrial hyperplasia and uterine cancer risk. Bone protective
  • AI = OP, joint and muscle aches, vaginal dryness
  • SERD = selective estrogen receptor degrader (fulvestrant) = flushes, fatigue, IM injection pain, nausea
  • Ovarian suppression = abrupt menopause, OP
  • Oopherectomy
  • CDK4/6i = neutropaenia, diarrhea, nausea, fatigue

Management

1. Neoadjuvant systemic therapy

  • chemotherapy: AC-T, TC
    • Doxorubicin, cyclophosphamide, paclitaxel
    • Docetaxel, cyclophosphamide
  • pembrolizumab in TNBC – KEYNOTE 522 – improved pCR by 10% and 5% OS benefit
    • carbo + taxol + pembro → AC + pembro → adj pembro
  • dual anti-HER2 treatment (TCHP)
    • docetaxol, carboplatin, trastuzumab, pertuzumab → adj HP for 12 mo or switch to TDM1 if no pCR
  • Clip positive node and primary tumour prior to NACT to ensure accurate resection

2. WLE or mastectomy

  • SLNB or axillary LND or targeted axillary node dissection

3. Adjuvant radiotherapy

  • whole breast radiotherapy +/- boost
  • regional nodal irradiation (IMC, SCF, axilla 1-3)
    • Include dissected axilla if <10 nodes, >50% positive, ENE
  • post-mastectomy irradiation indications:
  • Tumour size ≥ 50mm
  • N1 disease with high risk features (LVSI, G3, multifocal/mulicentric, ER-ve)
  • N2 or N3
  • IBC
  • SM+
  • Omission of PMRT CW and RNI if ypT0N0 and initially cN1
  • De-escalation of adjuvant nodal RT based on response (RAPCHEM, prospective registry):
    • ypN0 = whole breast alone if no mastectomy
    • ypN1 or 1-2 SLNB +ve = breast/CW +/- level 1-2 axilla if no ALND
    • ypN2 on ALND or multiple macromet on SLNB = breast/CW + RNI

4. Adjuvant systemic therapy if no pCR

  • Capecitabine after NACT – CREATE-X – improved OS by 8%. Not w/ concurrent RT
  • TDM-1 – KATHERINE - improved invasive DFS (IDFS)

5. Adjuvant systemic therapy regardless of pCR

  • Pembrolizumab – KEYNOTE 522, improves OS by 5%
    • Can be given concurrent with RT
  • Olaparib - OlympiA – improved IDFS and OS in high risk gBRCA. Not w/ concurrent RT
  • Abemaciclib + ET – monarchE – IDFS in high risk pts (pN2, G3 and any N+, high Ki67, T3+). Not concurrent w/ RT

Frail, unfit:

  • ER +ve consider use of neoadjuvant ET + CDK4/6i
  • HER2 +ve: NA anti-her2 therapy +/- ET
  • TNBC or unfit for any systemic: NA RT to downstage for definitive surgery

Re-irradiation:

  • RTOG 1014: 45Gy/30# BD, 90% of patients avoided mastectomy
  • Toxicity: fat necrosis, non healing ulcer, chest wall necrosis, rib fracture, cumulative cardiac and lung toxicity

P: prospective registry trial, cT1-2N1

Oligometastatic

Management · NRG-BR002

Clinic page: Nodes and PMRT

Management

1. Systemic therapy

  • ER+ve/HER2- : ET, CDK4/6 first line unless visceral crisis, rapid response required in which case chemo (paclitaxel) can be added
  • HER2+ve: chemo (paclitaxel)+ dual anti-HER2
  • TNBC: chemo (paclitaxel) + IO if PDL1 CPS >10

2. SABR to mets if ≤4 DM

  • No evidence of PFS benefit, thus not indicated

3. Oligoprogressive disease whilst on systemic therapy

  • No evidence of PFS benefit with MDT aside from Oligoma trial

4. Other:

  • Bone modifying agents: zoledronic acid given if bone mets to reduce SRE, no OS benefit
  • Repeat biopsy in oligorecurrence due to chance of biological transformation (acquired HER2, triple negative)

Pregnancy

Radiation effects on foetus · Management

Clinic page: Breast planning and consent

Radiation effects on foetus

  • 0-2 week pre-implantation: all or nothing, doses of 0.1Gy can be lethal
  • 3-8 weeks organogenesis: doses >0.1Gy can cause major organ dysfunction, cells are undifferentiated and radiosesnsitive. >0.5Gy more concerning for major dysfunction
  • 9-15 weeks: lower risk of organ dysfunction as cells are differentiated and more radioresistant. CNS is sensitive. >0.3Gy can cause growth retardation and microcephaly.
    • 1Gy has 40% change of intellectual disability
  • 16-term: CNS risk lower, and major malformation less likely, more likely growth retardation or pregnancy loss at 0.5Gy or higher
  • All doses increase life risk of carcinogenesis, increasing risk with increasing dose
  • dose-dependent deterministic injury and stochastic cancer risk

Estimating dose to fetus:

  • Depends on: distance from field, patient size, shielding, prescribed dose, technique
  • Medical physicist input, anthropomorphic phantom with TLD/ion chamber

Management

  • MDT, involve obstetrician
  • CT, MMG and PET not safe
  • MMG with shielding is allowed
  • BCS and mastectomy safe in all trimesters
    • No blue dye with SLNBx → risk of maternal anaphylaxis
    • Tc-99m used for SLNB instead, very low fetal radiation dose
    • Preference for mastectomy to avoid RT
  • Chemotherapy: AC safe in 2nd and 3rd trimester. Taxane has less data but likely safe
    • Stop chemo prior to delivery to avoid cytopenia
  • Immunotherapy, tamoxifen, and HER-2: not safe in pregnancy
  • RT: not safe in pregnancy
  • Aim for term delivery where possible
  • Avoid breastfeeding with chemotherapy, ET and targeted treatment
  • If RT given: Counsel on effects to fetus, termination of pregnancy not justified at fetal doses <0.1Gy, >0.5Gy is high risk and requires specialist counselling

Management

  • Tends to present later and retro-areolar mass
  • ER +ve: Tamoxifen 5 years is std; extend to 10 years if high-risk/tolerated. If tamoxifen contraindicated: AI + GnRH analogue for testicular suppression
  • CDK4/6i and chemotherapy as per usual considerations
