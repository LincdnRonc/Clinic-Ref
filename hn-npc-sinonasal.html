Nasopharyngeal carcinoma

IMRT 70/33–35. Stage I: RT alone. Stage III–IVA: induction (gemcitabine/cisplatin or TPF), then cisplatin chemoRT. Use MRI for all target volumes.

2024 international NPC dose-level volumes: Head and neck contouring guide, section 6.

By stage (ASCO/CSCO)

  • Stage I: RT alone 70/35 (10 y LC and DSS >90%).
  • T2 N0: chemo not routine; consider if bulky or EBV DNA high. Low-risk stage II: RT alone was non-inferior to chemoRT with fewer G3–4 AEs (46 → 17%) in one Chinese trial.
  • T2 N1: consider concurrent cisplatin.
  • T3 N0: chemoRT; induction or adjuvant may be considered.
  • Stage III–IVA: induction GC × 3 or TPF, then cisplatin 100 q3w or 40 weekly to a cumulative dose ≥200 mg/m². Without induction, give adjuvant PF × 3.
  • De novo M1: chemo, then locoregional RT for responders (SYSUCC5010: 2 y OS 55 → 76%).

Volumes (35 or 33 fractions)

  • Doses: 70/63/56 in 35, or 69.96/59.4/54 in 33.
  • CTVp_70 = GTVp + 5 mm (down to 1 mm near critical OARs). CTVn_70 = GTVn + 5 mm (1 cm if ENE).
  • CTVp_59.4 = GTVp + 1 cm + whole nasopharynx, at least 5 mm anterior to the choanae.
  • Elective nodes: retropharyngeal and levels II–V. Level Ib may be omitted unless anterior nasal cavity involved, bulky level II, ENE or bilateral disease. Consider omitting the lower neck on an uninvolved side.
  • GTVs include the pre-induction extent, especially in bone.
  • PTV ≥5 mm. PRV ≥2 mm around OARs.
  • Temporal lobe: Dmax <65 Gy (T1–2) or <70 Gy (T3–4) to the PRV; 72 Gy is an upper limit. Above Dmax 72 Gy or D1cc 62 Gy, necrosis risk rises.

Key trials

Intergroup 0099 (1998)147 · stage III–IV · RT ± cisplatin then adjuvant PF3 y OS 47 → 78%. Stopped early for benefit.
Zhang (GC induction) (2019; 5 y 2022)stage III–IVB · ± gemcitabine/cisplatin × 3 → chemoRT3 y RFS 77 → 85%. 5 y OS 79 → 88%. 97% completed induction.
Sun (TPF induction) (2016)± TPF → chemoRT3 y FFS 72 → 80%, OS 82 → 89%. More neutropenia (7 → 42%).
SYSUCC5010 (2020)de novo M1 · chemo response → ± locoregional RT2 y OS 55 → 76% (HR 0.42).

Nasal cavity and paranasal sinuses

Surgery and PORT is preferred. Nodal risk depends on site and histology. Treat the ipsilateral neck unless the tumour crosses midline.

Decisions

  • Surgery followed by PORT appears better than either alone for nasal cavity cancer. RT alone may suit T1 N0 (5 y OS >70%).
  • Maxillary sinus PORT: positive margins, PNI, LVSI or adenoid cystic histology.
  • Neck: cover RP and Ib–IV if node-positive. Elective coverage of the N0 neck is controversial; consider for G3, SqCC or undifferentiated histology. Adenoid cystic: neck coverage not needed.
  • Olfactory neuroblastoma: Kadish A → surgery (preferred) or RT 70 Gy. Kadish B/C → surgery then PORT.
  • PORT is usually SIB in 30 fractions.

Nasopharynx

From the exam summary.

Epidemiology

I: Very rare in western world, more common in Asia but still <1%

A: 50

G: M

R: Asian

RFs

E: nitrosamines (salted fish, cured meats), formaldehyde, pollution, coal dust/fumes, wood dust, smoking, ETOH, low fruit and veg

I: EBV

G: FHx with 1st degree relative

I: HIV, solid organ transplant

Staging

T1: confined to nasopharynx. Nasal cavity and oropharynx ok

T2: into parapharyngeal space

T3: into skull, VB, or nasal sinus

T4: into adj cranial structures: orbit, CN, parotid, hypopharynx

N1: unilateral cervical <6cm or bilateral VIIa nodes

N2: bilateral cervical

N3: >6cm or level IV

M1: DM

>60% have nodal involvement

Paraneoplastic

  • Dermatomyositis

EBV pathogenesis

  • EBV latent infection → EBNA/LMP-mediated epigenetic silencing of tumour suppressor genes, p53 pathway →
  • genomic instability, immune evasion, apoptosis resistance, chronic inflammation →
  • malignant transformation
  • High pre-treatment EBV DNA = worse prognosis
  • clearance/fall after treatment = better response
  • persistent detectable EBV DNA predicts relapse/distant failure

Subsites

  • Fossa of rosenmuller – most common site, on posterolateral wall
  • Eustachian opening (surrounded by torus tubaris), on lateral wall
  • Pharyngeal wall

WHO subtypes

Type 1: 25%

  • Keratinising SCC
  • Sporadic
  • Smoking, ETOH
  • Low EBV association
  • Poor treatment response and LC

Type 2: 10%

  • Non-keratinising SCC
  • More in sporadic cases than endemic
  • Moderate EBV association
  • Good treatment response
  • Locally aggressive

Type 3: 65% (95% in Asia)

  • Undifferentiated carcinoma, Lymphoepithelial carcinoma
  • Endemic type
  • Strong EBV assoc
  • Very locally aggressive
  • Good treatment response and LC
  • Distant recurrence

IHC: AE1/AE3, EBV LMP

EBER ISH

Other

Basaloid SCC, papillary adenoCa (rare, young age, indolent)

Relations

  • Superior: BOS/sphenoid bone, sphenoid sinus, clinoid, pituitary, cribriform plate, crista gali, ethmoid sinus
  • Inferior: soft palate
  • Anterior: nasal cavity, vomer, maxillary sinus
  • Lateral: pharyngeal wall, sphenoid bone, PPF, BoS foramen, cavernous sinus, temporal lobe
  • Posterior: prevertebral fascia of C1/C2

Cavernous sinus

  • Extends superiorly from the SOF to the petrous temporal bone

F/u

  • perform CN exams routinely

BOS Foramina (FROLIS, CN V: Front Row Offer)

  • SOF: CN III, IV, V1, VI
  • Rotundum: V2
  • Ovale: V3
  • Lacerum: vidian nerve forms here and passes through pterygoid canal
  • IAM: trigeminal, vestibulocochlear
  • Spinosum: medial meningeal artery
  • Jugular: glossopharyngeal (IX), Vagus (X), accessory (XI)
  • Hypoglossal canal: hypoglossal (XII)

Management

T1-2N0: Rare

  • RT 70Gy/56Gy/35# alone – low level evidence as very rare - Xiao et al
  • 3 yr OS >90%, LC >80%
  • Avoid surgery as this does not address lymphatics

T1-2 N1

  • Definitive RT with cisplatin (Chen et al)

T3N0+, OR N2+ (stage III)

  • Induction cis/5FU followed by RT with cisplatin (Intergroup 0099, Zhang et al). NB we use cis/gem 3 weekly x3c
  • Early data showed benefit for adjuvant chemo after CRT though it was poorly tolerated, however later data does not support this
  • Addition of IO (Sintilimab) to induction and definitive CRT improves EFS by 10% (CONTINUUM, ph III RCT in China)
  • Induction toripalimab with gem/cis then omission of concurrent cisplatin has non inferior for PFS with less toxicity (DIAMOND)

Recurrence

  • Salvage nasopharyngectomy, re-irradiation
  • Better 3 yr OS with salvage resection rather than post-op RT ~ 60Gy/30# - 85% vs 70% (Liu et al)
  • Alternative fractionation: 65Gy/54# BD at 1.2Gy per # – improved OS and reduced tox vs conv frac (You et al)
  • Brachytherapy

Metastatic

  • Carbo/taxol or cis/5FU
  • Definitive RT if no progression and at least partial response (You et al)
  • Addition of Torapalimab to gem/cis improves OS in metastatic or recurrent disease (JUPITER)

Pterygopalatine Fossa

  • Communicates with the nasal cavity via sphenopalatine fissure medially, maxilla via the pterygomaxillary fissure laterally, orbit via infraorbital fissure superiorly, and foramen lacerum via the pterygoid canal posteriorly
  • Foramen rotundum opens into the PPF on its posterior wall
  • Contains V2, vidian nerve, maxillary artery
