Early-stage Hodgkin lymphoma

ISRT after short chemotherapy. Favourable: ABVD × 2 → 20 Gy. Unfavourable: ABVD × 4 (or 2+2) → 30 Gy. Omitting RT after a negative interim PET slightly reduces PFS without changing OS; discuss the trade-off with the patient.

  • Risk factors (GHSG): large mediastinal mass (>⅓ thoracic width), extranodal disease, ESR >50 (or >30 with B symptoms), ≥3 nodal areas. EORTC adds age ≥50 and uses ≥4 areas.
  • Do not use 20 Gy after ABVD for unfavourable disease (HD11: 10 y PFS 76% vs 84% with 30 Gy).
  • NLPHL: ISRT alone 30 Gy for stage IA.

Advanced-stage Hodgkin lymphoma

Systemic therapy is curative (BEACOPPesc, BV-AVD, or nivolumab-AVD). RT is reserved for residual PET-positive masses ≥2.5 cm, and sometimes for initially bulky sites.

Role of RT

  • After BEACOPPesc: 30 Gy only to PET-positive residual masses ≥2.5 cm (HD15). PET-negative patients had 4 y PFS 93% without RT.
  • After PET-negative chemo: no consolidation needed (EORTC 20884: no EFS benefit in CR).
  • Residual PET-avid masses: 36–45 Gy (ILROG).
  • SWOG 1826: nivolumab-AVD improved on BV-AVD and is a new standard.
  • Relapsed/refractory: ISRT before or after transplant for limited or bulky relapse.

ISRT principles and late effects

Treat the pre-chemo involved sites (ILROG), not whole fields. Keep heart, breast and lung doses low; cardiac and second-cancer risks dominate late mortality.

ISRT volumes (ILROG)

  • GTV: pre-chemo involved nodes on PET/CT fused to the planning CT, and any residual disease.
  • CTV: pre-chemo extent, edited off normal tissues that were displaced (lung, muscle, bone). Do not include initially normal nodes. Add margin craniocaudally for uncertainty when image fusion is poor.
  • Use 4DCT/ITV or DIBH for mediastinal disease; DIBH helps most with upper mediastinal involvement and allows smaller PTV margins.
  • PTV margin per institution.
