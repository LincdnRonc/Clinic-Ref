Involved-site RT (ILROG: Specht 2014, Illidge 2014)

The CTV is the originally involved lymph nodes, not the whole nodal region. The key step is getting the pre-chemotherapy PET-CT fused to the planning CT, ideally in the treatment position.

Volumes

VolumeDefinition
Pre-chemo GTVAll initially involved nodes on PET-CT before chemotherapy.
Post-chemo GTVResidual mass (for boost or RT-alone settings).
CTVThe pre-chemo GTV volume, edited for normal tissue that was displaced but not invaded (lung, bowel, muscle, bone) now that the mass has shrunk. Separate nodes more than 5 cm apart can be separate CTVs. With poorer image registration, extend the CTV along the lymphatic chain (about 1.5 cm cranio-caudally).
ITVMediastinum and upper abdomen: 4D-CT or DIBH.
PTVInstitutional.

Indolent NHL or RT alone

When RT is the only treatment (early follicular, marginal zone), use larger CTVs: include the involved nodes with a wider margin along the lymphatic chain, as the involved-field era did, because there is no chemotherapy to sterilise nearby occult disease.

Extranodal lymphoma (ILROG, Yahalom 2015)

For most organs the CTV is the whole organ, because lymphoma infiltrates diffusely.

CTV by organ

SiteCTV
Stomach (MALT, DLBCL)Whole stomach from GEJ to duodenal bulb, plus involved perigastric nodes. Simulate fasting (about 6 h) with 4D-CT; ITV is large.
Orbit / ocular adnexaWhole orbit for orbital or lacrimal disease; conjunctival disease: the conjunctiva to the fornices (lens shielding possible for superficial disease).
Thyroid, salivary glandWhole gland ± involved nodes.
Waldeyer's ringWhole Waldeyer's ring ± involved nodes.
BoneInvolved bone with MRI-defined extent and margin; whole bone not always required.
Skin (cutaneous lymphoma)Visible lesion + margin (often 1–2 cm) with bolus.
Primary CNS lymphomaWhole brain (with posterior globe when the eye is involved) for WBRT; see Heme other page.
