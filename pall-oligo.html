Oligometastatic disease: SABR

For controlled primaries with up to 5 metastases, SABR doubled long-term survival in SABR-COMET, with a 5% risk of treatment-related death. Benefit depends on histology and how the disease is behaving.

Decisions

  • Selection: controlled primary, 1–5 metastases, good PS, and lesions that can be treated safely.
  • Oligoprogression: SABR to progressing sites while continuing systemic therapy (CURB: benefit in NSCLC but not breast).
  • Dose (OligoCare cohort): median 5 fractions of about 10 Gy. Median BED10 was lowest for prostate (64 Gy) and highest for CRC (126 Gy). Use site-specific SABR doses (see Thorax, GI and Spine SBRT pages).
  • Not every trial was positive: NRG-LU002 (NSCLC, SABR + maintenance) did not improve PFS.

Key trials

SABR-COMETPhase II · 1–5 metastases · SOC ± SABRMedian OS 28 → 50 months; 8 y OS 14 → 27%. Median PFS 6 → 12 months. G2+ toxicity 9 → 29%; treatment-related death 5% (3 patients).
CURBOligoprogressive NSCLC or breast · SOC ± SBRT to all progressing sitesNSCLC: median PFS 9 → 44 weeks. No benefit in breast cancer.
NRG-LU002NSCLC after first-line therapy · maintenance ± local therapy to ≤3 metastasesNo benefit: 2 y PFS about 36 vs 40%, 2 y OS about 58 vs 54%.
COREBreast, prostate, NSCLC · SOC ± SABRMedian PFS about 20 → 25 months (HR 0.79, not significant).

General palliation

Choose the shortest effective course for the expected survival. Common regimens for chest, pelvis, haemostasis and the coeliac plexus.
