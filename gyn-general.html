General gynae: IMRT, constraints and toxicity

Principles shared across gynaecological sites.

Nodal volumes by site

  • Endometrial: common, external and internal iliac, obturator (presacral if cervical stromal involvement).
  • Cervix: as above plus presacral.
  • Para-aortic: if common iliac or para-aortic nodes involved (isolated para-aortic involvement 1–3%).
  • Inguinal: if lower third of vagina or vulva involved.
  • Gross nodes: boost sequentially or as SIB (55–57.5/25 in EMBRACE II); parametrial boosts usually unnecessary if interstitial BT is used.
  • Simulate with full and empty bladder for IMRT; marker at the vaginal apex for cuff cases.

Constraints

  • Bone marrow V40 <37%, V10 <90% (RTOG 0418).
  • Bowel V40 <30% (TIME-C); EMBRACE: bowel D2cc <60 Gy, rectum D2cc <65–70 Gy total.
  • Duodenum (para-aortic fields): V55 <1 cc or V50 <4 cc.
  • Femoral heads/necks: ~50 Gy tolerance.

IMRT evidence

  • TIME-C (RTOG 1203): postoperative IMRT reduced patient-reported acute diarrhoea and GI toxicity; patients report far more toxicity than clinicians.
  • PARCER: IMRT halved late G2+ bowel toxicity after hysterectomy for cervical cancer (36 → 19%; G3+ 9 → 2%).
  • RTOG 0418: IMRT lowers haematological toxicity in the postoperative setting.

Toxicity management

  • High-dose probiotics (L. plantarum) during pelvic RT reduced days with loose stools (ProRad).
  • Chronic proctitis: optimise stool; vitamins A/E/C (90 d) or metronidazole 400 mg TDS (30 d); sucralfate enema 2 g BD; APC for persistent bleeding.
  • Vaginal dilators: start 4–6 wk after treatment, a few minutes 2–3× weekly, ≥9–12 mo; regular intercourse lowers G2 stenosis (23% vs 36%, EMBRACE I).
  • Insufficiency fractures, lymphoedema, ovarian failure and sexual dysfunction: discuss at consent.

Recurrence, re-irradiation and ovary

Isolated pelvic and para-aortic recurrences are often curable.

Recurrence and re-irradiation

  • About half of patients with isolated pelvic/para-aortic recurrence are alive at 3 y.
  • Curative re-irradiation SBRT 40–45/5: 3-y LC 61%, distant-metastasis-free 44%, OS 52%; G3+ 14%.
  • ABS re-irradiation review: LC 44–88% at 1–5 y, OS 40–82% at 2–5 y.
  • Vaginal recurrence after surgery alone: 45 Gy + BT to EQD2 ≥75 Gy; image-guided adaptive BT helps.

Ovarian and fallopian tube

  • Surgery and platinum chemo dominate (carbo/paclitaxel ± bevacizumab; PARP inhibitor maintenance especially with HRD/BRCA: olaparib, niraparib).
  • Complete secondary cytoreduction improves PFS in platinum-sensitive relapse; incomplete resection gives no benefit.
  • RT roles: palliation of bleeding, pain or nodal masses; selected oligometastatic disease (SABR-ROC trial asking about OS).
  • Germline and somatic testing for all epithelial ovarian cancers.
