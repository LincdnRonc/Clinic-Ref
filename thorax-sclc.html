Limited-stage SCLC

Start chest RT with cycle 1 or 2 of cisplatin/etoposide. Use 45/30 BID or 60–70 Gy daily, then durvalumab (ADRIATIC). For T1–2 N0, SABR or surgery followed by chemotherapy is an option.

Decisions

  • Chemo: cisplatin 75 d1 + etoposide 100 d1–3 q3w × 4 (or 60/120 as in Turrisi).
  • Chest RT dose: 45/30 BID (Turrisi, CONVERT) or 66/33 daily. 70/35 (CALGB 30610) is equivalent. Only about 25% of surveyed radiation oncologists favour BID.
  • Timing: start RT with cycle 1–2, or cycle 2 if bulky, treating post-chemo volumes but including initially involved nodal stations.
  • No elective nodal RT for PET-staged patients (CONVERT, C30610; isolated nodal failure about 2–3%). Consider covering the ipsilateral hilum.
  • Consolidation: durvalumab after chemoRT (ADRIATIC: median OS 37 → 56 months). Atezolizumab added concurrently did not help (NRG-LU005).
  • T1–2 N0: mediastinal staging (PET may suffice), then lobectomy or SABR, followed by adjuvant chemotherapy.
  • Thoracic RT benefit: about 5% OS and about one-third fewer local failures.

Brain: PCI or MRI surveillance

  • LS-SCLC with response: offer PCI 25/10 (Aupérin: about 5% OS benefit, pre-MRI era). MRI surveillance every 3 months is a reasonable alternative (ARS lists it second).
  • Start about 4 weeks after chemotherapy. Repeat brain MRI first.
  • Cognitive decline is clear after 30/10 but not clearly after 25/10. Hippocampal avoidance and memantine remain investigational (NRG-CC003; PREMER positive, Dutch trial negative).
  • ES-SCLC: MRI surveillance rather than PCI for most (Takahashi). PCI reduced brain metastases but median OS was 13.7 vs 11.6 months (p=0.09, favouring observation).

Key trials

Extensive-stage SCLC

Chemo-immunotherapy is standard. Chest RT is for palliation or for selected responders with residual thoracic disease. Median OS is about 12 months; 5 y OS is <5%.

Decisions

  • Systemic: platinum/etoposide × 4 with durvalumab (CASPIAN) or atezolizumab (IMpower133, carboplatin only), then maintenance immunotherapy.
  • Consolidative chest RT: consider 30/10 for responders with residual chest disease (CREST). Its role with immunotherapy is being tested (RAPTOR / NRG-LU007).
  • Brain: MRI surveillance; treat symptomatic brain metastases.
  • Palliation: RT for symptomatic sites. Low-volume asymptomatic brain metastases can start with chemotherapy.

Key trials

IMpower133 (2018)carboplatin/etoposide ± atezolizumabMedian OS 10.3 → 12.3 months. 1 y OS 38 → 52%.
CASPIAN (2019)platinum/etoposide ± durvalumabMedian OS 10.5 → 13 months. 2 y OS about 14 → 22%.
CREST (2015)498 · response to chemo · PCI ± thoracic RT 30/101 y OS 28 → 33% (primary endpoint not met, p=0.066), 2 y OS 3 → 13%. Intrathoracic progression 46 → 20%.
Jeremic (1999)210 · complete distant response · ± thoracic RTMedian OS 11 → 17 months. 5 y OS 4 → 9%.
Takahashi (Japan) (2017)ES-SCLC · MRI-staged · PCI 25/10 vs MRI surveillanceBrain metastases at 12 months 59% (observation) → 33% (PCI). Median OS 11.6 months with PCI vs 13.7 without.

SCLC planning

Planning is like NSCLC: 4DCT, IMRT, daily CBCT and adaptive replanning for rapid shrinkage. Do not refuse curative treatment just because V20 is 35–40%.

Volumes and limits

  • Treat the post-chemo primary plus all initially involved nodal stations.
  • Lung V20 <35% (CONVERT, CREST). In CALGB 70 Gy trials, V20 exceeded 40% in 30 of 100 patients, but only 3 had G3 toxicity.
  • Spinal cord: 36 Gy max for 45/30 BID (Turrisi era). 41 Gy in RTOG protocols. CONVERT allowed 42 Gy (BID) and 48 Gy (daily).
  • Cardiac dose independently predicted OS in RTOG 0617; a heart-dose analysis of C30610 is planned. Use IMRT and spare the heart.
