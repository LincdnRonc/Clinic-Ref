Lung (TNM 9th edition)

Limited-stage SCLC

Start chest RT with cycle 1 or 2 of cisplatin/etoposide. Use 45/30 BID or 60–70 Gy daily, then durvalumab (ADRIATIC). For T1–2 N0, SABR or surgery followed by chemotherapy is an option.

Decisions

  • Chemo: cisplatin 75 d1 + etoposide 100 d1–3 q3w × 4 (or 60/120 as in Turrisi).
  • Chest RT dose: 45/30 BID (Turrisi, CONVERT) or 66/33 daily. 70/35 (CALGB 30610) is equivalent. Only about 25% of surveyed radiation oncologists favour BID.
  • Timing: start RT with cycle 1–2, or cycle 2 if bulky, treating post-chemo volumes but including initially involved nodal stations.
  • No elective nodal RT for PET-staged patients (CONVERT, C30610; isolated nodal failure about 2–3%). Consider covering the ipsilateral hilum.
  • Consolidation: durvalumab after chemoRT (ADRIATIC: median OS 37 → 56 months). Atezolizumab added concurrently did not help (NRG-LU005).
  • T1–2 N0: mediastinal staging (PET may suffice), then lobectomy or SABR, followed by adjuvant chemotherapy.
  • Thoracic RT benefit: about 5% OS and about one-third fewer local failures.

Brain: PCI or MRI surveillance

  • LS-SCLC with response: offer PCI 25/10 (Aupérin: about 5% OS benefit, pre-MRI era). MRI surveillance every 3 months is a reasonable alternative (ARS lists it second).
  • Start about 4 weeks after chemotherapy. Repeat brain MRI first.
  • Cognitive decline is clear after 30/10 but not clearly after 25/10. Hippocampal avoidance and memantine remain investigational (NRG-CC003; PREMER positive, Dutch trial negative).
  • ES-SCLC: MRI surveillance rather than PCI for most (Takahashi). PCI reduced brain metastases but median OS was 13.7 vs 11.6 months (p=0.09, favouring observation).

Key trials

CONVERT (2017)45/30 BID vs 66/33 daily · cisplatin/etoposide2 y OS 56 vs 51% (not significant). 5 y OS about 33%. G3–4 oesophagitis about 20% in both arms.
CALGB 30610 / RTOG 0538 (2023)45/30 BID vs 70/35 dailyMedian OS about 29 months. 5 y OS 29 vs 33% (not significant). G3 oesophagitis about 17%.
ADRIATIC (2024)after chemoRT · durvalumab vs placeboMedian OS 37 → 56 months. 3 y OS 48 → 57%. Median PFS 9 → 17 months. G3–4 pneumonitis about 3%.
NRG-LU005 (2026)chemoRT ± atezolizumab (concurrent and adjuvant)No benefit: 3 y OS about 51 vs 46%, PFS HR 0.98.
NRG-CC003 (2025)PCI 25/10 ± hippocampal avoidanceNo significant difference in 6 month delayed recall (30 vs 26%). 1 y intracranial relapse about 15%.

Extensive-stage SCLC

Chemo-immunotherapy is standard. Chest RT is for palliation or for selected responders with residual thoracic disease. Median OS is about 12 months; 5 y OS is <5%.

Decisions

  • Systemic: platinum/etoposide × 4 with durvalumab (CASPIAN) or atezolizumab (IMpower133, carboplatin only), then maintenance immunotherapy.
  • Consolidative chest RT: consider 30/10 for responders with residual chest disease (CREST). Its role with immunotherapy is being tested (RAPTOR / NRG-LU007).
  • Brain: MRI surveillance; treat symptomatic brain metastases.
  • Palliation: RT for symptomatic sites. Low-volume asymptomatic brain metastases can start with chemotherapy.

Key trials

IMpower133 (2018)carboplatin/etoposide ± atezolizumabMedian OS 10.3 → 12.3 months. 1 y OS 38 → 52%.
CASPIAN (2019)platinum/etoposide ± durvalumabMedian OS 10.5 → 13 months. 2 y OS about 14 → 22%.
CREST (2015)498 · response to chemo · PCI ± thoracic RT 30/101 y OS 28 → 33% (primary endpoint not met, p=0.066), 2 y OS 3 → 13%. Intrathoracic progression 46 → 20%.
Jeremic (1999)210 · complete distant response · ± thoracic RTMedian OS 11 → 17 months. 5 y OS 4 → 9%.
Takahashi (Japan) (2017)ES-SCLC · MRI-staged · PCI 25/10 vs MRI surveillanceBrain metastases at 12 months 59% (observation) → 33% (PCI). Median OS 11.6 months with PCI vs 13.7 without.

More key trials

EORTC 08993-22993 (2007)Phase III · extensive-stage SCLC responding to chemo · PCI vs observation (no routine brain imaging)PCI reduced symptomatic brain metastases and improved 1-y OS; later, the Japanese MRI-surveillance trial (Takahashi) found no OS benefit, so MRI surveillance is an alternative. check

SCLC planning

Planning is like NSCLC: 4DCT, IMRT, daily CBCT and adaptive replanning for rapid shrinkage. Do not refuse curative treatment just because V20 is 35–40%.

Volumes and limits

  • Treat the post-chemo primary plus all initially involved nodal stations.
  • Lung V20 <35% (CONVERT, CREST). In CALGB 70 Gy trials, V20 exceeded 40% in 30 of 100 patients, but only 3 had G3 toxicity.
  • Spinal cord: 36 Gy max for 45/30 BID (Turrisi era). 41 Gy in RTOG protocols. CONVERT allowed 42 Gy (BID) and 48 Gy (daily).
  • Cardiac dose independently predicted OS in RTOG 0617; a heart-dose analysis of C30610 is planned. Use IMRT and spare the heart.

Exam notes

AS's Phase 2 summary, as written and not source-checked. Where it disagrees with this page, go by this page.

Limited SCLC

From the exam summary.

Epidemiology

I: Less common than NSCLC, prevalent in smokers

A: 70

G: M

R: Europe, Asia - high smoking rates

RF

E: smoking, asbestos has synergistic effect with smoking, second hand smoke, occupational: radon, arsenic, nickel, silica

I: nil

G: nil

I: nil

Staging

Veterans affair

  • LS-SCLC: safely encompassable within 1 radiation field /hemithorax – 30%
  • ES-SCLC: not encompassed in 1 radiation field – 70%
  • SCF LN is LS-SCLC
  • Pleural/pericardial effusion is ES-SCLC

TNM

Follows NSCLC

Paraneoplastic syndromes

SIADH – 10-15%

  • muscle weakness, confusion, seziures, hyponatremia,
  • Rx fluid restriction

Lambert-Eaton – <5%

  • proximal muscle weakness which improves with activity
  • Rx pyridostigmine

Cushings syndrome - <5%

  • weight gain, moon face, muscle wasting, high serum cortisol
  • Rx ketaconazole

Pathology

Small round blue cells

Crush artifact

Salt & pepper chromatin

Abundant mitoses & necrosis

IHC: chromogranin A, synaptophysin, CD56, INSM1, high Ki67, often TTF1 +ve

Radiologically

Central mass, frequent N+

All need MRIB to exclude DM

Chemo and radiosensitive

  • Small dose per # effective

Short doubling time, fast repopulation

  • BD # counteracts repopulation
  • Acceleration ↓TTT and allows ↑ dose

Other NEC

Large cell neuroendocrine

  • less aggressive than small cell
  • extensive mitoses & necroses
  • prognosis between SCLC and NSCL
  • peripheral circumscribed mass

Carcinoid tumour

  • less aggressive
  • low mitoses
  • circumscribed polypoid mass

IHC for both same as SCLC, lower ki67

Management

T1-T2N0

  • Uncommon presentation.
  • Lobectomy + nodal dissection followed by chemo can be appropriate if biopsy proven node negative and complete resection possible
  • SABR followed by chemotherapy also an option – retrospective data

1. Chemotherapy: cisplatin and etoposide q3weekly x4 cycles

  • beware of risk of TLS in bulky disease

2. Thoracic RT

  • RT improves 2 yr LC by 25% and 2 yr OS by 5%, survival benefit with commencing RT with cycle 1 or 2 of chemotherapy (MA Warde et al)
  • 45Gy/30# BD – 10% OS benefit at cost of ↑ oesphagitis though used 3DCRT and ENI - Turrisi
  • 60Gy/40# BD – THORA trial improves OS by 10% without sig tox difference
  • 54Gy/45Gy/30# SIB to iGTV BD – Sun et al improves OS by 20%
  • Alternatives: 66Gy/33# (CONVERT), 45Gy/15#
  • No role for ENI
  • Consider sequential chemo → RT for frail patients at likely expense of some survival benefit

3. Consider PCI for responders – 25Gy/10#

  • Rationale: high brain met risk, sanctuary site from chemo, treat occult micromet with RT
  • 3 yr 5% OS benefit with PCI in pre-MRI and IO era (PCI collaborative MA, Auperin et al)
  • Hippocampal avoidance non-inferior with less neurocognitive decline (NRG CC003)
  • Memantine can also reduce cognitive decline: 5mg OD titrated to 10mg BD for 6 months
  • Alternative: MRIB surveillance q3monthly
  • MAVERICK ph III trial: no survival detriment with MRI surveillance, higher CNS progression rates

4. Adjuvant consolidation Durvalumab q4 weekly 24 months

  • Fit patient without PD after CRT
  • improves 3 yr OS by 10% - ADRIATIC
  • The addition of consolidation Tarlatamab (bispecific T cell engager) to IO is being investigated in the DELPHI-306 trial

Turrisi et al

P: LS SCLC

I: 45Gy/30# BD vs 40Gy/25# OD

O: better 5 yr OS 16% vs 26%, median OS 23mo vs 19mo

worse acute G3 oesophagitis 11% vs 27% - NB used 2D planning and ENI

Subsequent trials looking at 45Gy/30# BD vs OD DE CONVERT 66Gy/33#, CALGB 70Gy/35# failed to show survival benefit

THORA

P: LS SCLC

I: 45Gy/30# BD vs 60Gy/40# BD

O: 5 yr OS 28% vs 39%, median OS 44mo vs 22mo

No acute tox diff. 2 oesophageal strictures in 60Gy arm – used mostly 3DCRT, some IMRT

Yu et al

P: LS SCLC

I: 54Gy/30# BD vs 45Gy/30# BD

O: median OS 60mo vs 40mo

CONVERT

P: LS- SCLC

I: 66Gy/33# OD vs 40Gy/30# BD

O: Designed as superiority, failed to demonstrate this however arms had similar outcomes

ADRIATIC

P: LS-SCLC completed definitive CRT without progression

I: Durvalumab for 2 years +/- tremelimumab vs placebo

O: Durvalumab improved 3 yr OS from 48% to 57%, median OS 56mo vs 33mo

NRG-CC003

  • PCI with HA did not improve the primary delayed-recall endpoint but reduced overall neurocognitive failure without compromising intracranial control

EBRT

Dose: 45Gy/30# BD, 6hrs between #, VMAT technique with concurrent cisplatin 75mg/m2 + 100mg/m2 etoposide IV 3 weekly

Offer 60Gy/40# in young, fit pt

Pre-sim: PFTs, smoking cessation; offer NRT, ensure recency of staging

Sim

Supine, arms above head, vac bag

4D-CT scan with IV contrast

If motion >10mm consider breath hold or respiratory gating

IV contrast

Fuse PET

Volumes

iGTVp & iGTVn: contoured on all phases of resp cycle and combined

ITVp: +7mm

ITVn: +5mm

PTV4000: +5mm

Daily CBCT with soft tissue match

OARS/toxicities: as per NSCLC

Troubleshooting exceeding lung OARs

  • Review and rationalise volumes
  • further cis/etop prior to RT, starting RT with cycle 2
  • Adaptive replan during RT as tumour shrinks
  • Sequential chemo → RT rather than concurrent
  • DIBH/resp gating rather than 4DCT
  • Risk-benefit discussion with patient however do not exceed critical OARs

PCI

Dose: 25Gy/10# with HA, AP/PA, commence 4-6 weeks post-chemo

Pre-sim: assess for stable or responsive disease post-chemo, counsel regarding memantine use (s/e = dizziness, headaches, constipation, confusion)

Sim: supine, arms down, TPS for head

Field borders

Anterior: cover crista gali and cribriform plate, avoid anterior orbit, cover BOS and anterior VB, avoid oropharynx

Inferior: caudal C2

Posterior & superior: 1cm flash

If HA then – VMAT, T1 post gad MRI fusion

CTV: whole brain – 5mm + hippocampi

PTV: +5mm

OARS

Hippocampi D100 <9Gy, max dose <16Gy

Lens max <6Gy

Toxicities

Acute: alopecia, dermatitis, fatigue, headaches, n/v, otitis media, seizure risk

Late: chronic fatigue, neurocognitive decline, xerostomia, cataracts

Extensive SCLC

From the exam summary.

Management

1. Chemoimmunotherapy: carboplatin and etoposide q3weekly x4+ either durvalumab or atezolizumab until PD or unacceptable toxicity

  • Immunotherapy improves OS (Caspian, Impower133)
  • Beware of TLS for bulky disease

2. Tarlatamab (bispecific T cell engager) in addition to maintenance IO

  • DELPHI-303 (ph 1) showed promising median OS of 25 months, ph 3 data pending from DELPHI-305
  • S/e: cytokine release syndrome, ICANS/neurotoxicity

3. Consolidation thoracic RT if partial response to chemo with residual dx

  • 30Gy/10# or 40Gy/15#
  • Improved 2 yr OS by 10% and reduced thoracic failure by 40% - CREST trial, pre-IO era
  • 30Gy/10# consolidation RT after chemoIO alone did not improve OS and had more toxicities including deaths than IO alone (Treasure trial, ph II)

4. PCI

  • PCI has 14% OS benefit and reduces symptomatic brain mets – Slotman et al
  • Now MRI surveillance is non-inferior to PCI for OS (Takahashi et al)
  • Less neurocognitive decline, higher brain mets rate
  • MRIB q3mo for year 1 then q3-6mo
  • Salvage SRS to brain mets reduces neurocognitive decline vs WBRT without clear OS detriment (ENCEPHALON)

5. Disease progression

  • Primary refractory: progression <6 months from chemo
    • Poor prognosis – months
    • Lurbinectedin, topotecan, clinical trial
  • Platinum sensitive: progression >6months post chemo
  • Prognosis can be several months
  • Rechallenge platinum chemo
  • Second line: topotecan, paclitaxel, tarlatamab
