Primary kidney SABR (IROCK, Dhar 2026; FASTRACK II)

The renal tumour itself is the target: there is no elective CTV. The work is motion management and protecting the bowel and the remaining renal function.

Volumes

VolumeDefinition
GTVEnhancing tumour on contrast CT (corticomedullary and nephrographic phases) or MRI, including any renal vein or IVC tumour thrombus that is treated.
CTVUsually equal to the GTV.
ITVFrom 4D-CT, or use breath-hold, abdominal compression or gating to reduce motion.
PTVSmall (commonly about 3–5 mm) with daily CBCT; check against the IROCK paper and the FASTRACK II protocol.

OARs

  • Both kidneys as separate structures (ipsilateral kidney minus GTV); use a DMSA or split-function scan if renal reserve is borderline.
  • Duodenum, stomach, small and large bowel (contour loops on every slice near the PTV), liver, spinal cord, and the renal pelvis and ureter for central tumours.

Liver SBRT: HCC and metastases

There is no ESTRO-type atlas for the liver; volumes come from trial protocols (RTOG 1112, the Princess Margaret series) and single-institution practice. Fiducials and multiphase imaging do most of the work.

Volumes

VolumeDefinition
ImagingMultiphase contrast CT and MRI (arterial, portal venous and delayed), registered to the planning scan in the same breathing phase. Fiducials help with registration and IGRT.
GTV (HCC)Arterial-phase enhancing tumour with portal venous or delayed washout, plus any tumour vein thrombus (portal vein or IVC). Previously treated (TACE) areas: use the MRI enhancement, not the lipiodol.
GTV (metastases)Lesion on the phase where it is most conspicuous (often portal venous or hepatobiliary-phase MRI).
CTVOften equal to the GTV, or a small margin (0–5 mm) by protocol.
ITV / PTV4D-CT ITV, or breath-hold / gating; PTV by IGRT method.

CTV and PTV margins vary by trial; check the protocol you follow (e.g. RTOG 1112).

OARs (Jabbour 2014 atlas)

  • Liver minus GTV (sometimes minus the sum of GTVs), for mean dose and the volume of liver spared (e.g. at least 700 cc below a threshold dose, per protocol).
  • Stomach, duodenum, bowel, oesophagus, heart, kidneys, cord, chest wall / ribs, and the central biliary tree for central tumours.
