Verify except: no renal monitoring or dose adjustment needed for denosumab, fewer acute-phase reactions and more hypocalcaemia with denosumab (pooled phase 3 analysis; Henry 2011).

Safety checks and radiotherapy

MRONJ, calcium, and what changes for the radiation oncologist.

Before and during treatment

  • Dental review before starting and 6-monthly during treatment (eviQ 1300). Do not start denosumab 120 mg with an unhealed extraction site or a jaw condition needing surgery (MHRA 2014).
  • Calcium: correct hypocalcaemia and vitamin D first. For denosumab 120 mg check calcium before the first dose, within 2 weeks after it, and if symptoms occur; before each dose in those at risk such as CrCl below 30 mL/min (MHRA 2014).
  • Daily calcium (at least 500 mg) and vitamin D unless hypercalcaemic (eviQ 1300).
  • Creatinine before each zoledronic acid dose.
  • Thigh or groin pain: image for atypical femoral fracture.

Medication-related osteonecrosis of the jaw

  • Exposed necrotic jaw bone persisting more than 8 weeks. Incidence with denosumab about 2% and may rise with longer use (eviQ 1300); similar rates with zoledronic acid in the phase 3 trials.
  • Risk factors: extractions and implants, poor oral hygiene, dentures, smoking, steroids, antiangiogenics (bevacizumab, sunitinib), duration of therapy.
  • Prevention: complete invasive dental work before starting; if an extraction becomes unavoidable, involve a special-needs dentist or oral surgeon; a drug holiday is common practice with limited evidence.
  • Head and neck RT adds osteoradionecrosis risk in the same jaw: record bone agent exposure at the H&N dental assessment.

For the radiation oncologist

  • Bone agents continue through palliative RT and SBRT; no need to hold.
  • They complement, not replace, RT for pain. RT to bone counts as an SRE in the trials.
  • After spine SBRT, vertebral compression fracture risk is driven by dose per fraction, lytic disease and SINS; a bone agent is reasonable but has not been shown to prevent it. Verify
  • Hypercalcaemia: fluids first, then zoledronic acid; treat the cancer.
  • Radium-223 with abiraterone increased fractures (ERA 223): a bone agent is mandatory with radium-223 combinations.
  • Links: bone and spine metastases · SINS, Mirels and Bilsky.
