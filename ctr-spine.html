Spine SBRT: intact vertebra (ISRC, Cox 2012)

Fuse a thin-slice MRI (T1, T2, and post-gadolinium) to the planning CT, registered locally on the treated vertebra. The CTV is built from anatomical sectors, not a margin.

Full guide with the St George workflow, ISRC sector table, PTV/PRV policies and 1–5 fraction constraints: Spine and bone SBRT contouring guide.

The six sectors

1 vertebral body · 2 left pedicle · 3 left transverse process and lamina · 4 spinous process · 5 right transverse process and lamina · 6 right pedicle.

Circumferential CTV (all six sectors) only when the disease is circumferential. Epidural disease: GTV includes the epidural component; the CTV around it stays outside the cord PRV.

Post-operative spine SBRT (Redmond 2017)

Contour the pre-operative extent of disease, not just what is left. Hardware artefact on MRI makes CT myelography useful in some cases.

Post-op and sacral volumes in full: protocol sections 4–5.

CTV

  • Pre-op bony disease plus the adjacent sectors (Cox rules), and the post-op residual.
  • Pre-op epidural disease: include the epidural space around the cord as a "donut" at the levels involved (circumferential if the pre-op epidural disease was circumferential).
  • Do not routinely include the surgical hardware, incision, or the whole operative bed; include paraspinal extension that was present pre-op.

Non-spine bone and sacrum

For sacral metastases Dunne 2020 adapts the sector approach; for long bones and pelvis, Nguyen's international work gives practice-based recommendations.

Non-spine bone, post-fixation and soft tissue volumes: guide section 6.

Approach

  • GTV on MRI (and PET where used), including the extra-osseous component.
  • CTV: a margin within the bone along the marrow (larger) and a smaller margin into soft tissue where there is extra-osseous disease, edited at cortical bone and fascia.
  • PTV small (about 2–3 mm) with daily CBCT.

Check the intra- and extra-osseous margins in Nguyen 2020 (and the later consensus by the same group) before using them.
