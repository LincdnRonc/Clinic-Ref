Paediatric late effects: dose thresholds

Use these thresholds when consenting families and choosing volumes. Younger children are most vulnerable, especially for IQ and growth.

Thresholds to quote

Organ / effectThreshold
IQ declineWorst with age <7 y and 36 Gy CSI. Children >6 y tolerate 23.4 Gy CSI fairly well. Modern ALL cranial RT is ≤18 Gy
Growth hormone deficiencyFrom about 18 Gy; NTCP D50 about 25 Gy
Puberty18–24 Gy to hypothalamus/pituitary: precocious or delayed puberty. >30 Gy: gonadotropin insufficiency
Central hypothyroidismNTCP D50 about 39 Gy
Hearing<5% loss if cochlea mean ≤35 Gy; risk rises above that, especially with cisplatin
ScoliosisClinically significant from ≥15 Gy to the spine; treat whole vertebral bodies symmetrically
TestisOligospermia from 0.15 Gy; azoospermia 0.5 Gy; infertility likely above 2–3 Gy. Testosterone deficiency above 12–14 Gy
StrokeRaised risk with ≥10–20 Gy cranial RT; RR about 16 with circle of Willis ≥40 Gy
Secondary breast cancerRisk rises with dose (OR 3.8 per 10 Gy); lower if ovaries received ≥15 Gy. Start early breast screening after chest RT
Second malignancy (Ewing series)None below 40 Gy; 130 per 10,000 above 60 Gy

Synthesised from the RadOncReview CNS and Peds document (collaborative, US-centric: NCCN, ASTRO, COG), including 2025–26 guidelines and ASTRO '26 abstracts. Doses are as reported in the source and its trials. Check eviQ, departmental protocols and drug availability before prescribing.
