Benign musculoskeletal conditions

Low-dose RT for heterotopic ossification, painful osteoarthritis, plantar fasciitis and early Dupuytren's disease. Use the lowest effective dose and explain the small theoretical cancer risk.

Doses

ConditionDoseNotes
Heterotopic ossification prophylaxis (hip)7/1Within 4 h before surgery, or within 72 h after. 5.5/1 post-op was less effective. Spare the medial third of acetabular hardware
Painful osteoarthritis3/6 (0.5 Gy/fx) 2–3 times a weekA second series may be given. Knee OA sham-controlled trial: 4-month response 42% (sham) vs 70% (3 Gy)
