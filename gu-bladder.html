Bladder: trimodality therapy

Trimodality therapy is a recommended alternative to cystectomy for selected cT2–4a N0 (ASTRO 2026, strong). RT alone is not curative.

Volumes and contouring step by step: Bladder contouring guide (FROGG 2020 doses, margins and constraints in sections 8–9).

Trimodality therapy: who

  • Best candidates: solitary tumour <5–7 cm, predominant urothelial histology, maximal TURBT, no extensive CIS, no hydronephrosis, good bladder function, cN0, will comply with cystoscopic surveillance.
  • Poor features (cT3–4a, hydronephrosis, CIS) are not absolute contraindications; cCR may be <50% with hydronephrosis.
  • cN1: TMT or cystectomy after neoadjuvant/induction systemic therapy. cN2–3 responding: consider consolidative local therapy.
  • Higher distant risk (cT3–4, N+): neoadjuvant / induction systemic therapy.
  • Outcomes: ~70% CR and keep bladder; 5-y OS ~50–57%; mostly distant failure. No RCT vs cystectomy (SPARE couldn't accrue).
  • Incomplete TURBT: pooled RTOG cCR still ~50%.

Bladder planning

  • Sim and treat with an empty bladder; start within ~8 wk of TURBT. IMRT/VMAT with daily CBCT; adaptive / plan-of-the-day if coverage is unreliable.
  • CTV: bladder + 6 mm without extravesical extension, 1 cm with it (Jenkins). Include prostate (males) and proximal urethra if bladder neck/base, T4 or prostatic urethra involved (22% stromal involvement at cystoprostatectomy).
  • BC2001 PTV: outer bladder wall + 1.5 cm; extravesical disease + 2 cm. Even with CBCT a 1.5 cm margin covers >90% of fractions.
  • BC2001 treated a "mini-pelvis" without intentional nodes, with <10% pelvic nodal failure.

Post-cystectomy RT, NMIBC and systemic therapy

Adjuvant RT for high-risk pathology, intravesical therapy for NMIBC, and the perioperative immunotherapy trials.

Post-cystectomy RT

  • Indications: (y)pT3–4, (y)pN+ or positive margins (urothelial, conditional); pure SCC with (y)pT3–4/N+. Neobladder is not a contraindication.
  • BART: 50.4/28 stoma-sparing pelvic IMRT improved locoregional control, essentially eliminated isolated pelvic failures; OS NS.
  • Dose 44–50.4/22–28 to bed + pelvic nodes; SIB up to 56 Gy to a positive margin; gross residual up to 64 Gy.
  • Start within 2–3 mo of cystectomy (≤4 mo OK for neobladder continence) or within 8 wk of adjuvant chemo. If on adjuvant IO, give RT before or during it.
  • Omit the cystectomy bed if neobladder and margin negative (failures are mostly pelvic sidewall).
  • 2-y local failure: pT3N0 12% · pT3N1 or pT4N0 33% · pT4N1 72% (Reddy).

NMIBC basics

  • Everyone gets TURBT. Low-grade Ta, solitary, <3 cm, no CIS: observe (cytology/cystoscopy q3m ×2 y, q6m ×2 y, then annually).
  • High risk (high grade, T1, CIS, multifocal, ≥3 cm, LVI): intravesical BCG (weekly ×6, 3–4 wk after TURBT) or MMC.
  • High-grade T1 recurrence after adequate intravesical therapy, unfit/declining cystectomy: TMT or trial (ASTRO conditional).
  • ~10% of high-risk NMIBC behave lethally; BCG vs cystectomy has never been settled.

Systemic therapy

  • Cystectomy path: NAC Gem/Cis ×4 → RC (pT0 ~15% → ~38% with NAC). No carboplatin NAC; go to surgery.
  • NIAGARA: durvalumab + Gem/Cis → RC → adjuvant durvalumab: EFS and OS benefit.
  • KEYNOTE-B15/EV-304: perioperative EV + pembrolizumab beat neoadjuvant Gem/Cis (EFS, OS, pCR).
  • IMvigor011: ctDNA-positive after RC → atezolizumab improved DFS/OS; ctDNA-negative did well on surveillance.
  • Metastatic: EV + pembrolizumab first line (EV-302 OS 16→32 mo). Maintenance avelumab after platinum response (JAVELIN Bladder 100). Erdafitinib for FGFR3.

Toxicity and follow-up

  • Acute: frequency, urgency, haematuria, spasm, bowel upset. Send urine culture for suspected UTI (leucocyte esterase often false positive during RT).
  • Late after TMT (pooled RTOG): G3 7% (GU 5.7%, GI 1.9%); no cystectomies for toxicity. Late: contracted bladder, haemorrhagic cystitis, stricture.
  • After TMT: TUR/biopsy at first assessment, cystoscopy + cytology q3–4m ×2 y, then q6m to 5 y, lifelong annual cystoscopy; CT chest/abdo/pelvis q3–6m ×2 y then annually ≥5 y (IBCG).
  • Cystectomy follow-up: retained urethra needs surveillance. 35% develop CKD 3.
