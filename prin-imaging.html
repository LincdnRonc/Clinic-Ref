CT and MRI for planning

CT numbers (Hounsfield units)

TissueApproximate HU
Air−1000
Lung−900 to −500
Fat−100 to −50
Water, CSF, simple cyst0 to +15
Soft tissue, muscle, unenhanced liver+30 to +60
Acute blood+50 to +80
Contrast-filled vessels+150 to +300
Cortical bone+700 to +1500 or more
  • The planning system converts HU to electron density with a calibration curve; contrast and metal artefact can distort dose slightly (override if large).
  • Window/level starting points: soft tissue W400 L40; lung W1500 L−600; bone W1500–2000 L300–400; brain W80 L40; liver W150 L60.
  • 4DCT sorts images into breathing phases: use the maximum-intensity projection (MIP) to draft an ITV for lung tumours, check every phase, and do not use MIP for tumours abutting the diaphragm or liver (use average or individual phases).

Using PET in planning

  • Delineate on CT or MRI and use PET to confirm extent; avoid automatic SUV thresholds as the sole method (they depend on scanner, motion and lesion size).
  • Acquire planning PET in the treatment position if possible; otherwise deformable registration with care.
  • Do not chase the PET-avid edge of a moving lung tumour: motion blurs the signal.
  • After RT, wait about 12 weeks before FDG response assessment (H&N: PET-NECK showed PET-guided surveillance at 12 weeks is safe).

Matching and correction

  • Define in the plan what to match on (bone, soft tissue, fiducials, carina) and the tolerance for action; register on the structure the margin was designed around.
  • Online correction (every fraction) reduces both systematic and random error; offline protocols (e.g. no-action-level, eNAL) reduce systematic error with fewer images.
  • Rotations that cannot be corrected (no 6-DOF couch) matter most for long targets (CSI, H&N with nodes) and for SRS with multiple targets.
  • Imaging dose is small but not zero: daily CBCT adds a few cGy per image.
  • Adaptive replanning: weight loss or tumour shrinkage in H&N and lung, changing bladder or rectal filling in pelvis. Escalate when contours no longer fit the PTV.
