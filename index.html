Thorax

Early-stage NSCLC (SABR)

Dose by location (peripheral, central, ultracentral), HILUS caution, operable vs inoperable, SABR constraints and toxicity.

Stage III NSCLC

ChemoRT then durvalumab or osimertinib, hypofractionation for unfit, PORT, neoadjuvant chemo-IO, superior sulcus, constraints.

Small cell lung cancer

45/30 BID vs 66–70 Gy, ADRIATIC, PCI vs MRI surveillance, extensive stage and CREST.

Thymic, mesothelioma and other thoracic

Thymic PORT by resection, mesothelioma RT, thoracic re-irradiation, VT radioablation.

Oligometastases and general palliation

SABR-COMET, CURB, NRG-LU002; palliative regimens for lung, SVC, H&N, bleeding, liver and coeliac plexus.
