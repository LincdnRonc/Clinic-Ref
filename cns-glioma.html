Glioblastoma (IDH-wildtype)

After maximal safe resection, give Stupp (60/30 + TMZ, then adjuvant TMZ) ± TTFields. For older or frail patients, use 40/15 ± TMZ, or 25/5. MGMT methylation predicts TMZ benefit.

Contour step by step with the CNS contouring protocol.

Decisions (ASTRO/ASCO, ESMO)

  • Good PS, any age: 60/30 + TMZ 75 mg/m² daily → TMZ 150–200 mg/m² d1–5 q28d × 6. Add TTFields during adjuvant TMZ if the patient can wear it about 18 h a day.
  • Older (≥65–70) or fair PS: 40.05/15 + TMZ (Perry). MGMT-methylated benefits most (median OS 7.7 → 13.5 months).
  • Frail or poor KPS (50–70): 25/5 (no worse than 40/15; IAEA Roa), TMZ alone if MGMT-methylated, or best supportive care.
  • Avoid 60/30 in patients >70 y with modest PS (Nordic).
  • Not standard: dose escalation above 60 Gy, bevacizumab upfront (PFS only), concurrent nitrosoureas outside trials.

  • Reducing the margin to 1 cm nearly halved CTV (235 → 137 cc) without changing the pattern of failure (in-field 87 vs 86%).
  • Constraints (RTOG 0825): brainstem <54 Gy (V60 <0.03 cc); optic chiasm and nerves <54 Gy (V55–56 <0.03 cc); retina <45 Gy; cochlea mean <45 Gy; cord <45 Gy.
  • A planning MRI close to the start of RT can detect early progression and may allow smaller volumes. Pseudoprogression occurs within 3 months of chemoRT in 20–30%; do not change treatment on the first MRI (use RANO 2.0). Early progression is usually MGMT-unmethylated.

IDH-mutant astrocytoma and oligodendroglioma (grade 2–3)

For high-risk grade 2 and for grade 3 disease, RT followed by chemotherapy: PCV for oligodendroglioma (1p/19q codeleted) and TMZ for astrocytoma. Vorasidenib can delay RT in low-risk grade 2 after surgery.

Volumes (GTV + 10 mm grade 2, + 15 mm grade 3): CNS contouring protocol, section 3.

Decisions

  • Low risk (<40 y, gross total resection): observation (RTOG 9802 low-risk arm), or vorasidenib for residual non-enhancing IDH-mutant grade 2 (INDIGO).
  • High risk grade 2 (≥40 y or subtotal resection): 50.4/28 or 54/30 → PCV × 6 (RTOG 9802).
  • Grade 3 oligodendroglioma (codeleted): 59.4/33 + PCV (RTOG 9402, EORTC 26951). CODEL: TMZ alone was worse than RT-containing treatment.
  • Grade 3 astrocytoma (non-codeleted): 59.4/33 → adjuvant TMZ (CATNON). Concurrent TMZ did not help IDH-wildtype tumours.
  • No dose escalation in grade 2 (EORTC 22844: 45 vs 59.4 Gy, no difference). Early vs delayed RT: PFS but not OS benefit (EORTC 22845).

Volumes (ESTRO-EANO)

  • GTV: residual tumour + cavity, including T2/FLAIR change (for grade 3, exclude FLAIR change thought to be oedema).
  • CTV: GTV + 1 cm (grade 2) or 1.5 cm (grade 3). PTV ≤3 mm.
  • Optional SIB: 54.45/33 with 59.4/33 to the T1c/bed + 1–1.5 cm.

Recurrent glioma: re-irradiation

Re-irradiation is reasonable for small, well-defined recurrences in fit patients, at least 6 months after prior RT. It improves PFS but not clearly OS (RTOG 1205).

Approach

  • Consider re-resection first. Select by KPS, volume, interval and MGMT/IDH status.
  • Dose: 35/10 with bevacizumab (RTOG 1205). Follow the ESTRO/EANO re-irradiation guideline for other schedules.
  • CTV: T1c (0 mm; 5 mm allowed for <3.5 cm lesions).
RTOG 1205Phase II · recurrent GBM · bevacizumab ± 35/10Re-irradiation was safe and improved PFS but not OS.
