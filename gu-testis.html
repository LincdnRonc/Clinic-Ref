Testis (seminoma)

RT is for seminoma only. NSGCT gets surgery and chemo.

Seminoma by stage

StagePreferredAlternatives
ISurveillance (5-y relapse 10–20%)Carboplatin AUC7 ×1–2, or para-aortic 20/10 (relapse <5%). Treat if unreliable for follow-up
IIA (≤2 cm)RT 20/10 dog-leg + boost 10/5 = 30 GyEP ×4 or BEP ×3. SAKK 01/10: carbo ×1 → INRT 30 Gy
IIB (2–5 cm)BEP ×3 / EP ×4, or RT if <3 cm: 20/10 + 16/8 = 36 GySAKK 01/10: carbo ×1 → INRT 36 Gy (relapses were systemic)
IIC / IIIBEP ×3–4 or EP ×4RT alone not recommended (5-y RFS 44%)
  • Post-chemo residual <3 cm: surveillance. >3 cm: PET ≥6 wk after chemo; if positive, RPLND or second-line chemo.
  • Prefer chemo with horseshoe kidney, IBD, prior RT or pelvic surgery.
  • SAKK 01/10: 3-y PFS 94% (IIA 95%, IIB 93%); RT volume ~¾ smaller than dog-leg.

Fields and planning

  • Supine, clamshell on the contralateral testis, penis out of field. Sperm banking before treatment (40% azoospermic pre-RT).
  • Para-aortic (stage I): bottom of T11 to bottom of L5 (T11 spares kidney vs T10 without RFS loss); ~10 cm wide. Left primaries: include left renal hilum.
  • Dog-leg (stage II): add ipsilateral pelvic nodes, inferior border at top of acetabulum. Also if prior inguinal surgery.
  • Vascular CTV: aorta + 1.9 cm, IVC + 1.2 cm, iliacs + 1.2 cm; nodes + 0.8 cm; PTV + 0.5 cm; +0.7 cm to block edge (≈2 cm on nodes).
  • INRT (SAKK 01/10): pre-chemo GTV + 2 cm CC / 1 cm axial, PTV + 1 cm; plan CT 2 wk after carbo.
  • Inguinal nodes only with scrotal invasion or prior scrotal/inguinal surgery.
Constraints
  • Each kidney D50% ≤8 Gy; mean both <9 Gy. Single kidney V20 ≤15%. Boost: each kidney D50 ≤2 Gy, mean <3 Gy. Bowel V20 <70%.
  • Antiemetic prophylaxis (ondansetron).

Markers, work-up, follow-up

  • Seminoma: never AFP (raised AFP = non-seminomatous elements); mild β-hCG in ~15%. Markers before and ~1 wk after orchiectomy; don't use them to monitor stage I seminoma.
  • Half-lives: AFP 5–7 d; β-hCG and LDH ~1–1.5 d. False AFP: liver disease; false hCG: marijuana, LH cross-reaction.
  • Radical inguinal orchiectomy, never scrotal biopsy. CT abdo/pelvis; CT chest if stage II+. Repeat imaging at 4–6 wk for borderline IIA.
  • Relapse: most within 3 y for seminoma (2 y NSGCT). On surveillance relapses land in the para-aortic "landing strip"; after para-aortic RT, pelvis or mediastinum/neck; after dog-leg, above the diaphragm.
  • Follow-up: H&P + CT abdo ± pelvis roughly q3m → q6m → q12m over years 1 → 2 → 3+. Second cancer risk keeps rising for decades.
