Oesophagus and GEJ (Wu 2015; ESTRO-ACROP nodal stations 2021)

The disease spreads along the submucosa and lymphatics, so the CTV is long and narrow. Use endoscopy (distances from incisors), EUS and PET to draw the GTV.

Volumes

VolumeDefinition
GTVPrimary (wall thickening, PET, endoscopic extent fused or marked with clips/fiducials) and involved nodes.
CTVGTV + 3–4 cm cranio-caudally along the oesophagus and 1–1.5 cm radially, edited off heart, lung, bone and liver. Distal and GEJ tumours: extend into the cardia / lesser curve and include coeliac, left gastric and lesser curve nodes. Cervical / upper thoracic: include supraclavicular and paratracheal nodes.
ITV / PTV4D-CT for distal tumours (more motion); PTV per IGRT, commonly 5–10 mm.

CROSS used GTV to PTV directly (4 cm cranio-caudal, 3 cm distal into the stomach, 1.5 cm radial). Neo-AEGIS and newer trials use CTV-based volumes as above.

Stomach (EORTC-ROG 2009; ESTRO-ACROP 2021)

Nodal stations depend on where the primary sits. The 2021 ESTRO-ACROP guideline describes each station's borders and has a free FALCON practice case.

Nodal coverage by tumour site (pre-op)

PrimaryNodes usually covered
Proximal / cardiaPerigastric (cardia, lesser curve), left gastric, coeliac, splenic artery; lower mediastinal and paraoesophageal if GEJ involved
BodyPerigastric (both curves), left gastric, coeliac, splenic, common hepatic, suprapancreatic
Antrum / pylorusPerigastric, suprapyloric and infrapyloric, common hepatic, hepatoduodenal, coeliac, pancreaticoduodenal

Simulate fasted (consistent stomach filling); 4D-CT for motion.

Pancreas

Two different jobs: a post-op elective volume built around the vessels (RTOG, Goodman 2012), and SBRT to gross disease and the tumour–vessel interface (AGITG/TROG, Oar 2020; NRG atlas, Sanford 2025).

Post-op pancreatic head (Goodman 2012)

  • Contour the pancreaticojejunostomy, the proximal coeliac axis, the proximal SMA, the portal / splenic vein confluence and the aorta (from the highest of these structures down to about L2–L3), then expand the vessels asymmetrically as the paper specifies and join them with the PJ.
  • Use the pre-op CT and the clips to find the tumour bed.

SBRT (Oar 2020; Sanford 2025)

  • GTV on pancreatic-protocol contrast CT (arterial and portal venous phases), fused with MRI where available; fiducials and breath-hold or gating.
  • Add the tumour–vessel interface (where tumour abuts the SMA, coeliac axis or SMV) as a high-dose target in borderline / locally advanced disease.
  • Duodenum, stomach and small bowel PRVs drive the plan; contour them on the treatment-position scan.

Upper abdominal OARs (Jabbour 2014)

RTOG atlas for the liver, kidneys, stomach, duodenum, small and large bowel, and spinal cord.

Tips

  • Duodenum: from the pylorus to the duodenojejunal flexure (ligament of Treitz); separate from the stomach and small bowel.
  • Small bowel: individual loops for SBRT; bowel bag for conventional pelvic/abdominal fields.
  • Liver: whole liver minus GTV for mean-dose constraints; exclude the gallbladder and IVC.
