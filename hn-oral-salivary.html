Oral cavity and lip

Oral cavity cancer is managed with surgery first, and neck dissection for most. Give PORT for adverse features and chemoRT for a positive margin or ENE. Start RT within 6 weeks of surgery.

Decisions

  • Neck (ASCO): T2+ (>2 cm or depth of invasion >5 mm) → ipsilateral neck dissection levels Ia–III (≥18 nodes). T1 → neck dissection, or ultrasound surveillance in highly selected patients. Add level IV if cN+. Dissect the contralateral neck for midline or T3–4 floor of mouth tumours.
  • Neck RT: not needed for pN0 or a single node without ENE after a good dissection, unless there is PNI, LVSI or T3–4. Undissected neck: 50–56 Gy.
  • PORT to primary: pT3–4, LVSI, PNI, close margin (≤5 mm), depth of invasion >3–4 mm (oral tongue). Starting within 6 weeks improves 5 y LC (about 40 → 80% in one series).
  • Margins: goal >5 mm. Margins <5 mm are associated with worse outcomes.
  • Definitive RT can be considered for oral commissure, buccal mucosa or retromolar trigone tumours where surgery would be morbid.

Doses and volumes

  • CTV_63–66: positive margin or ENE + 1 cm. CTV_60: tumour bed + 1–1.5 cm (pre-op tumour + 2 cm for oral tongue and floor of mouth).
  • Dissected involved neck EQD2 60 Gy; undissected at-risk neck 50 Gy; high-risk nodal (ENE) 64–66 Gy. Add 5 mm around the nodal bed (10 mm with ENE).
  • Lip: RT if the commissure is involved, upper lip, ≥2 cm or >50% of the lip. T1 lip 50/20. Commissure involved → cover levels Ib–II to 54 Gy.
  • Brachytherapy (GEC-ESTRO): LDR 60–70 Gy at 0.4–0.6 Gy/h, or HDR 60 Gy (5 Gy BID × 12) as sole treatment for small tumours.
  • Keep the mandible ≤70 Gy.

Salivary gland

Surgery first. PORT for high-grade, positive margins, PNI, nodes, LVSI or T3–4, and for all adenoid cystic carcinomas. Treat named nerves to the skull base for PNI. Concurrent chemo is not routine.

PORT (ASCO)

  • Offer PORT: G3, positive margin, PNI, node-positive, LVSI, T3–4, and all resected adenoid cystic carcinoma.
  • May offer: G2 or close margins.
  • Start within 8 weeks of surgery.
  • Dose: 60–66/30 (RTOG 1008). The trial tests adding weekly cisplatin; it is not standard yet.
  • Neck: ipsilateral I–III dissection for G3 or cN+. Occult nodal rates are about 10% for G1–2 and about 50% for G3. Adenoid cystic: cover the nerve; the neck usually does not need treating.
  • Pleomorphic adenoma: consider 50–60 Gy for multiple recurrences or deep lobe involvement.
  • Particle therapy has no proven advantage over photons.
