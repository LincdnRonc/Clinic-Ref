Thyroid cancer

Differentiated thyroid cancer is treated with surgery, RAI and TSH suppression. EBRT is for gross residual or RAI-refractory disease. Anaplastic carcinoma needs rapid assessment for BRAF and trimodality treatment with ≥60 Gy.

Decisions

  • Differentiated (papillary, follicular, Hürthle): thyroidectomy + central/lateral neck dissection if node-positive. RAI for pT2+ or raised thyroglobulin: 30 mCi (low risk), 100 mCi (T3+ or N+), 200 mCi (metastatic).
  • EBRT for differentiated disease: gross residual neck disease after RAI, bulky metastases, lesions with poor RAI uptake. Consider if >45–55 y with a high chance of microscopic residual. Evidence is mostly retrospective.
  • Medullary: the role of EBRT is less clear. It may benefit R1/R2, extrathyroidal extension and/or N+ disease. RET inhibitors for advanced disease.
  • Anaplastic: maximal safe resection, then PORT ± chemo; otherwise definitive RT. Doses ≥60 Gy with IMRT and trimodality therapy are associated with better survival. Test for BRAF V600E (20–50%).

Squamous cell carcinoma of unknown primary (neck)

Search hard: PET/CT before biopsy, examination under anaesthetic with tonsillectomy and base-of-tongue biopsy. Test HPV and EBV. Most hidden primaries are tonsil or base of tongue.

Decisions

  • Workup (ASCO 2020): HPV testing on level II–III nodes; EBV if HPV-negative. CT neck, then PET/CT. Primary found in tonsil 45%, base of tongue 40%, pyriform sinus 10%.
  • Node level hints: I → oral cavity; II → HPV+ oropharynx; III–IV → larynx/hypopharynx; V → skin or nasopharynx; IV/supraclavicular adenocarcinoma → below the clavicles. Intraparotid node → skin. p16+ but high-risk HPV-negative → skin.
  • cN1: neck dissection then RT, or RT alone.
  • cN2–3: neck dissection → RT ± chemo, or definitive chemoRT.
  • Mucosal volume: p16+ → oropharynx; EBV+ → nasopharynx. Hypopharyngeal mucosa is not routinely treated.
  • With neck dissection alone, about 50% fail at the primary. Treating the mucosa and both necks reduced contralateral nodal failure (44 → 14%) and primary emergence (44 → 8%) in one series. Overall: 5 y OS about 50%, subsequent mucosal primary 10–25%.
