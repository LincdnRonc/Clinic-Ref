Solitary plasmacytoma and myeloma

Solitary plasmacytoma is curable with RT alone (35–50 Gy). In myeloma, use low doses for palliation; even 4 Gy relieves bone pain, though slowly.

Doses (ILROG)

SettingDose
Solitary bone plasmacytoma <5 cm35–40 Gy (NCCN minimum 40 Gy)
Solitary bone plasmacytoma >5 cm40–50 Gy
Extramedullary plasmacytoma40–50 Gy. Nodal involvement is more common than for bone (about 25% in the head and neck)
Myeloma, palliative8/1, 20/5 or 30/10 (ILROG prefers 30/10 for large volumes); 4 Gy (2 × 2 or 4 × 1) works but slowly
  • CTV: GTV + 2–3 cm within anatomical boundaries.
  • Progression to myeloma: bone 65–85%; extramedullary 10–30% at 10 y.

Total body irradiation and ALL CNS RT

Myeloablative TBI is 12–15 Gy in 6–12 fractions over 3–5 days, BID, at low dose rate with lung shielding. Reduced-intensity TBI is 2–6 Gy.

TBI

  • Myeloablative: 12–15 Gy in 6–12 fractions BID over 3–5 days.
  • Reduced intensity / non-myeloablative: 2–6 Gy in 1–4 fractions.
  • Dose rate <20 cGy/min (commonly 5–15 cGy/min) to reduce pneumonitis. Uniformity ±10% along the body axis. Partial lung blocks to a mean lung dose of 50–85% of the prescription.
  • Consent: acute parotitis about 75% within 24 h; G3+ pneumonitis about 2%; cataract about 16% (median 1–4 y).

ALL: cranial RT

  • CNS3 disease: 18/10. CNS1–2 (intermediate risk, if given): 12/8.
  • 24 Gy caused more IQ decline than 18 Gy; modern protocols keep cranial doses ≤18 Gy.

Mycosis fungoides and cutaneous lymphoma

Localised MF responds to very low doses: 8/2 gives about 90% CR. Total skin electron therapy (TSEBT) 12 Gy is now standard because it can be repeated.

Doses

  • Localised MF/CTCL: 8/2 preferred (CR 92% vs 30% with 4 Gy in 2 fractions). Thicker or larger lesions: 20–30 Gy in 2–3 Gy fractions.
  • TSEBT: 12 Gy in 1 Gy fractions over 2–3 weeks (historically 30–36 Gy). Boost shadowed areas (soles, perineum, scalp). It can be repeated.
  • Technique: 80% isodose at ≥4 mm depth and 20% isodose <20 mm (EORTC).
  • Primary cutaneous B-cell lymphoma (MZL, FCL): curative 24–30 Gy (ILROG range 20–45 Gy, bolus required); palliative 4/2.
