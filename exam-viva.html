Zarosky Tips LD

Use guidelines (NCCN/EviQ/treatment algorithms)

Have a prepared script of what to say for standard questions

    • workup o setup o margins o doses o volumes
  • if there is a metric to assess symptoms then use it eg. I will assess their IPSS score and organise for a formal uroflow

Answer the question being asked, succinctly

  • when recommending a treatment, consider pausing to summarise to say “this pt has cancer X, stage X, risk group X. Based on “NCCN” guidelines, my recommended tx is X.”
  • dont go off on tangents about “there was a study” - examiner might have been on the study
  • questions are different difficulty levels, not always expected to know every high levels question
  • lower level = anatomy; mid = stage, options, doses; high = chemo doses, prep

If you don’t know, say you don’t know, and you would look it up. You don’t want to recommend the incorrect treatment for the pt.

Sometimes, the best dose is 0 Gy (i.e., observation)

  • know a guideline concordant dose and constraint set. multiple options, but choose one.
  • discuss the pt's case at tumour board. That's what radoncs normally do anyway :-)
  • moderate hypofractionation (vs conventional 2 Gy fractions) has become the standard of care for multiple disease sites, eg breast, prostate.
  • for some disease sites, there is a condensed formula for treatment

If you don't want to memorize dose ranges, consider picking a number in the range

Summary

Firstly, categorise pacemaker into either Level 1, Level 2 or Level 3:

  • Level 3 = Any dependant pacemaker OR using >10MV photons (or >20MeV electrons) OR dose to pacemaker > 5Gy
  • Level 2 = Does not meet level 3 criteria AND dose to pacemaker between 2-5Gy
  • Level 1 = Does not meet level 2 or 3 criteria (i.e. dose to pacemaker < 2Gy)

  • Exposure to ionising radiation may lead to the malfunction of cardiac implantable electronic devices (CIEDs).
  • The risk of CIED malfunction from ionising radiation is serious but rare.
  • When applying the CIED assessment and management strategies in this resource it is important to consider the potential impact on treatment quality or delays in commencing treatment and the subsequent risk and benefit to each individual patient and clinical scenario.
