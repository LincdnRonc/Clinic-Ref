What to do when OAR not met

  • OAR vs coverage, depends on clinical scenario
  • Review all parameters of the volume in concern

V – volumes

  • ?over contouring of target volume or over/undercontouring of OAR
  • Fusion
  • Peer review
  • Can PTV margins be reduced by improving immobilisation with full body vac bag/TPS, image verification with CBCT, setup accuracy with 6 DOF couch and reducing correction margin acceptance
  • Can CTV margins be reduced by motion mgmt: resp motion management, gating
  • Positioning to change anatomy (eg prone setup, incline)

O – optimise plan with RT (see below), create PRV around OAR

  • For PRV: optimiser the PTV target should be prescription dose whilst the target for the overlap region should be the OAR goal dose

D – dose reduction, induction chemo, give more time between #, adaptive RT or 2 phases (ie seq boost to allow for shrinkage first)

A – alternate Rx options – brachy, protons, surgery, systemic

C – Consider clinical impact when making decision to accept higher OAR or lower PTV coverage, change in intent to palliative, risk/benefit discussion w/ pt

IMRT optimisation

Weighting in optimiser – push desired objectives (min doses, max doses, certain constraints or targets)

Ring structures – to get prescribed dose into structure and drop off dose outside structure

Avoidance structures – to avoid dose within this structure, can be used to move a hot spot by contouring the hot spot and setting it as an avoidance

PRVs around OAR – avoid dangerous dose gradients and hotspots near critical OAR

Number of arcs – increasing improves conformality but increases QA and treatment duration

Direction of beams – non-coplanar arcs, weighting of dose, partial arcs
