CNS & Paediatrics

Benign and functional CNS

From the Benign and functional CNS page, where sources and trials are cited.

Doses and outcomes

  • SRS: 11–14 Gy to the margin (ISRS), most often 12–13 Gy (Gamma Knife at the 50% isodose). Targets up to about 3 cm.
  • Fractionated SRS: 18–21/3 or 25/5 for larger tumours (3–4 cm) or near the brainstem. Conventional 50.4/28–54/30 is also an option.
  • PTV margin 0–2 mm (0 mm preferred). Keep the cochlea mean <4 Gy where possible.
  • Consent: 10 y local control about 97%. Facial neuropathy <1%; trigeminal symptoms about 3%. Serviceable hearing at 5 y about 41–79% (falls with time). Transient enlargement in about 14%. Malignant transformation 0.3%.
  • Full page: Vestibular schwannoma (decision by Koos grade, work-up, SRS planning, consent numbers, V-REX and ACOUNEU).

Doses

TechniqueNon-functioningSecreting
Single-fraction SRS (<2–2.5 cm, away from chiasm)13–16 Gy16–25 Gy
Fractionated SRS21/3 or 25/5
Conventional45–50.4/25–28; 54/30 for aggressive tumours or carcinoma
  • CTV margin 2–3 mm for invasive or aggressive tumours.
  • Local control after RT >90% for non-functioning tumours. Hormone normalisation in 50–60% of secreting tumours, over years.
  • Hypopituitarism is the main late effect (GH deficiency from as low as 18 Gy). Monitor pituitary function long term.

Doses and volumes

  • Pre- or postop: 45/25 (strong). Boost 5.4–9 Gy for R2/gross residual. Definitive: 45/25 CTV + 54–59.4 Gy GTV.
  • Preop CTV: whole stomach, perigastric extension, ≥1 cm proximal oesophagus (more for cardia/GOJ) and regional nodes (coeliac, common hepatic, splenic, left gastric, hepatoduodenal, peripancreatic, SMA, regional para-aortic).
  • Postop CTV: tumour bed, anastomoses, duodenal stump, 2 cm beyond margins, regional nodes. Include gastric remnant only if margin positive. Proximal third: add paraoesophageal and hepatogastric; middle/distal: supra- and infrapyloric.
  • Coverage: preop PTV D95 ≥95%, D2cc ≤107%; postop PTV D100 ≥95%.
  • Fast 2 h before sim and treatment. 4DCT/motion management with IMRT; daily IGRT.
  • D2 dissection: recurrences still locoregional as often as distant; >¾ of regional relapses outside the D2 field (stations 16a2, 8, 12, 16b1, 9).

  • RTOG 1112 schema: start at 50/5 and de-escalate by mean liver dose (13 Gy at 50/5 up to 17 Gy at 27.5/5). Needs liver − GTV >700 cc and GTV >1 cm from stomach/duodenum/bowel.
  • Acceptable floor (CP-A): mean <15 Gy, ≥700 cc <15 Gy; otherwise drop to 40–45/5. CP-B: mean liver <9 Gy.
  • If goals still unmet: mid-treatment break (3 fx, ~30 d, reassess CP/mALBI), spare ~35% of functional liver <12–15 Gy, or non-ablative options.
  • Ablative = BED10 ≥80 Gy (e.g. 45/3, 50/5, 60/10, 72.6/22); 27.5/5 or 30/6 (BED10 ~45) still gives useful control.
  • ASTRO ranges: non-cirrhotic 40–60/3–5 · CP-A 40–50/3–5 · CP-B7 30–40/5 · or 45–67.5/15, 60/20, 50.4/28, 60/30.

Dose and BED

  • MDACC (Tao): BED10 >80.5 Gy → 3-y OS 38 → 72%, LC 45 → 78% for inoperable IHCC (median 7.9 cm). 80.5 Gy ≈ 43.5/5.
  • Brunner SBRT: Dmean BED10 >76 Gy (~41.5/5) → 2-y LC 39 → 80%; Dmax BED10 >91 Gy (~47/5) → MS 13 → 24 mo.
  • Biliary stricture after RT is hard to tell from progression.
  • Adjuvant volumes (SWOG 0809): retropancreaticoduodenal, coeliac and portal vein nodes + bed.

Haematology

Non-Hodgkin lymphoma

From the Non-Hodgkin lymphoma page, where sources and trials are cited.

Doses

  • Curative stage I–II: 24/12 (24–30 Gy). Larger margins if RT alone.
  • Palliative: 4/2 (FORT: CR 49% with 4 Gy vs 68% with 24 Gy). Re-treat as needed.
  • Stage I–II FL with systemic therapy: adding R-CVP to 30 Gy improved 10 y PFS 43 → 62% (TROG 99.03).
  • Grade 3 FL is treated like DLBCL.
UK FORT548 · indolent NHL · 24/12 vs 4/2ORR 91 vs 81%, CR 68 vs 49%. 24 Gy better for durable control.
TROG 99.03 ANZ150 · stage I–II FL · 30 Gy ISRT ± R-CVP10 y PFS 43 → 62% with systemic therapy.

WBRT doses

  • CR after R-MPV: 23.4 Gy WBRT, then cytarabine × 2.
  • PR: ILROG 36–45 Gy WBRT (1.5–1.8 Gy) without boost; NCCN 30–36 Gy with a 45 Gy boost to residual. Example: 36/24 + SIB 43.2/24.
  • Median survival about 6 y with this approach (MSKCC).
  • Intraocular involvement: CTV includes the entire globe and optic nerves to the chiasm.
